# 14 — FOUNDER LETTER TO KAT

*(Draft — James should personalise freely; this is a starting point, not a script to read verbatim.)*

---

Kat,

Happy anniversary. Before you read another word of what's attached to this — I need you to know that this started as something much simpler than a business plan. It started with me watching you work.

I've watched you get up before dawn to do a bride's makeup and stay calm when everything around you wasn't. I've watched you teach a friend a technique in fifteen minutes that they'd been trying to get right for years. I've watched people ask you, over and over, "can you teach me?" — and I've watched you say yes, one person at a time, for over a decade, on top of everything else you already do.

So I started wondering: what if that yes could reach further, without costing you anything you don't want to give?

What's in this package is the answer I came up with — researched properly, not just imagined. It's an idea for turning what you already know how to do into something that's fully, structurally yours: a business built around your name, your skill, and your voice. Not a side project bolted onto your real work — a real, considered plan, with real numbers, real research into what's already out there, and an honest account of what would need to be true for it to work.

I want to be upfront about a few things, because I respect you too much not to be:

I put myself down as the person handling the legal and financial setup at the start — not because I want control over something that's built on you, but because someone has to hold that administrative weight while it's new, and I didn't want that to land on you before you'd even said yes to the idea. If you want in, the very first thing we do properly — before a single dollar of real revenue shows up — is sit down and put a real, fair, written agreement in place. Not a handshake. Something that actually protects you, in writing, because your name and your face are the most valuable things in this plan, and that deserves to be treated with real weight, not assumed.

I also want to be honest about the numbers. There's a version of this that does very well — and there's a version, if a lot of things go right at once, that does spectacularly well. I've put both in here, clearly labelled, because I never want you to feel like I've oversold you a fantasy. The real plan is the modest one. Everything past that is a bonus, not a promise.

And most of all — this is yours to take or leave. You can take the whole thing, take pieces of it, change the name, change the price, change the entire shape of it, or tell me it's not for you at all. None of that changes anything about how much I believe in what you already do, with or without a business attached to it.

I love you. Happy anniversary.

James
