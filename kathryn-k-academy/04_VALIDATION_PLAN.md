# 04 — VALIDATION BEFORE SPENDING
*(Directive Round 5 — no major spend before evidence)*

**Principle:** Kat's audience is warm but small. Every dollar of production spend is gated behind a paying-customer signal. Digital products make this easy: the "prototype" is a presale page and a live workshop, not tooling.

---

## The four validation instruments (total cost < $1,000 — see budget 1)

### 1. Audience-demand probe (cost: $0, days 1–7)
- Kat posts 3 IG stories with polls: "Would you take an online course from me?"; "What's stopping you doing makeup professionally?"; "Course topic you'd pay for first?" + a DM keyword ("BLUEPRINT") auto-reply via ManyChat free tier.
- Mine her existing lesson clients: 10-minute calls with 5 past students — what did they want more of, what would they pay?
- **Measures:** problem resonance, topic priority, language customers actually use (feeds all copy).

### 2. Waitlist landing page (cost: ~$50–150, days 3–10)
- One page (Carrd $19/yr or Squarespace add-on to her existing site): promise, 3 bullets, email capture, "founding member" framing. Link in bio on both IG accounts + FB.
- **Measures:** waitlist conversion. Benchmarks (ESTIMATED, creator-economy norms): ≥3% of profile visitors joining = strong; 300+ emails in 30 days from ~10k followers = strong.

### 3. Paid micro-workshop — the MVP (cost: ~$0–100, revenue-positive, weeks 2–4)
- 90-minute live Zoom class: "Bridal-Ready Base: the 5 mistakes I see every week" — $29–49, capped at 40 seats. Sell via IG + waitlist only.
- **Measures:** will this audience *pay* and *show up*; live teaching format check; recording becomes Tier 1 sample content; attendees get the Tier 2 founding offer first.

### 4. Founding-member presale of Tier 2 (the real gate, weeks 3–6)
- Offer: The Working Artist Kickstart, founding price $649 (vs $799), limited to 25, delivered as a live-taught first cohort starting ~4 weeks later (so content is filmed *as it is taught* — zero wasted production).
- Payment collected upfront via Stripe; honest framing: "founding cohort, you shape the course."
- **Measures:** actual purchase intent — the only metric that matters.

## Decision rules

| Signal (by end of week 6) | Decision |
|---|---|
| ≥15 founding members ($9,750+ collected) AND ≥300 waitlist | **GO** — build Tier 2 fully, schedule Tier 1 filming, open Tier 3 deposit list |
| 6–14 founding members OR waitlist 100–300 | **IMPROVE** — offer/price/angle iteration; re-run presale to the enlarged list in 30 days; do NOT build yet |
| <6 founding members but micro-workshops sell out repeatedly | **PIVOT** — market wants live/in-person from Kat, not self-paced: shift to premium live workshop calendar + 1:1 mentorship, revisit courses at 25k followers |
| <6 founding members AND workshops don't sell AND polls cold | **STOP** digital-course line; Kat keeps services business; reassess in 6 months. Total sunk cost target: <$1,000 |

**Also being validated en route (directive checklist):** product names (A/B two names in stories polls), pricing (founding vs full anchoring), professional vs consumer demand (workshop attendee mix), Tier 3 demand (deposit waitlist — $100 refundable deposits only, no build), purchase intent, preorders. No inventory exists in this model; the equivalent discipline is **no filming ahead of presales** for Tiers 2–3.
