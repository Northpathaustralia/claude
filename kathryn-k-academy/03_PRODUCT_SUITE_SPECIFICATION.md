## ROUND 4 — THE THREE HERO PRODUCTS (FULL SPECIFICATION)

Names below are recommended finals (scoring in `05_BRAND_GUIDE.md`). All prices AUD incl. GST, ESTIMATED pending validation.

### TIER 1 — **The Beginner's Blueprint** — $199 (launch $149)
*"Makeup that finally makes sense — from a working artist, not an algorithm."*

- **Customer:** Aspiring Aimee; also everyday consumers who just want skills. 18–45.
- **Format:** Self-paced, ~4 hours video in 8 modules, lifetime access, phone-filmable production standard (high quality but authentic).
- **Curriculum:** 1) Skin theory, prep & Australian-climate longevity · 2) Colour theory & undertones · 3) Base: match, correct, conceal · 4) Eyes I: shapes & everyday looks · 5) Brows & lashes · 6) Blush/bronzer/highlight placement by face shape · 7) Lips & finishing · 8) The 15-minute full face + hygiene fundamentals.
- **Includes:** printable face charts, budget vs pro kit list (AU retailers), private community space, Certificate of Completion (non-accredited, stated).
- **Repeat-purchase hooks:** seasonal look packs ($29–49), upgrade credit toward Tier 2 within 60 days.

### TIER 2 — **The Working Artist Kickstart** — $799 (launch/founding $649)
*"From 'I love makeup' to your first paying clients — the full Australian playbook."*

- **Customer:** Aspiring Aimee ready to earn; hobbyists formalising side income.
- **Format:** Self-paced core (~10 hrs, 12 modules) + fortnightly live group Q&A for the first cohort + templates vault.
- **Curriculum — Part A, Client-ready artistry (7 modules):** consultation & skin analysis; bridal & event makeup start-to-finish; photographic makeup (flash vs natural); mature skin & diverse skin tones; speed & setting for QLD heat/humidity; working on real faces (Kat's real client footage with consent); kit building at $500/$1,500/$3,000 levels.
- **Curriculum — Part B, The Australian MUA business (5 modules):** ABN registration, sole trader vs company basics, GST threshold awareness, insurance (public liability/product), record-keeping *(general information, not financial advice — stated in-product)*; pricing & packages for the AU market; contracts, deposits & no-show protection (lawyer-reviewed templates); getting the first 10 clients (local SEO, IG, vendor networks with photographers/celebrants/salons); kit hygiene & basic compliance.
- **Includes:** contract + invoice + client-intake templates, pricing calculator spreadsheet, 90-day launch checklist, community, Certificate of Completion (non-accredited).
- **This is the validation product** — presold as a founding cohort before full production (see `04_VALIDATION_PLAN.md`).

### TIER 3 — **The Master Artistry Program** — $2,995 · VIP $3,995
*"The complete professional standard — taught live, start to finish, including SFX."*

- **Customer:** Working Wendy; ambitious Tier-2 graduates. Cap: 20–30 per cohort, 2–3 cohorts/year.
- **Format:** 12-week guided cohort. Weekly module drops + **weekly 90-minute live online seminar** (taught + critique of submitted work) + community. **VIP:** adds 2-day in-person intensive on the Gold Coast (hired studio, max 10, hands-on SFX day + editorial shoot day with photographer, portfolio images included).
- **Curriculum arc:** Weeks 1–3 pro base/complexion mastery & advanced colour · 4–5 editorial & runway · 6 airbrush + camera-ready (4K/HD) · 7–9 **SFX block:** wounds/bruising/ageing, prosthetic basics & bald caps, product safety and skin-safe materials · 10 speed, set etiquette, working with photographers/directors · 11 portfolio building & pricing at pro level · 12 business scaling: agencies, film/TV pathways, teaching income.
- **Includes:** graduate directory listing, personalised critique on 6 submitted assignments, Certificate of Completion (non-accredited, stated), alumni community.
- **Gate:** built only after Tier 2 validation passes and 8+ deposits are held for cohort 1.

### Ecosystem & repeat purchase (directive: "capable of expanding beyond one product")
Order bumps ($29–49 look packs, kit checklists) → Tier upgrades → annual alumni membership (year 2, ~$29/mo) → in-person workshop calendar → **Lash Lock physical tool** (year 2: by then there is an audience to validate shape/price with, an organic channel to launch through, and cash flow to fund a small tooling run — every problem that kills it as a first product disappears when it's product #5).

## PRODUCTION GUIDE (Round 10, adapted from manufacturing to course production)

**Equipment (mostly owned already — ASSUMED; budget in `06_FINANCIAL_MODEL.md`):** modern phone camera (4K), $80–150 wireless lav mic (Rode Wireless GO or budget equivalent), $60–120 key light + existing ring light, phone tripod. Studio = Kat's existing workspace. Total new gear target: **under $400**.

**Platform stack (ESTIMATED monthly):** Thinkific or Kajabi for hosting ($59–199/mo — start Thinkific Basic); Stripe/PayPal (~1.7–2.2% + fees); MailerLite or Kit for email (free → $30/mo); Canva Pro ($18/mo); CapCut (free) or a contract editor later. **Total fixed stack at launch ≈ $110–250/mo.**

**Production process per course:** outline → slide/demo split → batch-film in 2–3 full days per tier (checklist: clean audio, consistent framing, natural light + key light, model releases signed for every face on camera) → edit → upload → caption (accessibility + retention) → QA watch-through at 1.5×.

**Quality-control checklist:** audio clarity test on phone speaker · colour accuracy of swatches on 2 screens · every claim in business modules cross-checked against current ATO/ASIC public guidance with "general information only" disclaimer · model consent forms filed · no accreditation implications anywhere · captions on all videos.

**Risk reduction (adapting the directive's supplier-risk list):** no large upfront spend before presale evidence; film Tier 2 only after founding members pay; contracts template reviewed by an Australian solicitor once (~$300–600, ESTIMATED) then reused; public-liability + professional-indemnity insurance quote before first in-person intensive (RVAL: broker quote); refund policy published (14-day on self-paced, cohort-specific terms for Tier 3) consistent with Australian Consumer Law (lawyer to confirm wording).
