# 01 — EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
## Kathryn K Academy — Digital Makeup Education Business
**Prepared for:** James Forycki (Gold Coast, QLD) · **Prepared:** 15 July 2026 · **Anniversary date:** 19 July 2026

---

## The one-paragraph version

Turn Kat's 12+ years as a working Gold Coast makeup artist — and the 1-on-1 lessons and group training she **already sells** — into a three-tier digital education business under her existing brand (Kathryn K / @kathrynkmua), launched with under $2,500, marketed organically through Instagram and TikTok, and scaled toward a $1M annual run-rate through a ladder of one entry course, one career course, and one flagship pro program with live seminars. Digital products carry ~90% gross margins, zero inventory risk, and use skills Kat demonstrably has today.

## Strategic decision: digital-first, Lash Lock deferred

The original directive proposed **Lash Lock**, a physical under-eye fallout shield. The directive explicitly permits repositioning when it creates a stronger business, and the evidence is one-sided:

| Factor | Physical tool (Lash Lock) | Digital education suite |
|---|---|---|
| Startup capital | Prototyping, tooling, MOQs, freight | Camera she owns + ~$150/mo software |
| Inventory risk | Real and front-loaded | Zero |
| Gross margin | ~50–65% (ESTIMATED) | ~85–92% (ESTIMATED) |
| Copyability | High (silicone shields are commodity items on AliExpress) | Low — the product IS Kat's expertise and personality |
| Proof of demand | None yet | She already sells lessons and training (VERIFIED) |
| Time to first dollar | Months | Weeks |

**Decision: build the education business first.** Lash Lock survives as a Year-2 ecosystem extension (a branded tool sold to an audience that already exists), covered in `03_PRODUCT_SUITE_SPECIFICATION.md` and the roadmap. This is a repositioning within the mission, not an abandonment of it.

## The honest $1M answer

$1M in year one from a 9.8k-follower account is a **breakout scenario, not a base case**. The package includes the full step-by-step to a $1M run-rate — but gated: the base case is ~$180–280k year one, and every scenario states exactly what must be true for the next one to become realistic. Do not spend ahead of evidence. Full model in `06_FINANCIAL_MODEL.md`; conditions in `12_RED_TEAM_AND_VERDICT.md`.

## Verdict (Round 20 summary)

**GO AFTER VALIDATION.** Launch the validation sprint (waitlist + founding-member presale of Tier 2) within 14 days for under $1,000. Green light Tier 3 only after 15+ paid founding members. Full rules in `04_VALIDATION_PLAN.md`.

## Critical ownership warning (read before the anniversary)

The brand asset here is **Kat's name, face and reputation**. A structure where James holds 100% of a company trading on her personal identity, without a written agreement, is the single largest risk in this plan — legally (she owns her name and likeness) and personally. Recommended structure, presented as her opportunity with a documented path to equity, is in `10_OWNERSHIP_ROLES_OPERATIONS.md`. This requires an Australian accountant and commercial lawyer before incorporation.
