   - Teach (2–35s): demonstrate skin prep step being skipped (no primer suited to skin type / not letting moisturiser absorb); show correct technique side-by-side on real skin.
   - Payoff (35–50s): show the visible longevity difference (time-stamped, real, no filter).

### Weekly filming plan
One batch-filming day every Sunday or Monday (3–4 hours): 5–7 pieces of raw footage covering that week's calendar, filmed in the existing studio space, phone + lav + key light. Edit in CapCut across the week (30–45 min/day) or hand to a contract editor once Scenario 2 (doc 06) is reached.

### Posting schedule
IG Reels: 5×/week. TikTok: 4–5×/week (can reuse Reels edits with native captions). Stories: daily. YouTube Shorts: 3×/week (repurpose top-performing Reels). Grid feed posts: 2×/week for launches/testimonials/educational carousels.

### Content-batching system
Monthly planning session (James + Kat, 45 min) → weekly batch-film day → daily 15-minute caption/schedule task → Friday performance review (15 min).

### KPIs
Follower growth rate (target ESTIMATE: +10–15%/month during validation, +5–8%/month sustained); Reel average watch-through %; saves-per-post (education content proxy for value); DM/comment "how do I join" mentions (purchase-intent signal); email list growth; waitlist→presale conversion %; cost per email lead if any paid boosting used.
