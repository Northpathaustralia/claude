# 09 — HIGGSFIELD AI CONTENT SYSTEM
*(Directive Round 14)*

**Locked rule:** AI never fabricates a customer, review, professional endorsement, product result, or before/after. AI is used for storyboarding, world-building, brand visuals, and editing assistance around real footage — never as a substitute for it.

## The three-bucket rule

| Bucket | Definition | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **REAL FOOTAGE REQUIRED** | Anything showing a technique working, a student result, a testimonial, or product performance | All tutorial demos, client transformations, testimonials, live seminar clips, "day in the life" content |
| **AI-ASSISTED** | Real footage enhanced or framed with AI tools — background, color grade, motion graphics, captions, storyboard pre-viz | B-roll background replacement for a clean studio look, animated text overlays, thumbnail generation, trailer editing assistance |
| **FULLY GENERATED CREATIVE CONCEPT** | No real footage involved; explicitly conceptual/brand-world content, never claiming to show a real result | Brand mood films, "product world" launch teasers, moodboards, concept ads for future physical product (Lash Lock), anniversary presentation visuals |

Every piece of AI-touched content gets a one-line internal tag (REAL/AI-ASSISTED/GENERATED) in the content calendar so nothing crosses the line by accident.

## Workflow

1. **Storyboard in Higgsfield** (GENERATED bucket) → 2. **Kat films the real technique** per the storyboard (REAL bucket) → 3. **Edit with AI-assisted backgrounds/motion graphics/captions** (AI-ASSISTED bucket) → 4. **Publish with the correct bucket's disclosure norms** (GENERATED brand-world content is captioned as concept/creative, never implied to be a real class or result).
