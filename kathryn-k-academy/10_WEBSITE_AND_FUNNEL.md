## Email sequences (draft copy)

**Email popup (site visit):** "Get my free guide: 5 Makeup Mistakes Even Talented Artists Make" — email capture, delivers a short PDF/video repurposed from content bank.

**Waitlist confirmation email:** Subject: "You're on the list 🤍" — Body: thank you, what to expect next, follow Kat on IG for real-time updates, reply-to enabled for questions.

**Launch email sequence (5 emails over 10 days):**
1. "Doors are open" — announce, full offer details, founding price.
2. "Why I built this the way I did" — founder story angle, builds trust.
3. "Inside the curriculum" — module walkthrough, addresses objections.
4. "Your questions, answered" — FAQ-style, social proof if available.
5. "Closing tonight" — honest final-hours reminder, real deadline only.

**Abandoned-cart sequence (3 emails over 3 days):**
1. (2 hours later) "Forget something? Your spot's still here" — simple reminder, no pressure.
2. (24 hours later) "Quick answer to the #1 question I get" — addresses top objection (time/cost/experience level).
3. (48 hours later) "Founding price ends soon" — honest urgency only if genuinely time-limited.

**Post-purchase sequence (4 emails):**
1. Immediate: welcome, login details, what to do first.
2. Day 3: "How's it going?" check-in, encourage first-module completion, support contact.
3. Day 14: momentum check, community reminder, upsell soft-mention to next tier if relevant.
4. Day 30 (post-completion for self-paced): request honest review/testimonial, invite to alumni community, present next-tier offer.
