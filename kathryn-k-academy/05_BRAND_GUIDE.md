### Brand story (draft)
Kat started at seventeen with a certificate and a brush belt, and spent twelve years doing every kind of face the industry could throw at her — brides at sunrise, festival glitter at midnight, wounds and prosthetics for camera. People kept asking her the same question: *"can you teach me?"* She said yes one student at a time. Kathryn K Academy is that yes, at scale: real training from a working Australian artist — the technique, and the truth about turning it into a living.

- **Mission:** turn makeup lovers into confident, paid, professional artists — honestly.
- **Vision:** Australia's most trusted artist-led makeup education brand.
- **Values:** Real skill over trends · Honesty (real faces, real results, no fakery) · Craft + Commerce (art deserves income) · Generosity in teaching.
- **Personality:** the talented big sister who tells you the truth. Warm, direct, funny, technically fierce.
- **Customer promise:** "Everything I teach, I've done for a paying client."
- **Voice:** first person, plain-spoken, specific (never "enhance your glam" — always "stop your base sliding off by 2pm in QLD humidity").
- **Taglines:** *Learn from a working artist.* (primary) · *Skill you can charge for.* (Tier 2) · *The full craft. Start to finish.* (Tier 3)

### Visual system
- **Colours:** Ink Black `#101014` · Warm Ivory `#F7F2EC` · **Signature Copper-Rose `#B76E5B`** (metallic accent) · Deep Teal `#14424A` (Tier 3/SFX accent) · Blush `#E8C9BD` (backgrounds). Light-first with a dark premium variant for Tier 3.
- **Type:** Display: an elegant high-contrast serif (e.g. Fraunces / Canela-alike). Body/UI: clean geometric sans (e.g. Inter / Söhne-alike). License-check before use.
- **Logo direction:** primary wordmark "KATHRYN K" with "ACADEMY" letterspaced beneath; secondary monogram "KK" with the second K mirrored to suggest a compact mirror / open book; icon = the KK monogram in copper-rose on ink.
- **Photography:** natural Gold Coast light, real skin texture visible (no blur-filter retouching — brand proof point), copper/ivory palette, hands-working shots, before/during/after triptychs.
- **Social visual direction:** consistent ivory-card educational carousels; Reels cover template with serif hook line; SFX content uses the teal/dark variant.
