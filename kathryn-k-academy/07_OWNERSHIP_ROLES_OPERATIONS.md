## ROUND 8 — OWNERSHIP AND STRUCTURE

### Practical Australian structure options (general information — confirm with an accountant and lawyer before acting)

**Recommended starting point: a single Pty Ltd company, James as sole director and 100% shareholder at incorporation, with a written Founder & Brand Agreement executed with Kat on day one — not added later.**

Why this shape, and why the agreement can't wait:
- Company structure gives limited liability (relevant once contracts, refunds, and eventually insurance are involved) and is simple to administer with one director.
- **The brand is built entirely on Kat's name, face, voice and professional reputation.** That is a legally and personally significant fact: her name and likeness are not something a company can simply "own" by James being the director. Operating this without a written agreement — even with the best intentions — creates real risk to both the business and the relationship if left implicit.
- A written agreement does **not** need to transfer control away from James on day one. It needs to clearly state: what Kat is contributing (name, likeness, expertise, content), what she receives (see profit-sharing options below), what happens to the brand if the relationship changes, and the path to formal equity.

### Matters requiring professional advice (do not treat any of the below as settled)
- Whether Kat should hold a trademark licence, a personal services contract, or a small equity stake from day one — **an IP/commercial lawyer should advise**, given the name/likeness issue above.
- GST registration timing (mandatory once turnover exceeds $75,000/yr — VERIFIED general ATO threshold, confirm current figure with accountant).
- Whether Kat should be paid as a contractor (ABN, invoices) or engaged under a personal services/brand agreement with royalty-style payments — **accountant and lawyer input required**; each has different tax and entitlement consequences.
- Trademark ownership: recommend the company owns the registered marks (Kathryn K Academy, course names) via a documented licence from Kat for use of her personal name/image — standard practice for personal-brand businesses, confirm structure with a lawyer.
- Employment vs contracting rules if Kat becomes a paid operational lead (Fair Work considerations) — **employment specialist required** if her role becomes a regular paid position rather than founder/equity participation.

### Recommended role split (intent — locked; mechanics — provisional pending advice)

| | James | Kat |
|---|---|---|
| Legal role at start | Sole director, 100% shareholder | Brand founder & product expert (contracted role via written agreement) |
| Responsibilities | Strategy, finance, legal/commercial decisions, systems, growth, paid ads/funnel, reporting | Course content, teaching, live seminars, demonstrations, creative direction, student community tone |
| Compensation model (options, needs advice) | Owner draws / director role | Options: (a) fixed content fee per course + revenue share on sales attributable to her likeness; (b) percentage royalty on all tiers (e.g. 15–25% of gross, ESTIMATED starting range); (c) formal equity vesting over 12–24 months tied to agreed milestones |
| Future equity path | Retains control unless he chooses to formalise a transfer | Documented option to earn toward a minority equity stake (e.g. 10–30%, ESTIMATED range) as the business proves out — this should be offered proactively, not left to be asked for |

### Non-negotiable principle
**Sole control is not a substitute for a fair, clear, written agreement.** The recommended sequencing is: (1) present the opportunity to Kat as a gift and an offer, not a fait accompli; (2) if she wants in, formalise the Founder & Brand Agreement and profit-sharing terms with proper advice within the first 60 days of operating — before real revenue makes the conversation harder, not after.

## ROUND 16 — OPERATING SYSTEM

### James's responsibilities
Strategy & pricing decisions · financial management & reporting · legal/contract oversight · funnel, email, and paid-channel systems · analytics & KPI tracking · supplier/software vendor management · hiring contractors (editor, community manager) as scenarios in doc 06 justify them.

### Kat's responsibilities
Filming course content & live seminars · answering student questions in community/Q&A · content demonstrations for social · reviewing/critiquing Tier 3 submissions · providing the "voice" for social captions (or approving James/contractor drafts) · flagging real client stories/wins usable (with consent) as content.

### Daily tasks
- **James:** check sales dashboard & email inbox (15 min); respond to any customer service escalations; review/schedule that day's social post if not already batched; check ad/analytics if running.
- **Kat:** post or film per the content calendar (doc 08); respond to DMs/comments on her own posts (community warmth is part of the brand); answer any pending student questions in the community space.

### Weekly tasks
- **James:** revenue & KPI review against doc 06 scenarios; content calendar planning meeting with Kat (30–45 min); review upcoming Tier 3 live seminar logistics; process any refunds per policy.
- **Kat:** batch-film per the weekly filming plan (doc 08); host the Tier 3 live seminar (once running); record one behind-the-scenes/personal piece for content variety.
- **Both:** 30-minute weekly sync — what's working, what's not, what to test next.

### Monthly tasks
- **James:** full P&L review vs. doc 06 scenario tracking; software/subscription audit; update the validation/growth metrics; plan next month's promotions or launches; review any legal/compliance items (refund requests, contract disputes).
- **Kat:** review student outcomes/testimonials for (consented, honest) case studies; refresh curriculum notes based on common student questions; plan next month's live seminar topics (Tier 3).
- **Both:** cohort retro after each Tier 3 cohort closes — what to improve for the next one.

### Functional coverage
- **Customer service:** James owns email/help-desk; Kat owns in-community tone and escalated "is this actually correct technique" questions.
- **Inventory/orders/fulfilment:** N/A for digital tiers (automated via platform); becomes relevant only when the Year-2 physical product launches — revisit this section then.
- **Returns/refunds:** James administers against the published policy (see doc 10 for policy copy).
- **Content production:** Kat films; James/contractor edits, capations, schedules.
- **Product testing:** Kat, plus a small trusted beta group of 5–10 past lesson clients before each new tier ships.
- **Creator outreach:** James identifies and contacts; Kat approves who she wants to collaborate with (brand fit is hers to protect).
- **Reviews:** James requests them systematically post-purchase (see email sequences, doc 10); never fabricated (locked rule, doc 08).
- **Finance/reporting:** James, monthly, against doc 06.
