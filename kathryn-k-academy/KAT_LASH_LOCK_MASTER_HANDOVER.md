**Customer problem identified:** Two customer segments are underserved in the Australian market —
1. **"Aspiring Aimee"** (18–34): stuck between free-but-unstructured YouTube tutorials and expensive ($1,500–$5,000) campus-based beauty colleges, with almost nobody teaching the **Australian business side** (ABN, GST, insurance, pricing, contracts) of becoming a paid MUA.
2. **"Working Wendy"** (1–5 yrs in): has basic skill but hits a ceiling — wants pro/editorial/SFX training and confidence to charge more, and SFX training is scarce outside full-time campus programs.

**Proposed solution:** A three-tier digital education ladder under the brand **Kathryn K Academy**, sold organically through Kat's existing Instagram/TikTok/Facebook presence, requiring near-zero inventory risk and roughly 85–92% gross margins (vs. an estimated 50–65% for a physical tool with real manufacturing/inventory risk).

**Business opportunity / vision:** Grow Kathryn K Academy into a $1M/year-run-rate education brand within 12 months (labelled a *breakout scenario*, not the base case — see financial model), while validating every spending decision against real presales before building. In Year 2, once an audience and cash flow exist, launch **Lash Lock** as a physical product extension sold to that audience — de-risking the exact physical-product problems (inventory, tooling, unproven demand) that made it a weak Year-1 choice.
