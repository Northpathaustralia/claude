# START HERE — What To Actually Do Next (no jargon)
This is the simplest possible version. Each step is one sitting. Detail lives in `docs/19-implementation-guide.md`; this page just tells you what to do and in what order. Don't skip ahead — the order protects your money.

## Week 1 — Make it legal and yours (before spending anything on the brand)
**1. Get your ABN and company.** Go to abr.gov.au, register. Free, ~20 minutes. You need this for everything else.
**2. Check the name "Evenfall" is free.** Go to IP Australia's free trademark search (search "ATMOSS"), type "Evenfall", look at classes 3 and 35. Also pay a trademark attorney (~$1,500–4,000, one-off) to do a proper search and file it. **Do not buy anything branded until they say it's clear.** If it's taken, doc 03 has the naming rules — pick again, everything else stays the same.
**3. Buy the domains.** evenfall.com.au and evenfall.co (~$40/yr total). Grab the Instagram/TikTok handles the same day (@evenfall.ritual as backup).

## Week 2 — Get the product moving (longest lead time, start early)
**4. Email 3 Australian cosmetic contract manufacturers** (search "cosmetic contract manufacturer Melbourne bath salts"). Send them `docs/02-product-specification.md`. Ask for: price at 3,000 jars, lead time, and a sample of their base blend. Pick one; pay the deposit.
**5. Order packaging samples** (jar, wooden lid, pouch, shipping box) from the specs in doc 02 — your manufacturer will usually have suppliers; otherwise Alibaba samples first, local suppliers for the real run.

## Week 3 — Build the shop (a weekend of clicking)
**6. Follow doc 19, steps 2–10, in order.** Shopify → payments → email address → products (copy-paste from `docs/06-website-copy.md`) → apps → shipping → GST → analytics. The prototype in `website/` shows your web designer (or Shopify theme) exactly what every page must look like — hand them the folder.
**7. Connect the marketing accounts:** doc 19 steps 11–14 (Meta, TikTok, Pinterest, Klaviyo). Each is an app-store install and a login.

## Week 4 — Make the ads (your Higgsfield account earns its keep)
**8. Open the `higgsfield/` folder.** Read its README once (5 min). Make Card 01 today: copy Scene 1 into Higgsfield, paste the style block, generate, download, repeat for 5 scenes, stitch in CapCut. Your first ad exists. Make cards 02–08 over the week — one per day.

## Week 5–8 — Prove it quietly (the beta)
**9. Send 200 jars to a founder circle** — friends-of-friends, your email list, a $30 "founders price". The goal is 50+ honest reviews and photos. **This is the only legal source of the reviews on your website.**
**10. Fix what they tell you.** Their exact words become your website FAQ edits and your next ads.

## Week 9 — Launch
**11. Turn on the ads** (doc 19 step 16: $150/day total to start, and set the rule that auto-pauses any ad that spends $150 with no sale). Post one piece of organic content daily from the 30-day calendar in doc 08.
**12. Every Monday, one hour:** read the KPI email, kill losing ads, make 3 new videos from the A/B lines in the winning card, ship one small website improvement from customer messages. That hour, repeated weekly, is the whole growth system.

**When something breaks or numbers look bad:** doc 19 step 18 has the "if red, do this" playbook, and `docs/13` + `docs/20` tell you when to slow spending. You never need to guess.
