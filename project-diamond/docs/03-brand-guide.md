# 03 — Brand Guide
## EVENFALL

### Name & trademark
- **Name:** EVENFALL — archaic English for the fall of evening. Short, ownable, emotionally exact, works globally, no negative meanings in major languages.
- **Trademark plan:** file AU TM classes 3 (cosmetics/bath preparations), 4 (candles, future), 30 (tea), 35 (retail). Pre-filing gates (launch checklist): IP Australia ATMOSS search, USPTO/UKIPO knockout search, evenfall.com.au + evenfall.co acquisition, social handle sweep (@evenfall.ritual fallback). Budget A$4k with attorney. Rationale: coined-adjacent compound word → low collision risk vs. dictionary words like "Dusk" (taken in AU retail).

### Positioning
**For** overstretched Australians who can't switch off, **Evenfall** is the premium evening-ritual brand **that** turns the last hour of the day into something you look forward to, **because** rest deserves the same design, care and ceremony we give everything else. **Unlike** supplement-coded wellness brands, Evenfall is a design object and a nightly ritual — the Aesop of winding down.

### Brand platform
- **Mission:** give people their evenings back.
- **Vision:** a world where rest is a ritual, not a failure state.
- **Values:** Stillness over stimulation · Craft over claims · Nature, precisely (Australian botanicals, exactingly sourced) · Honesty about what a bath can and can't do.
- **Tagline:** **"The night, reclaimed."**
- **Secondary lines:** "An hour that's yours." / "Made for the end of the day."
- **Personality:** the calm friend with excellent taste — assured, warm, quietly literary, never preachy, never clinical, allergic to hustle-culture and to wellness woo alike.

### Tone of voice
- Short sentences. Lowercase ease, uppercase restraint.
- Sensory and specific ("kunzea, cut with cedar") over abstract ("natural wellness blend").
- Honest hedging is on-brand: "A bath won't fix everything. It fixes the next hour."
- Never: exclamation marks, "girlboss/grind" language, medical claims, "detox".

### Visual identity
- **Logo:** wordmark "EVENFALL" in a modern serif (GT Sectra / Freight Display class), wide tracking; monogram "E." for lids and seals. Direction: engraved-plate elegance, not spa-script.
- **Palette:**
  - Ink `#16161A` (near-black, primary)
  - Bone `#F2EEE6` (warm off-white, ground)
  - Eucalypt `#5B6558` (muted green-grey, accent)
  - Dusk Lilac `#A99AB5` (sparing accent — dusk sky)
  - Brass `#B08D57` (foil/details only)
- **Typography:** Display — GT Sectra (or Fraunces as open licence). Text — Söhne / Inter. Numerals old-style in editorial contexts.
- **Photography & art direction:** last-light hour only (dusk tones, long shadows), real Australian interiors, steam, hands, weight-of-jar shots; textures: stone, linen, water. No stock-smiley models, no clinical white. Film-grain grade, slightly warm blacks.
- **Layout language:** generous whitespace, single-column storytelling, full-bleed dusk imagery, rules and folios like a well-set book.
- **Packaging concepts:** matte-black glass + beechwood + debossed foil (see 02); the "Rest Notes" card series makes every delivery collectible.

### Luxury positioning rules
1. Never discount the hero jar publicly — value moves through the subscription and bundles.
2. Scarcity honestly: limited accords are genuinely limited (one production run).
3. Retail scarcity: max 10 wholesale doors year 1.
4. Every touchpoint passes the "would Aesop ship this?" test.

### Brand guardrails (one-page for contractors)
Do: dusk light, texture, ritual, restraint, specificity, honesty.
Don't: claims (sleep "cure"), urgency theatrics, neon, emoji in brand voice, influencer-brief clichés ("obsessed!!"), visible logos of other brands.
