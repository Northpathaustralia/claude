# 12 — Marketing Strategy & Growth Roadmap
## Strategy in one line
Organic-credibility engine (honesty + UGC on the sleepmaxxing wave) feeding a premium-converting site, with paid amplifying only proven creative, and retention/subscription doing the profit.

## Channel plan (launch year)
- **Meta (45% of media):** whitelisted UGC + honesty ads (⭐ set from 07). Structure: 1 ASC campaign + 1 testing campaign (broad, creative-as-targeting). Kill $150 rule; scale winners +20%/72h. Landing: PDP for product hooks, The Ritual page for top-funnel hooks.
- **TikTok (20%):** organic-first (daily spine posts), Spark Ads on anything >50k organic views.
- **Pinterest (10%):** evergreen — 100 PIN hooks as fresh pins over 90 days; boards: Evening Ritual, Bath Aesthetic, Gifts That Give Time, Honest Sleep. Compounds into gifting seasons.
- **Google (15%):** brand + "magnesium bath soak australia" exact/phrase + Shopping. Journal cluster captures top-funnel organic.
- **Influencer/UGC seeding (10%):** 30 creators at launch (gifted + 10 paid), rights-cleared for whitelisting. Selection: mid-tier AU lifestyle/wellness/fitness, engagement >4%.
- **PR/earned:** "the brand that won't overclaim" angle to AFR/Broadsheet/Urban List; founder op-ed on honest wellness.

## Retention system (profit engine)
Klaviyo flows per doc 08; subscription (Ritual Club) pushed at PDP, post-purchase, and day-24; churn savers (skip/pause/cadence); Rest Notes weekly editorial keeps non-buyers warm without discounting. Referral: give $15/get $15 on thank-you page + day-14. Loyalty-lite: Rest Note card collectibility + early access to limited accords (status, not points).

## Seasonal calendar
Feb–Mar: brand launch, UGC wave. Apr–May: **Mother's Day** (biggest AU gifting moment for this category) — gift guide PR, bundle push, cutoff SMS. Jun–Aug: **winter peak** — limited smoked-eucalyptus accord drop. Sep: recovery/spring reset (Dan segment). Oct–Dec: **Christmas gifting** — corporate gifting outreach from Oct 1, cutoff campaigns. Jan: "evenings, kept" resolution angle (anti-hustle counter-programming).

## Growth roadmap
- **0–6m:** AU DTC only; prove CAC ≤ $55, sub take ≥ 30%, creative engine 5 assets/week.
- **6–12m:** NZ shipping; wholesale pilot (10 premium doors); hotel minis pilot (2 boutique hotels); SEO cluster compounding; 1,150 subs.
- **12–24m:** US launch (counter-seasonal: their winter = our summer trough) via US 3PL + localised site; UK via distributor; morning-ritual line; retail flagship pop-up (Melbourne, winter).
- **24m+:** international wholesale, spa back-bar program, category extension (textiles), potential retail partner (Mecca-style) only if brand equity established — never discount-channel.

## Automation roadmap (AI leverage)
Now: Higgsfield creative system (doc 09), Klaviyo flows, Meta auto-rules, CS macros. M3: AI-assisted CS drafting (Gorgias AI, human-approved), review-mining → weekly copy insights, auto-repurposing pipeline (doc 08). M6: creative-performance loop (auto-brief new Higgsfield variants from winning hook attributes), LTV-based bid audiences, churn-risk scoring. M12: personalised send-time by stated bedtime, predictive inventory (velocity + seasonality model).
