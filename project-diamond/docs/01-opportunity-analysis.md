# 01 — Opportunity Analysis
Project Diamond · Venture selection record · July 2026

This document records the full discovery funnel: 50 opportunities generated, scored, debated by the Council, attacked by the Red Team, and reduced to one winner.

## Scoring model

Each idea scored 1–10 on eight weighted criteria (max weighted score 10.0):

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Gross margin potential (landed COGS < 20% of RRP) | 20% |
| Emotional/identity purchase strength | 15% |
| Repeat purchase / subscription potential | 15% |
| Visual appeal & UGC/AI-video ease | 15% |
| Shipping economics (small, light, non-fragile) | 10% |
| Return rate risk (inverted) | 10% |
| Legal/regulatory risk (inverted) | 10% |
| Premium-brand pricing power in Australia | 5% |

## Final debate — top 4

**#1 Magnesium sleep-ritual system** vs **#2 home fragrance** vs **#3 matcha** vs **#4 dog treats**:

- **Home fragrance**: beautiful UGC but Dusk, Glasshouse and Circa own AU retail; candles are heavy and melt in transit in Australian summer; margin drag from glass + freight. CFO score 7, Ops 6.
- **Matcha**: strong subscription but supply is Japan-constrained, quality variance risk, and category is import-price exposed. Supply Chain score 6.
- **Dog treats**: great LTV but pet food is regulated per-state (PFIAA), requires food-grade manufacturing, and premium pricing power caps out. Legal 7, CFO 7.
- **Sleep ritual**: 80%+ gross margin, light (soak refill pouches ship <1kg), zero sizing returns, consumable (28-day cycle → native subscription), emotionally charged (sleep is the #1 reported wellness complaint), enormous UGC surface (125M+ sleepmaxxing posts), cosmetic classification (not therapeutic if claims are worded correctly), and the AU premium/design-led position is genuinely vacant — Salt Lab and The Base Collective are wellness-coded, not luxury-coded. Council consensus: **9.55/10**.

## Red Team attack on the winner (and repairs)

| Attack | Repair |
|---|---|
| "Magnesium absorption through skin is scientifically contested — claims risk." | All claims are ritual/relaxation/cosmetic ("warm water ritual", "skin feel", "wind-down"), never therapeutic ("treats insomnia", "raises magnesium levels"). Reviewed in Legal section of Master Spec. Efficacy positioning leans on the *ritual* and warm-bath evidence, which is robust. |
| "Not everyone has a bathtub." | Hero system includes foot-soak protocol (marketed explicitly), plus non-bath products (mist, balm, tea) so tub-less customers have a full ritual path. Bath penetration in AU owner-occupied homes ~70%+; foot soak covers the rest. |
| "Commodity ingredients — anyone can copy." | Moat = brand, not formula: design-led identity, proprietary scent (Tasmanian kunzea + lavender + cedarwood accord), packaging, content engine, and subscription ecosystem. Same moat logic as Aesop (glorified soap, $2.5B exit). |
| "Winter-skewed demand." | Southern-hemisphere winter (May–Aug) is AU peak → counter-cyclical to northern markets, enabling year-round demand once US/UK launched (Year 2). Summer positioning: recovery + air-conditioned "cool room ritual" + foot soak. |
| "CAC in wellness is rising." | Organic-first engine (sleepmaxxing UGC surface), gifting seasonality (Mother's Day, Christmas), and subscription LTV (~$210 y1) supports CAC up to $55 at target LTV:CAC ≥ 3.5. |
| "Trademark collision risk on name." | Name selected for low collision; formal IP Australia + ATMOSS search and .com.au/.com acquisition is a launch-checklist gate (see 13-risk-register, 14-launch-roadmap). |

Red Team final position: **no remaining structural objections**. Residual risks logged in the risk register.

## Decision

**Proceed with Opportunity #1.** Brand developed in `03-brand-guide.md`: **EVENFALL** — a premium Australian evening-ritual brand. Hero product: **The Deep Soak** (magnesium bath soak, Tasmanian botanicals). Full product architecture in `02-product-specification.md`.
