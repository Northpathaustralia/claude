# 02 — Product Specification
Brand: EVENFALL · Category: Premium evening-ritual (cosmetic bath & body)

## Hero product — THE DEEP SOAK

A magnesium-chloride bath soak with Australian botanicals, positioned as the centrepiece of a nightly wind-down ritual.

**Formulation (cosmetic classification, AICIS-listed ingredients only):**
- Magnesium chloride flakes (food/cosmetic grade, min 47% MgCl₂) — 78%
- Dead-sea-style sodium chloride coarse salt — 12%
- Colloidal oatmeal — 4%
- Coconut-derived emollient (caprylic/capric triglyceride carrier) — 3%
- Essential-oil accord "Evenfall No.1": Tasmanian kunzea, French lavender, Virginian cedarwood, vetiver — 2.5%
- Dried calendula + cornflower petals (visual layer, top of jar) — 0.5%

**Format:** 900g in matte-black glass jar with beechwood lid + debossed aluminium label. 12 soaks per jar (75g dose) or 24 foot-soaks. Includes brushed-steel dosing scoop (first purchase).

**Sensory spec:** crystals must be translucent-white with visible petals; scent throw noticeable at arm's length dry, room-filling in hot water; zero residue ring on tub (emollient % capped for this).

**Claims language (approved):** "supports your wind-down ritual", "skin feels soft, mind feels quiet", "the warm-water ritual, perfected." **Never**: treats insomnia/anxiety, raises magnesium levels, therapeutic benefit. Cosmetic, not therapeutic — keeps us outside TGA listing.

## Packaging specification
- Jar: matte-black glass (recyclable), 89mm dia, beechwood lid with silicone seal; front label debossed aluminium foil on textured stock, ink: warm off-white on near-black.
- Refill: kraft home-compostable pouch, tin-tie, letterpress-style label.
- Shipper: rigid kraft box, custom black tissue, wax-seal-look sticker, A6 "Rest Notes" ritual card (collectible series), honeycomb paper void fill. No plastic anywhere.
- Unboxing beat sheet (for UGC): outer box → tissue reveal → ritual card → jar weight-in-hand moment. Target unboxing time 20–30s (ideal Reel length).

## Manufacturing & supply
- **Contract manufacturer:** Australian cosmetic blender (VIC or NSW; e.g. tier of Syndet/PLC-style contract fillers), GMP cosmetic standard. Blend + fill + label in one facility.
- **Magnesium chloride:** imported flake (Tibetan-plateau or Dead-Sea grade) via AU ingredient distributor; dual-source from month 1.
- **Kunzea oil:** Tasmanian distiller, 12-month supply agreement (niche oil — the genuine scarcity in the BOM; secured early, 2 suppliers identified).
- **MOQs:** first run 3,000 jars + 2,000 pouches + 2,000 mists ≈ A$68k inventory. Reorder lead time 6 weeks; safety stock 8 weeks of forecast.
- **QC standards:** batch scent panel vs retained standard, moisture <2%, fill weight ±2%, stability test 12 weeks accelerated, IFRA compliance certificate per accord, AICIS ingredient check, batch traceability.
