# 14 — Launch Checklist, 90-Day & 365-Day Roadmaps

## Launch checklist (gated — nothing ships until all ✅)
**Legal/brand gates:** ATMOSS + knockout TM search passed → TM filed (cl. 3, 35) · evenfall.com.au + .co secured · social handles secured · claims list lawyer-approved · policies (privacy/terms/returns) reviewed · product liability insurance bound · AICIS ingredient check complete · labels: INCI, batch, best-before, patch-test note.
**Product gates:** accord locked vs retained standard · 12-week accelerated stability passed · rinse-clean test on 3 tub materials · fill/dose calibration signed · packaging drop-test passed · first 3,000-unit run QC-released.
**Commerce gates:** Shopify live with full copy (doc 06) · checkout + express pay tested on 5 devices · subscription flows tested incl. pause/skip · Klaviyo flows live (W/A/P/S/R) · SMS compliance (opt-in, ID, opt-out) · GA4 + CAPI verified · 3PL pack spec signed with photo audit.
**Marketing gates:** 200-unit founder-circle beta shipped, ≥50 reviews banked · 30 creators seeded, rights signed · ⭐ launch creative set produced (20 assets) · Pinterest boards seeded (40 pins) · press kit + honesty op-ed pitched · Rest Notes #1–4 written.

## 90-day execution roadmap
**Days 1–14 (foundations):** company + bank + insurance; TM search/file; supplier POs (deposit); domain/handles; brand assets finalised; Shopify build starts; beta list recruitment (waitlist page live day 3).
**Days 15–35 (build):** production run 1; website copy in; flows built; creative shoot (dusk day) + Higgsfield batch 1 (15 exemplars); creator outreach (30 signed); founder-circle beta ships day 30.
**Days 36–56 (soft launch):** beta feedback loop → PDP FAQ updates; reviews imported; soft launch to waitlist (no paid); CVR instrumentation; fix top-3 friction points; press embargo lifts.
**Days 57–70 (public launch):** paid on (Meta $250/day rising), TikTok daily spine, Spark on winners; PR wave; referral live.
**Days 71–90 (optimise):** first kill/scale cycle done weekly; A/B #1 (hero CTA); sub take-rate push (PDP ordering test); wholesale kit drafted; Mother's Day inventory build PO placed; 90-day board review vs KPIs (doc 10) — go/adjust decision.

## 365-day roadmap (quarterly)
**Q1 (launch):** everything above; exit Q1: $90k cum. revenue, 4.8★, sub take 25%+.
**Q2 (Mother's Day + winter):** gifting campaign; limited winter accord (one run); hotel minis pilot; content engine at full cadence; exit: $340k cum., 600 subs.
**Q3 (deepen):** recovery/Dan segment push; SEO cluster complete (30 posts); wholesale 10 doors; CRO audit + A/B roadmap items 2–4; NZ on; exit: $600k cum., 850 subs.
**Q4 (Christmas + scale prep):** corporate gifting program; Christmas cutoffs; US launch plan finalised (3PL, pricing, localisation) for Y2 Q1; morning-line R&D locked; annual Red Team re-audit of brand, claims, economics; exit: **$968k Y1 revenue, 1,150 subs, LTV:CAC ≥ 2.5, guarantee claims < 3%.**
