# 00 — Executive Summary & Business Plan
## EVENFALL — The night, reclaimed.

**The business:** a premium Australian evening-ritual brand. Hero product: **The Deep Soak**, a magnesium-chloride bath soak with a proprietary Tasmanian botanical accord ($64 RRP, 82.5% gross margin), inside a ritual system (Mist, Balm, Tea, Ritual Set) with a native subscription (Ritual Club refills, $39).

**Why this, why now:**
1. **Market:** sleep is the dominant wellness complaint; benefit-led bath soaks are the fastest-growing bath subsegment carrying 50–80% price premiums; AU subscription commerce compounding ~38% CAGR.
2. **Culture:** "sleepmaxxing" (125M+ posts) supplies an enormous organic content surface — and a backlash against overclaiming gadgets that our radical-honesty positioning converts.
3. **Whitespace:** AU incumbents (Salt Lab, The Base Collective) are wellness/supplement-coded. Nobody owns the design-led premium position — "the Aesop of winding down."
4. **Economics:** consumable (28-day cycle), light (<1kg), zero sizing returns, cosmetic classification (no TGA listing), 68% contribution margin before marketing, gifting spikes twice a year, counter-seasonal to northern markets for Y2 international.

**The moat:** brand, not formula — proprietary accord (single-origin Tasmanian kunzea under supply agreement), packaging system engineered for a 25-second unboxing, honesty positioning competitors can't copy without confessing, subscription base, and a content engine (1,000-hook lab + 250-video Higgsfield system) producing at agency scale for founder cost.

**Model:** launch capital $154k → Y1 revenue $968k (~breakeven, deliberately reinvested), M12 exit rate 1,150 subscribers ($45k/mo recurring), Y3 $4.8m at 13% EBITDA, Y5 $9.5m. Kill/pivot criteria pre-agreed (doc 11).

**Go-to-market:** 200-unit founder-circle beta banks 50+ reviews pre-launch → creator-seeded UGC wave → paid amplifies only proven creative ($150 kill rule) → retention/subscription does the profit. Peaks: Mother's Day, AU winter, Christmas.

**Governance:** council-scored decisions (consensus ≥9.5/10 achieved on venture selection), standing Red Team audit (pre-launch and annually), risk register with monthly review, all claims legally fenced (cosmetic, never therapeutic).

**The ask/next step:** execute the 90-day roadmap (doc 14). Every specification required to build — product, brand, site, copy, creative, ops, finance — is complete in docs 01–14 and consolidated in `MASTER_SPEC.md`.
