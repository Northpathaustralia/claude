# 04 — Customer Personas & Voice-of-Customer

## Persona 1 — "Wired Amelia" (primary, ~55% of revenue)
- 32–45, professional (marketing, law, health, education), metro AU (Sydney/Melbourne/Brisbane), HHI $120k+, buys Aesop, Go-To, Bed Threads, Frank Green.
- **Pain:** brain won't shut off; doom-scrolls in bed; wakes at 3am rehearsing tomorrow; feels rest is "unproductive" and resents that she feels that.
- **Dream outcome:** an evening that feels like hers; falling asleep without negotiating with her own brain; being "the person with lovely rituals" rather than "the person who's always tired".
- **Objections:** "Is this just expensive salt?" / "Does magnesium through skin even work?" / "I don't have time for baths." / "$64 for a bath product?"
- **Triggers:** identity ("I'm someone who takes care of myself"), sensory anticipation, permission ("an hour that's yours"), aesthetic shelf-presence.
- **Journey:** IG/TikTok sleepmaxxing content → saves post → retargeted with founder story → PDP → reads FAQ on absorption honesty → buys Ritual Set → subscribes to refills after jar 1.
- **Review she'd write:** "I was sceptical it was just pretty salt. Three weeks in, my 9pm bath is the thing I look forward to all day. I'm calmer at bedtime and my skin has never been softer. The jar is so beautiful it lives on the bath edge."
- **Questions she asks:** Does it stain the tub? Is it safe in pregnancy? How many baths per jar? What if I only have a shower? (→ foot-soak protocol + mist path)
