Privacy.

The short version: we collect what we need to ship your order and send the emails you asked for. We never sell your data.

What we collect

Name, delivery address, email, phone (for delivery updates), and order history. If you opt in: your usual bedtime, so the Rest Notes arrive at the right hour. Payment details are handled by our payment processors and never touch our servers.

What we use it for

Fulfilling orders, support, the emails/SMS you opted into, and anonymised analytics to improve the store. Marketing cookies fire only after consent.

Who we share it with

Only the services that operate the store: our fulfilment centre, payment processors, email platform and analytics — each under contract. Never sold, never rented.

Your controls

Unsubscribe is one click on every email; STOP ends SMS. Email hello@evenfall.com.au to access, correct or delete your data — we action requests within 30 days under the Australian Privacy Principles.

This plain-English summary sits above the full policy, which is reviewed by our lawyers before launch and whenever practices change. Last updated July 2026.
