What a bath can and can't do.

We won't tell you magnesium soaks through skin and fixes your sleep — the science there is genuinely unsettled, and brands that pretend otherwise are selling you certainty they don't have.

Here's what is well-established: a warm bath before bed helps your body do what it's designed to do — warm, then cool, then rest. Twenty screen-free minutes does the rest.
