## 1. The honesty framework (publish this, follow this)

Three content classes. Every asset gets classed before it's made:

### CLASS 1 — REAL FOOTAGE REQUIRED (no AI substitution, ever)
- All product performance demonstrations (the catch demo, peel-away test, side-by-side, hands-free seating)
- All before/after results
- All fit-panel and testing footage
- All customer/creator/MUA reactions, testimonials and reviews
- Durability evidence (wash counts, IPA tests)
- Anything a reasonable viewer would take as proof the product works

### CLASS 2 — AI-ASSISTED (AI helps; the truth content is real)
- Storyboards and shot lists for real shoots
- Background plates/set extensions behind real footage (e.g., studio ambience)
- B-roll that carries no performance claim (silk, light, glitter-in-air mood shots)
- Editing assistance, captions, cutdowns, hook-variant text
- Colour/look development for real footage

### CLASS 3 — FULLY GENERATED CREATIVE CONCEPT (labelled "concept/AI visual")
- Cinematic brand-world films (dreamlike product universes)
- Product visualisation renders pre-tooling (always captioned as renders)
- Moodboards, packaging explorations, campaign concepts
- Launch-scene fantasies (product on a runway of light etc.) — clearly stylised beyond literal belief, still labelled

**Forbidden regardless of class:** fake customers or reviews; synthetic "professional MUA" endorsers; simulated product performance presented as real; misleading before/afters; using Soul ID to put words in any real person's mouth. Kat's Soul ID may be used **only** with her written consent, only for Class 2/3 assets she approves individually, and never to fabricate a demonstration.

**Disclosure standard:** Class 3 posts carry "AI concept visual" in-caption; Class 2 background/b-roll use is covered by the published channel policy ("our demos are always real; some backgrounds and concept shots are AI-assisted").

## 2. Workflow

1. **Plan (Claude/Codex):** pick the week's scripts (`07_CONTENT_SYSTEM.md`), class each asset 1/2/3.
2. **Storyboard (Soul 2.0 images):** generate 4–6 frame boards for real shoots — saves filming time.
3. **Generate (Class 2/3):** stills in Soul 2.0 → motion in DoP/Cinema Studio for camera-controlled moves; pick engine per shot (DoP for cinematic product moves; Veo/Kling-class for naturalistic motion).
4. **Shoot (Class 1):** Sunday batch per the filming plan; storyboards in hand.
5. **Assemble:** real demo = spine of every product video; AI b-roll ≤30% of runtime on brand films, 0% of "proof" moments.
6. **Label & log:** asset register records class, prompt, model, date — the audit trail behind the honesty policy.
