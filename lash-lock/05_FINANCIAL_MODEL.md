## What must be true for each scenario

- **S1 (validation era):** waitlist ≥300; preorder conversion ≥3%; content posted 5×/week; nothing viral required. This is the *expected* first 60–90 days if gates pass.
- **S2 (months 3–9):** 2–3 videos exceeding 100k views total lifetime; 500+ email list; review flywheel started (≥30 reviews at 4.5★+); repeat/gift purchases appearing. Requires consistent cadence, not luck.
- **S3 (months 9–18):** an owned audience (~20k+ followers) converting predictably; creator/UGC engine (10+ creators posting monthly); Pro Kit adopted by ≥100 working MUAs; email driving 25%+ of revenue; possibly first wholesale accounts. Requires the brand to be *the* name in its niche in AU.
- **S4 (month 18+ outlier):** a genuine viral cycle *plus* retail distribution (Priceline/Adore-class conversations, as Quick Flick achieved — precedent VERIFIED) *plus* paid acquisition working at CAC <$15. Treat as an option, never a plan. Inventory and cash-flow discipline (never >6 weeks cover) protects against planning for S4 and living S1.

**Cash-flow warning:** inventory is paid 60–90 days before revenue returns. At S3, a reorder is ~$8–12k cash out. Growth eats cash before it pays — reorders must be funded from gross profit, not hope. ⚖️ Accountant to set aside GST/income-tax provisions from month one of profitability.
