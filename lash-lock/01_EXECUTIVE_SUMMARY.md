## The business in one paragraph

Eye-makeup fallout — glitter, shimmer and pigment ruining the finished under-eye — is a real, constant, visually demonstrable problem (VERIFIED: an entire product niche exists to fight it, from Shadow Shields' disposables to $6 generic silicone paddles). Nobody has built the premium, artist-led, reusable answer. **Lash Lock™** is an anatomically contoured, feather-edged silicone shield with a fallout catch-channel and a liner/mascara wing edge, sold as a **Duo Kit (2 shields + hygiene case + mini brush + technique cards) at AUD $34.95 (EST)** under a new house brand, **Katch Beauty** — named for Kat, a working professional makeup artist whose real demonstrations are the marketing engine. Organic short-form video (the 7-second "watch it catch everything" demo) is the primary channel; Higgsfield AI assists with concept and b-roll content under a strict published honesty policy (real demos only, ever). The Australian precedent is verified: Quick Flick turned one eye tool into a $10M, 1,500-store brand.

## The two existential risks (red-team, Round 19)

1. **Demand depth:** free workarounds (tissue, powder) may be "good enough" — tested for ~$0 by gates 1/3/7 before any tooling.
2. **Copyability:** the shape will be cloned if it works — countered by AU registered design (filed pre-reveal), the kit/system format, and the one asset no factory can copy: Kat's professional credibility on camera.
