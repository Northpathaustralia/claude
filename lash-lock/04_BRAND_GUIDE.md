## 6.2 Top five

1. **Katch Beauty (39)** — "Catch" (the product's literal job) + "Kat" (the founder-face). Ownable story, expandable, strong handles, premium-able with the right typography.
2. **Halo & Arc (39)** — elegant, geometric (the product *is* an arc under the eye's halo), very premium, highly expandable; weaker instant beauty-problem relevance.
3. **Smudge Society (36)** — community-flavoured, great social energy; slightly casual for premium packaging.
4. **Katch & Co. (35)** — Katch story with a broader-than-beauty wrapper.
5. **Shieldé / Aurelle (34)** — reserve premium options.

## 6.3 Brand architecture decision

- **Company/umbrella brand: KATCH BEAUTY** — provisional pending clearance; fallback order: Halo & Arc → Smudge Society.
- **Hero product name: LASH LOCK™** — kept as the *product*, not the company. As a product name its lash-crowding risk is lower (used descriptively under a house mark) though still requiring clearance, and it names the flagship benefit hero moment: lock the look in place, nothing escapes onto the skin.
- So: **"LASH LOCK™ by Katch Beauty — the Clean Eye System."** Lash Lock is the hero product under a larger beauty brand built to hold future tools (Brow Lock, Lip Lock… naming runway is obvious and fun).
- **Anniversary meaning:** the company is named for Kat. This is disclosed in the founder story and the presentation — it is the emotional heart of the brand.

## 6.4 Brand core

- **Brand story:** Katch Beauty began with a working makeup artist on the Gold Coast who was tired of watching a perfect base get ruined in the last five minutes — and a partner who believed her craft deserved a company built around it. The first tool, Lash Lock, does the simplest thing in beauty: it catches the mess before it happens.
- **Mission:** give every person who loves makeup the clean, professional finish an artist would give them — with tools honest enough to show, not tell.
- **Vision:** the world's most trusted name in clean-application beauty tools.
- **Values:** Craft truth (real demos, always) · Respect the face (skin-safe, gentle, hygienic) · Waste less (reusable beats disposable) · Teach generously (education is the marketing) · Premium without pretence.
- **Personality:** the brilliant working artist friend — warm, direct, quietly confident, a little playful, never preachy.
- **Customer promise:** *Your base stays flawless. Every time. Or your money back (30 days).*
- **Brand voice:** first-person warmth ("we", "your"), short sentences, artist's vocabulary used naturally, zero fake hype, honest about what the tool doesn't do. Says "catches fallout"; never says "miracle", "revolutionary", "antibacterial".
- **Taglines:** primary — **"Lock the look. Lose the mess."** · secondary — "Caught before it counts." · pro line — "Every client. Every time. Clean." · playful — "Glitter without the guilt trip."

## 6.5 Visual system

- **Colour system:**
  - *Katch Blush* — soft warm pink `#F2C9C2` (primary field)
  - *Deep Espresso* — near-black brown `#2B1E1C` (type, premium anchor)
  - *Porcelain* — warm off-white `#FAF5F0` (backgrounds)
  - *Gilded Sand* — muted gold `#C9A227` (accents, foil moments only)
  - *Sage Whisper* — grey-green `#AEBBA8` (secondary/pro line accent)
  - Rule: blush + porcelain fields, espresso type, gold ≤5% of any layout, sage reserved for Pro Kit.
- **Fonts:** headlines — a modern high-contrast serif (e.g. Fraunces, Canela-class); body — a humanist sans (e.g. Inter, Söhne-class); product/label small caps with generous tracking. Free-tier pairing: **Fraunces + Inter** (both open licence, VERIFIED as open-source families).
- **Logo direction:** wordmark "KATCH" in the serif, with the crossbar of the "A" drawn as a subtle crescent arc (the product silhouette); "BEAUTY" in tracked small caps beneath. No literal eye imagery — the arc is the whole idea.
- **Icon direction:** standalone crescent-arc glyph enclosing a single falling dot (the caught particle) — works at 16 px, embosses beautifully on silicone and case lid.
- **Packaging direction:** porcelain matte box, blush interior reveal, espresso type, single gold-foil arc icon; inside lid prints the promise line; technique cards as premium inclusions; recyclable board, no PVC windows.
- **Photography direction:** real skin with texture (no plastic retouching), warm daylight, macro honesty on the tool, hands always in-frame doing real work; pro line shot moodier on espresso backgrounds.
- **Social visual direction:** vertical-first; consistent porcelain/blush set with one signature prop (a gold tray of caught glitter); captions in-frame in the serif; Kat's face and hands as the recurring visual anchor; demo shots always show the *mess being caught* — the channel of glitter is the brand's money shot.
