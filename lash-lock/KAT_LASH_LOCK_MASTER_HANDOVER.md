## 6. Product design and specifications currently agreed (see `03_PRODUCT_SPECIFICATION.md` for full detail)

**PROVISIONAL v1 spec, pending fit-panel validation:**
- Shape: crescent with feather-thin contact edge (tapering to 0.5mm), raised 4mm catch-channel on outer lower face, straightened wing-edge section for liner/mascara use, teardrop grip tab
- Dimensions: ~78mm wide × 26mm tall × 1.6mm body thickness (test ranges given, not fixed)

- No adhesive — holds by shape/conformity, hands-free
- **Duo Kit** (hero): 2 shields + ventilated clamshell case + mini fallout fan brush + 4 technique cards
- **Pro Kit**: 4 shields (Noir) + larger caddy + hygiene workflow card

**REQUIRES VALIDATION:** actual fit success rate across face shapes (target ≥7/10 in a 10-face panel); durability (30-wash + IPA-wipe claims); whether "IPA-sanitisable" claim is materially true (supplier cert needed before any hygiene claim is printed).

## 8. Target customer and customer problem

**Problem (VERIFIED via live research as a real, marketed-against problem — see `02` Round 1–2 for sources):** eyeshadow/glitter "fallout" and mascara/liner transfer ruins finished under-eye base makeup during application. Current $0 workarounds (tissue, powder) are clumsy; existing paid competitors (Shadow Shields, ShadowMoon, J.Cat, Frends Beauty, generics) are cheap, disposable, or generic-fit with no premium brand or hygiene system — this gap is the identified white space.

**Customers:**
1. Primary — glam/occasion makeup wearers, 18–45, active on TikTok/Reels, who do shimmer/glitter looks
2. Secondary — working professional makeup artists (MUAs) who face the problem on every client and need hygiene + speed

**REQUIRES VALIDATION:** whether demand is deep enough to support premium pricing rather than the $6-generic ceiling most existing products sit at — this is flagged as the single most important open question (Round 1.5, Round 19 W1).
