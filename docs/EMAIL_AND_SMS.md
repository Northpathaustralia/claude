# EMAIL & SMS FLOWS — TIDESTATE

Platform: Klaviyo (free tier to 250 profiles → paid). SMS: collect consent at checkout +
VIP form; **SMS reserved for drops, restocks of requested items, and delivery updates
only** — max 2–4/month. All marketing complies with the Spam Act 2003: express consent,
sender ID, working unsubscribe in every message (see AUSTRALIAN_SETUP.md).

Voice: founder-personal, plain text over heavy design for founder sends; product emails
in brand template (email header asset in /assets/packaging-concepts.svg set).

## FLOW 6 — CART ABANDONMENT — 3 emails (+1 SMS if consented)
1h: "Left something at first light?" — cart contents, fit-tool link, no discount.
24h: "The two questions everyone asks" — sizing + shipping/returns answers.
48h: "Real stock talk" — only if true, current stock level; otherwise product-proof video.
SMS at 20h (if consented, high-value carts only): "Your TIDESTATE cart's holding. Sizes
move fast on tees — reply SIZE if you want fit help. Stop=unsub."
**No panic discounts** — protects price integrity.

## FLOW 9 — POST-PURCHASE — 5 emails
T+0 confirmation (founder tone: "Order in. Here's exactly what happens next").
T+1 "While it ships": care guide + fit-video for their items.
Delivery day: "It's there. Two things before you cut the satchel." (unboxing + size-check

## FLOW 10 — FIT ASSISTANCE (triggered by exchange request or SIZE reply)
Instant: measurement walkthrough + which size to swap to; prepaid label for first
exchange (first-time customers). Human handoff for edge cases.

## FLOW 11 — PRODUCT CARE (T+21) 
"Make it last years, not washes": cold wash, no dryer for heavyweights, embroidery care.
Positions durability as shared responsibility → fewer quality complaints.

## FLOW 12 — REVIEW REQUEST (T+14, delivered orders only)
"30 seconds of honesty, please — including if we got something wrong." Photo/video
reviews earn store credit ($10) **clearly disclosed on the review**. Never incentivise
positive-only.

## FLOW 13 — REFERRAL (T+30, satisfied reviewers)
"Give a mate first-exchange-free + $15; get $15 when they order." Simple, margin-safe
(within the 11% allowance).

## FLOW 14 — WIN-BACK (90/180 days inactive)
90d: "Since you've been gone: two restocks and a new colour you voted on."
180d: "One email a season, that's all — want to stay?" (sunset policy keeps list healthy).

## FLOW 15 — RESTOCK (back-in-stock subscribers)
Instant on restock: "It's back — {product} in {size}. You asked, so you get 24h before
we tell anyone else." + matching SMS if consented. First-come honesty: no overselling.

## FLOW 16 — BIRTHDAY (collected optionally at account creation)
"No discount spam — just a free BREAKLINE sticker pack in your next order this month,
and first pick of anything that restocks. Happy birthday from the coast."

## FLOW 17 — VIP CUSTOMER (2+ orders or $300+ lifetime)

numbered patch in your next parcel." Quarterly: private preview of next drop + vote
that actually decides something.
