# TIDESTATE — BRAND BLUEPRINT

**Category created:** CLIMATE-BUILT PREMIUM STREETWEAR
**HQ:** Gold Coast, Queensland, Australia · **Founder:** James Forycki

---

## 1. Core positioning

> **Premium streetwear engineered for the Australian climate.**
> **Built on the Gold Coast for first light, late nights and everything between.**

Australian streetwear is dominated by heavyweight fleece designed for Melbourne winters
and imported northern-hemisphere assumptions. TIDESTATE builds the opposite: garments with
**heavyweight presence and warm-climate physics** — structured oversized silhouettes that
hold their shape at 30°C, breathe through a training session, and still look premium at
dinner. One outfit that moves through an entire Australian day.

**Primary tagline:** `CHANGE YOUR STATE.`
(State of mind. State of dress. The state you live in. The state you're building.)

## 2. The customer promise

*"Wear the same premium outfit to breakfast, to work, to the gym, to the airport, to the
beach, to dinner — and have it look right in every room."*

We keep this promise through the **COASTFORM Construction** system (see
PRODUCT_RANGE.md / MANUFACTURING_BRIEF.md): structured compact-knit fabrics, pre-shrunk
and enzyme-washed, reinforced necks, anti-twist construction, real-body fit data.

## 3. Target customer

**Primary:** men and women ~23–42, metro + coastal Australia (Gold Coast, Brisbane,
Sydney, Melbourne, Newcastle, Central Coast, Sunshine Coast, Perth).
Into training, travel, business-building, cars, coffee, restaurants, beach life, music,
social events. Values quality; won't pay designer-luxury prices. Buys clothes that feel
premium, photograph well and survive repeat wear.

**Persona anchors**
- *"Jordan, 29, Burleigh"* — trains 5.30am, agency job, weekend markets + dinners; wants
  one wardrobe, not three.
- *"Ash, 34, Sydney"* — runs a small business, flies BNE–SYD monthly, lives in premium
  basics; hates gym-merch logos at meetings.

## 4. Brand personality

Confident. Premium. Direct. Ambitious. Relaxed. Australian. Bold without arrogance.
Motivational **through product stories and founder reality**, never through poster quotes.

**Voice rules**
- Short sentences. Concrete nouns. Australian rhythm without slang cosplay.
- Talk about millimetres, grams and wash counts — proof, not adjectives.
- Never: "be the best version of yourself", "never give up", "hustle harder", "dream big".
- Founder-led, not influencer-merch: James narrates decisions, not lifestyle flexes.
- Honesty is structural: real stock, real timelines, real costs, real mistakes.

## 7. Visual identity

### 7.1 Colour system

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **TIDESTATE INK** | `#090909` | Primary ground, wordmark, garments |
| **TIDESTATE BONE** | `#F1EEE6` | Light ground, garments, packaging interior |
| **CONCRETE** | `#BFC0BA` | Secondary neutral, dividers, secondary text |
| **DEEP COBALT** | `#1747FF` | Accent ≤10% of any layout; CTAs, tide-data lines |
| **SIGNAL ORANGE** | `#FF4A1F` | Alert accent ≤5%; drop tags, sale-of-truth markers |

Rule: Ink + Bone carry 85%+ of every surface. Cobalt is the "data" colour, Orange is the
"signal" colour. Never both accents at equal weight in one composition.

### 7.2 Design language

Inspired by: Australian coastal infrastructure, tide charts, road markings, location
coordinates (28.0167°S 153.4000°E), industrial signage, sunrise/sunset tables, transit
graphics, architectural type. **Banned:** palm trees, souvenir surf clichés, generic
varsity/collegiate layouts, US college crests.

Recurring motifs (all original, specified in `/assets`):
- **Tide bar** — a horizontal bar chart of a real GC tide day, abstracted to 5 bars.
- **Coordinate lockup** — `28.0167°S / 153.4000°E` in mono type under the wordmark.
- **State line** — a single cobalt rule that "rises" through layouts like a tide gauge.
- **Signal chip** — small orange rectangle used like a road-marker on tags and drops.

### 7.3 Typography (open-source, commercially usable)

| Role | Font | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| Display / wordmark base | **Archivo (Expanded, 600–800)** | SIL OFL 1.1 — free commercial use, embeddable |
| Body / UI | **Archivo (400–500)** | SIL OFL 1.1 |
| Data / mono accents | **Space Grotesk** (or IBM Plex Mono for tables) | SIL OFL 1.1 |

OFL requirements: fonts may be used, embedded and sold with products; the fonts
themselves can't be *sold standalone*; reserved font names can't be reused for derivative
fonts. No attribution required in marketing materials. Keep licence files with brand kit.

## 8. Founder story (approved narrative — do not embellish)

James Forycki is building TIDESTATE on the Gold Coast. He's spent years building
businesses and working directly with people, and kept hitting the same wardrobe problem:
the day doesn't happen in one outfit's worth of settings. Training at first light, client
work by nine, the airport by Thursday, the beach on Saturday, dinner that night — and
nothing premium was actually built for that sequence in this climate. Gym merch looked
like gym merch. Fashion pieces couldn't handle a Queensland February.

So he's building the answer, publicly. Every sample, every supplier call, every mistake
and every millimetre-level argument goes on camera. The community sees the wins and the
duds, and votes on what gets made. No invented backstory, no fake factory tours, no
pretending to be bigger than it is — just a company being built in daylight.

*(Rules: no invented achievements, no corporate-CEO voice, no "empire" talk. James sounds
like a Gold Coast operator explaining decisions to mates who happen to be customers.)*

## 9. The three marketing engines

1. **THE FOUNDER BUILD** — document everything: naming, sketches, bad samples, fabric
   tests, supplier calls, packaging, first order, first 100 orders, mistakes, fixes.
2. **THE PRODUCT PROOF** — show, never claim: neck-rib comparisons, shrink tests, seam
   pulls, GSM scales, breathability demos, cost breakdowns. Never trash competitors by name.
3. **THE GOLD COAST UNIFORM** — one outfit filmed through one real day: 5am carpark,
   café, office, gym, airport, beach, dinner. The repeatable visual signature.

Full execution in CONTENT_STRATEGY.md, 100_VIDEO_HOOKS.md, PAID_AD_PLAN.md.

## 10. Honesty standards (non-negotiable)

No fake scarcity, timers, reviews, testimonials or sales. Stock counts shown only when
true. Pre-orders only with accurate communicated delivery dates. No medical/performance
fabric claims without evidence — COASTFORM is described as a **construction standard**,
not a technology claim. Competitor comparisons use generic samples, never named brands.
