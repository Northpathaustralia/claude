# CONTENT STRATEGY — THE THREE ENGINES

Machine-readable 90-day schedule: `/data/content-calendar.csv`. Hooks library:
`100_VIDEO_HOOKS.md`. Every script below uses the 9-field format required
(opening visual, first line, on-screen text, shot list, length, caption, CTA, target,
why it works) in compact notation.

**Cadence:** 1 founder video/day (phone-shot), 3 product-proof/week, 3 lifestyle/week,
1 long-form recap/week. Batch filmed Mondays + opportunistically.

**Voice guardrails:** no poster motivation, no fake urgency, no competitor naming,
Australian and direct. Mistakes are content, not PR problems.

---

## ENGINE ONE — THE FOUNDER BUILD (30 founder-video scripts)

Format per script: **Open** (first frame) · **Line 1** (spoken) · **OST** (on-screen
text) · **Shots** · **Len** · **Caption** · **CTA** · **Target** · **Why**.

1. **The Problem.** Open: James in 3 different outfits laid on bed. Line 1: "I change clothes three times a day and I'm sick of it." OST: "why I'm starting a clothing company". Shots: outfit pile → calendar → GC skyline. 30s. Caption: Day 1. Building it in public. CTA: Follow the build. Target: 25–40 builders. Why: relatable pain + public-commitment hook.
2. **Naming Fight.** Open: whiteboard with 20 crossed-out names. Line 1: "Naming a company is easy until it's legal." OST: "20 names. 1 survivor." Shots: whiteboard, IP Australia screen (blurred), shortlist. 45s. Caption: Which would you wear? CTA: Vote in comments. Target: all. Why: participation drives comments → reach.
3. **The $1,200 Lawyer Call.** Line 1: "I just spent $1,200 to be told 'maybe'." Why: honest cost transparency is rare.
4. **First Sample Unboxing (it's bad).** Open: knife on satchel. Line 1: "This is not what I ordered." Why: failure content outperforms success content.
5. **The 8mm Argument.** Line 1: "We argued for three weeks over eight millimetres." Neck rib macro. Why: obsession signals quality.
6. **What a Tee Actually Costs.** Whiteboard breakdown $ by component. Why: transparency builds trust + pre-sells price.
7. **Fabric Scale Test.** Weighing 3 tees on kitchen scale. "Yours is 160. Ours is 240. Here's what that means in February."
8. **The Colour Vote.** 3 Washed Ink shades held to camera. CTA: comment 1/2/3. Community-decides mechanic.
9. **Supplier Call Notes.** Line 1: "A factory just tried to talk me out of quality. Good." (no names).
10. **Fit Day.** 5 mates, 5 bodies, 1 tape measure. Why: sets up the fit tool story.
11. **Wash Test Week.** Timelapse of 3 washes + tape measure. "Size L is still size L."
12. **The Money Video.** "Here's exactly what I've spent so far." Running total OST.
13. **Packaging Decisions.** Mailer options on table; cost per unit on screen.
14. **The Logo Almost Sucked.** Early drafts → final. Why: process > reveal.
15. **First Production Order.** Hand hovering over "confirm" button. "This is $6k. Filmed live so I can't chicken out."
16. **Why I'm Not Doing 30 Products.** "Five. That's it. Here's why." Range discipline story.
17. **VIP List Milestone.** "500 of you now. First access is yours. That's the whole deal."
18. **Photoshoot on a Phone.** BTS golden hour Burleigh. "No agency. Here's the setup: $0."
19. **The First Order.** Screen-record of order #1001 arriving. Raw reaction. Why: milestone authenticity.
20. **Packing Order #1.** Full ritual, tissue to handwritten card.
21. **First 100 Orders Recap.** Numbers on screen: orders, mistakes, refunds given.
22. **A Refund I'm Proud Of.** "We sent the wrong size. Here's what we did." CX as content.
23. **What Sold Out / What Didn't.** Honest sell-through chart. "The cap surprised us."
24. **Restock Decision Live.** Spreadsheet on screen; community input on next colour.
25. **The Mistake That Cost $800.** Print misalignment batch. "These will never be sold. They're going to the crew who helped."
26. **Drop 002 Sketches.** Early look; comment-gated: "500 comments and I'll reveal the third piece."
27. **Event #1 Recap.** Sunrise walk footage. "25 strangers showed up at 5.45am."
28. **Answering the Hard DM.** "Someone asked why they shouldn't just buy a $30 tee. Fair question." → proof answer.
29. **90-Day Numbers, All of Them.** Revenue, spend, profit, hours. Why: radical transparency is the moat.
30. **What's Next.** Model 2 factory samples arriving. "This is where it gets real."
