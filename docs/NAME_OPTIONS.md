# BRAND NAME OPTIONS & SCORING

**Working name: TIDESTATE** (used throughout the build). This document generates and
scores 20 original alternatives so the founder can make a final, informed choice.

> ⚠️ **All availability notes below are PRELIMINARY DESK CHECKS ONLY** (search-engine and
> obvious-use scans, July 2026). They are **not** trademark searches, not legal advice and
> not confirmation of availability. Before committing to any name James must:
> 1. Search **IP Australia** (search.ipaustralia.gov.au — Australian Trade Mark Search) in classes **25** (clothing), **35** (retail) and ideally **18/24**.
> 2. Engage a trademark attorney for a clearance opinion + file an application.
> 3. Check ASIC business/company name availability.
> 4. Confirm domain + social handles the same day, and buy before announcing anything.

## Scoring method

Each name is scored /10 on ten criteria, summed to /100:
**MEM** memorability · **VIS** visual-identity potential · **PREM** premium positioning ·
**AUS** Australian relevance · **INT** international scalability · **PRON** pronunciation ·
**DOM** domain potential (.com / .com.au realistically obtainable) · **SOC** IG/TikTok
handle potential · **TM** trademark risk (10 = lowest apparent risk) · **COMM** ability to
become a lifestyle community.

## Recommendation

**Proceed with TIDESTATE (89/100)** — highest composite score, and the only candidate where
the name, the tagline (**CHANGE YOUR STATE**), the product system (**COASTFORM**), the drop
(**FIRST LIGHT**) and the community (**FIRST LIGHT CLUB**) lock together as one architecture:

- *Tide* = coast, timing, data (tide charts drive the visual identity).
- *State* = state of mind, state of dress, Queensland "the state", and the imperative
  tagline **CHANGE YOUR STATE**.
- Coined compound → distinctive, ownable, pronounceable in every English market, no
  meaning collisions found in major non-English markets on desk review.
- Backup shortlist if clearance fails: **DAYRANGE (80)**, then **HIGHSET (78)** /
  **SWELLSTATE (78)**.

## Desk-check summary (preliminary only)

- Web/social scan (July 2026) found **no active apparel brand trading as "Tidestate"** in
  Australia, US or UK on obvious channels. A handful of unrelated uses of "tide state"
  as a descriptive phrase (marine apps, tide tables) exist — descriptive uses in other
  categories are normal and not automatically blocking, **but only a professional search
  can confirm**.
- Handles to attempt immediately: `@tidestate`, `@tidestate.au`, `@wearetidestate`.
- Domains to attempt immediately: `tidestate.com`, `tidestate.com.au`, `tidestate.co`,
  with `tidestate.store` as fallback.

3. Register the trademark (word mark first; logo mark second).
4. Secure domains + handles the same day the name is confirmed.
5. Re-run the same checks for COASTFORM, FIRST LIGHT CLUB and STATE CREW as sub-brands.
