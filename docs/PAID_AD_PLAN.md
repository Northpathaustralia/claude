# PAID MARKETING PLAN — META (LEAN)

Principle: **ads amplify proof, they don't replace it.** Nothing gets paid spend until it
has organic signal. All AUD, all include-GST unless noted.

## 1. The economics that govern everything

From UNIT_ECONOMICS.md (Model 1 launch): AOV $138 → ex-GST revenue $125.45, contribution
before ads ≈ $58.80/order (47%). Model 2: ≈ $66.30 (53%).

| Metric | Model 1 | Model 2 |
|---|---|---|
| **Break-even ROAS** (revenue ÷ spend where contribution = spend) | **2.13** | **1.89** |
| Target blended CAC | ≤ $35 | ≤ $38 |
| Hard-stop CAC (pause + fix creative) | $45 | $50 |
| Target new-customer ROAS | ≥ 2.6 | ≥ 2.4 |

**Rules:**
- Never scale on revenue or platform-ROAS alone — check **contribution margin after
  product cost, shipping, packaging, payment fees, discounts, returns and GST**, weekly,
  in a spreadsheet the platform can't massage.
- Separate **new customer** vs **returning**: retargeting and email will always look
  great; they don't prove acquisition works. Judge prospecting on new-customer CAC only.
- First-order breakeven is acceptable **only because** repeat rate and email revenue are
  tracked; if 60-day repeat rate <8%, first orders must be profitable on their own.

## 2. Budget structures

### $30/day (launch, weeks 1–4)
| Campaign | Budget | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| ASC/Sales — creative testing | $25 | 1 campaign, 1 ad set, 4–6 creatives (proof + uniform + founder), broad AU 23–45, auto placements |
| Retargeting | $5 | 30-day engagers + site visitors + ATC; honest-proof creatives, no discount pressure |

At $30/day expect ~1 purchase/day at $35 CAC — enough signal after 2–3 weeks, not before.
Kill creatives at >$15 spend with no ATC; keep anything with CPA < $45 and 2%+ CTR.

### $75/day (weeks 5–10, only if blended CAC ≤ $40)
| Campaign | Budget |
|---|---|
| Prospecting — proven winners (2–3 creatives) | $45 |
| Creative testing — 4 new/week | $20 |
| Retargeting + cart/checkout recovery | $10 |

### $150/day (scale, only after 2 consecutive weeks ≥ target ROAS)
| Campaign | Budget |
|---|---|
| Prospecting — winners, broad | $90 |
| Testing lab (new angles: women's styling, jersey story, bundles) | $35 |
| Retargeting (segmented: viewed / carted / bought-adjacent) | $15 |
| Drop/event promos (First Light Club, restocks) | $10 |

## 3. Testing method

- **Creative > audience.** Broad targeting; the creative is the targeting. Test one
  variable at a time: hook (first 3s) → format → offer framing.
- Name convention: `[engine]-[hook#]-[format]-[v]` (e.g. `proof-h7-neckrib-15s-v2`).
- Minimum decision data: 2,000 impressions or $15 spend per creative.
- Weekly ritual (Fridays): kill / iterate / scale table, and log fatigue — when frequency
  >2.5 and CTR drops ~30% from peak, rotate the hook, keep the body.

## 4. Funnel + measurement

- Pixel + Conversions API via Shopify; UTM everything; verify with post-purchase survey
  ("how did you find us?") — survey beats attribution fights at this size.
- Retargeting windows: 14-day ATC (strongest), 30-day view, 180-day buyers (exclude from
  prospecting).
- Creative for retargeting = answers to objections: sizing (fit tool), price (cost
  anatomy), trust (founder + reviews once real).

## 5. Scaling rules & guardrails

1. Increase budget ≤25% per 48h; big jumps reset learning.
2. Scale only creatives with stable CPA over ≥7 days.
3. If new-customer CAC > hard-stop for 5 consecutive days → drop to $30/day, fix
   creative, don't "spend through it".
4. Ads capped at 20% of trailing-30-day revenue until profitable two months running.
5. No discount-led ads. Offer = product truth + free-shipping threshold + free first
   exchange.

## 6. 20 launch ad creatives (matrix)

**Proof (6):** neck-rib duel 15s · wash-test timelapse 30s · kitchen-scale GSM 15s ·
cost-anatomy pie 30s · phone-pocket torture 15s · hood-stand test 15s.
**Uniform (5):** 18-hour day hero 30s · airport speedrun 15s · gym-to-dinner cut 15s ·
his 5am/her 9pm hoodie 15s · February heat test 30s.
**Founder (4):** "why five products" 30s · "$1,200 lawyer call" 30s · first-100-orders
recap 30s · honest stock policy 15s.
**UGC cut-downs (3):** creator day-vlog 15s · fit-report on 5 bodies 30s · numbered cap
unboxing 15s.
**Offer-honesty (2):** "free shipping over $150, free first exchange, no fake timers —
that's the whole trick" 15s · bundle maths ("the uniform for $235") 15s.

Each runs 4:5 + 9:16 crops, captions burned in, hook text in first 1.5s, product +
price + CTA ("Shop DROP 001") in final 3s.

## 7. What we do NOT do

No fake scarcity/timer ads · no competitor-name targeting or comparison ads · no bought
engagement · no broad international spend until AU CAC is proven · no scaling into a
stockout (check cover ≥ 4 weeks before raising budget).
