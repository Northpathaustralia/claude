# UNIT ECONOMICS — THREE LAUNCH MODELS

All figures **AUD, conservative planning estimates — not supplier quotations**. GST
treated at 10% on domestic sales (registration recommended from day one if projecting
>$75k turnover — see AUSTRALIAN_SETUP.md). Retail prices include GST; margins computed
ex-GST.

## Shared assumptions

| Assumption | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Launch AOV (with bundles) | $138 | 1.55 units/order across the 5-product range |
| Payment fees | 1.9% + $0.30 | Shopify Payments AU standard tier |
| Domestic shipping cost | $9.50 avg | AusPost/courier satchel incl. packaging handling |
| Shipping revenue | $0 over $150; $9.95 under | Free-shipping threshold policy |
| Packaging per order | $2.80 | Mailer, tissue, tag, card, stickers |
| Returns/exchange allowance | 6% of revenue | Apparel DTC norm 5–10%; free first exchange policy |
| Discount allowance | 5% of revenue | VIP list + bundles |
| 3PL pick/pack (if used) | $5.50/order | Otherwise founder self-fulfils at launch |

## MODEL 1 — LEAN VALIDATION (premium blanks + local decoration)

AS Colour-tier premium blanks, Gold Coast print/embroidery, small runs, pre-orders
allowed only with honest delivery dates. Range limited to Tee, Hoodie, Cap (short and
jersey wait for Model 2 — blanks can't hit their spec).

### Per-unit costs (estimates)

| Item | Tee | Hoodie | Cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Premium blank | $14.00 | $34.00 | $8.00 |
| Print/embroidery | $7.50 | $9.00 | $6.50 |
| Relabel/finishing | $2.50 | $2.50 | $1.00 |
| **Unit COGS** | **$24.00** | **$45.50** | **$15.50** |
| Retail | $89 | $179 | $54 |
| GM% ex-GST | 70% | 72% | 68% |

### Startup budget

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Inventory: 100 tees, 50 hoodies, 75 caps | $5,838 |
| Samples & test prints | $600 |
| Packaging (250 orders' worth) | $700 |
| Shopify + domain + email tools (3 months) | $350 |
| Photography/content (self-shot + 1 creator day) | $500 |
| Trademark search + application (1 class, attorney-assisted) | $1,200 |
| Contingency 10% | $920 |
| **Total cash required** | **≈ $10,100** |

*(A sub-$3k ultra-lean variant exists — see COST CONTROL budgets below.)*

### Order economics (AOV $138, one order)

| Line | $ |
|---|---|
| Revenue ex-GST | 125.45 |
| COGS (blended 30%) | −37.60 |
| Shipping + packaging | −12.30 |
| Payment fees | −2.92 |
| Returns + discount allowance (11%) | −13.80 |
| **Contribution before ads** | **58.83 (47% of ex-GST rev)** |
| Ad allowance (target CAC) | −30.00 |
| **Contribution after ads (new customer)** | **28.83** |

**Break-even orders on $10.1k startup:** ≈ 350 contribution-positive orders (or ~175 if
ads underspend and organic carries half). **Break-even ROAS** at 30% blended COGS ≈ **2.1**.
Inventory risk: low ($5.8k). Cash cycle: fast. Weakness: fit/fabric not proprietary yet.

## MODEL 3 — FULL CUSTOM CUT & SEW (scale phase, not launch)

Custom patterns, custom-milled fabric, full trim/packaging program, 300–500+ units/style.

| Item | Estimate |
|---|---|
| Inventory 2,000 units blended | $46,000 |
| Freight/duty/clearance | $5,500 |
| Pattern-making + grading + sampling | $6,000 |
| Trims/packaging tooling + stock | $6,500 |
| Working capital (3PL onboarding, ads float) | $12,000 |
| Contingency | $7,600 |

colour demand. Cash pressure: significant (60–90 day production + freight cycle).

## Model comparison

| | Model 1 | Model 2 | Model 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cash required | ~$10k | ~$41k | ~$84k |
| COGS % | 30% | 24% | 20% |
| Contribution before ads | 47% | 53% | 57% |
| Break-even ROAS | 2.1 | 1.9 | 1.75 |
| Product differentiation | Low–med | High | Highest |
| Inventory risk | Low | Moderate | High |
| Time to launch | 3–5 weeks | 10–14 weeks | 16–24 weeks |

## Recommended path

**Phase A (days 1–55): Model 1** to validate names, graphics, sizes and CAC with ~$10k
at risk. **Phase B (DROP 001 proper): Model 2** funded partly by Phase A revenue and VIP
pre-orders. **Model 3 only after** two profitable drops and a proven restock curve.

## COST CONTROL budgets (what to do at each funding level)

| Budget | Play |
|---|---|
| **<$3,000** | Model 1 with one style only (HEATFORM tee, 60 units, 2 colours) + cap sample run; free Canva/Figma design, self-shot content, organic + $10/day ads after first sales; skip 3PL; trademark search only (file later — risk noted) |
| **$5,000** | Model 1 tee + cap (100/75 units), $500 content, $1.2k trademark, $600 ads testing |
| **$10,000** | Full Model 1 as budgeted above — **recommended minimum** |
| **$20,000** | Model 1 launch + immediate Model 2 sampling in parallel; brings DROP 001 custom range forward ~6 weeks |

**Smartest option: $10k Model 1 → reinvest into Model 2.** Premium result, minimum
irreversible risk, and the samples/mistakes become the content engine rather than sunk cost.

Everything above **requires real supplier quotations** before commitment: blanks pricing,
decoration, small-batch FOB prices, freight, duty rates per HS code, 3PL rates.
