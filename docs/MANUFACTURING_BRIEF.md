## 2. The COASTFORM CONSTRUCTION standard (contract every style to this)

1. **Fabric:** compact-knit combed cotton (tees/fleece) or specified blends; supplier to
   provide mill spec sheet, composition certificate and GSM verification per lot.
2. **Pre-shrunk:** residual shrinkage ≤5% length/width after 3× AS/NZS-style domestic
   wash cycles (40°C wash, line dry). Test reports required pre-production.
3. **Enzyme wash / garment wash** where specified for hand-feel and shade (Washed Ink).
   Shade bands approved by us per batch.
4. **Neck construction (knits):** ≥2cm rib with elastane recovery, shoulder-to-shoulder
   taping, internal Bone half-moon back-neck panel; rib must recover ≥95% after 50
   stretch cycles.
5. **Anti-twist:** side-seamed bodies, skew-controlled cutting; garment skew ≤3% after
   3 washes.
6. **Stitching:** twin-needle hems; bar tacks at all pocket mouths and drawcord exits;
   flatlock where specified (jersey).
7. **Hardware:** YKK or equivalent zips; metal-tipped drawcords rated ≥20N tip retention;
   coated metal buckles (cap).
8. **Labelling:** woven neck label + woven side tide-bar label + care label including
   **QR garment passport** (URL supplied) printed on care label; country of origin and
   fibre content per Australian requirements (see AUSTRALIAN_SETUP.md).
9. **No unsubstantiated claims** are to be printed on garments or tags (no "antibacterial",
   "cooling technology" etc.). Construction facts only.

## 5. Sampling program

1. **Proto sample** per style (any colour) — fit + construction review on 3 body types
   (filmed for content). 2 rounds budgeted.
2. **Wash test:** 3 domestic cycles → measure shrink/skew/shade. Fail = revise.
3. **Pre-production sample** in correct fabric/colour/trims — signed off against this
   brief before bulk.
4. **Top-of-production sample** air-freighted for approval before ship release.
5. Gold-seal samples retained by both parties.

## 6. QC + acceptance

AQL 2.5 major / 4.0 minor on bulk inspection. Critical defects (holes, wrong labels,
missing care/fibre content, broken zips): zero tolerance. Measurement audit 10 units per
style per size run.

## 8. Supplier selection (work the tiers in UNIT_ECONOMICS.md)

- **Model 1 (validation):** AS Colour / Ramo premium blanks + Gold Coast/Brisbane
  embroidery & print houses; relabelling only where blank brand permits — confirm terms.
- **Model 2 (small batch):** AU agents or direct offshore small-batch factories
  (typically China/Vietnam/Portugal-quality tier; 150–300 MOQ) — request compliance docs
  (BSCI/Sedex), references, and run the full sampling program above.
- **Model 3 (full cut & sew):** 300–500+/style; custom patterns + full trim package.
- Always: written specs, payment via traceable terms (30/70 or LC), no full prepayment,
  pre-shipment inspection (third-party ~US$300/day) for offshore bulk.

**Requires real quotations before any commitment:** all landed costs, freight, duty
(most knitted apparel imports attract 5% duty + 10% GST at import — confirm HS codes with
a customs broker), MOQs and lead times.
