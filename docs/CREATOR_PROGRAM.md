# STATE CREW — CREATOR PROGRAM

Philosophy (learned from the competitor audit): **people genuinely wearing the gear beats
paid posts.** Seed small, measure conversion, pay only what performs. No bought
followers, no big-influencer cheques before conversion is proven.

## 1. Focus regions (phase order)
1. Gold Coast · 2. Brisbane · 3. Sunshine Coast · 4. Sydney · 5. Melbourne.
Expansion city-by-city alongside First Light Club events.

## 2. Who we want (micro-creators, 2k–50k)
Fitness/training lifestyle · café & food · cars · travel · small-business builders ·
fashion styling (esp. women's styling angle) · GC locals with real community presence.
**Signals over size:** comment quality, saves/shares, face-to-camera ability, posts
consistently, audience actually Australian.

## 3. Creator scoring system (score /100; seed at ≥65)

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Engagement quality (comments per 1k followers, real conversations) | 25 |
| Audience fit (AU %, 23–42, metro/coastal) | 20 |
| Content craft (hooks, framing, natural talking) | 15 |
| Brand alignment (trains, travels, builds something; no cringe hustle content) | 15 |
| Locality (target city, attends events) | 10 |
| Reliability signals (posts weekly, answers DMs professionally) | 10 |
| Prior brand work quality (did it convert/look organic?) | 5 |

## 4. Program tiers

| Tier | Entry | They get | We get |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Seed** | Score ≥65 | 1 numbered cap + 1 tee (~$29 cost) | No obligation; we watch what happens |
| **Crew** | Posted organically + audience responded | Full uniform (~$95 cost) + 10% affiliate code + own discount 20% | 2 posts/month best-efforts, content we may license |
| **Core** | Consistent tracked sales (≥10 orders/mo) | 15% commission + early product input + event co-host + paid content days ($150–$400/day) | Content rights package, launch-day participation |

**Customer discount on codes: 10%** (comes from the discount allowance in unit economics;
code doubles as attribution).
**Commission: 10% of ex-GST order value (15% Core), paid monthly, 30-day return buffer.**

## 5. Seeding rules
- Max 15 seeds/month at launch; every seed logged (name, score, cost, outcome).
- Seeds get a personal note from James, not a media kit. No posting demand on Seed tier.
- Numbered orange caps reserved for creators + first customers — the collectible signal.
- Kill rule: no organic post or reply after 2 seeds → stop seeding that creator.

## 6. Creator application page (built in site prototype: /creators)
Fields: name, city, handles, follower count, why the uniform fits your life, one video
link that shows your face-to-camera. Auto-reply sets expectations (score → seed → crew).

## 7. Creator brief (standard)
**Do:** film in your real day (gym, café, work, travel); show the details you actually
notice; say what you'd change; tag @tidestate + #changeyourstate; disclose gifted/paid
per ACCC/AANA rules (#gifted / "Paid partnership" toggle — mandatory).
**Don't:** scripted ad reads, fake sellout claims, competitor bashing, discount-code
spam in unrelated content.
**Formats that work:** 18-hour-day test · honest unboxing · fit report with your
height/weight · hood/pocket/wash tests · styling 1-tee-5-ways.

## 8. Content rights (recommend lawyer-reviewed template)
Seed tier: we may reshare with credit (in-app repost norms). Crew+: written licence —
organic reuse perpetual with credit; **paid-ad usage licensed per 90-day window per
creative** (renewable, small fee or included in Core day rate). Creators keep ownership;
we never claim their whole channel.

## 9. Performance tracking
Per creator: code redemptions, affiliate revenue, assisted signups (UTM), content views,
CPM-equivalent value, event attendance. Monthly league table reviewed; move creators up/
down tiers on 60-day data. Target at launch: 10 active Crew members generating ≥15% of
revenue by day 90.

## 10. Budget guardrail
Total program cost (product + commissions + content days) capped at 12% of revenue.
No individual creator >3% of monthly revenue in cost until Core-tier proven.
