# DROP 001: FIRST LIGHT — PRODUCT RANGE

Five hero products. No more. Every product answers one question: *can it move through an
entire Australian day without looking wrong in any room?*

All costs are **conservative planning estimates in AUD, ex-GST, not supplier quotations**
(see UNIT_ECONOMICS.md for the three sourcing models; landed costs below assume Model 2 —
premium small batch — at 150–300 units/style).

**COASTFORM CONSTRUCTION standard (applies to all garments):**
compact-knit combed cotton or cotton-rich blends · pre-shrunk + enzyme-washed ·
reinforced neck rib with internal sweat-management half-moon panel · anti-twist
(tubular-avoided, side-seamed, skew-controlled) · drop shoulders with structured seam ·
accurate published garment measurements · QR garment passport on care label linking to
fit videos and care guide. *(No performance/medical claims — construction facts only.)*
