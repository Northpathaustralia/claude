# AUSTRALIAN BUSINESS SETUP — PRACTICAL CHECKLIST

> **Not legal, tax or customs advice.** Items marked 👩‍⚖️ need a professional
> (lawyer / accountant / trademark attorney / customs broker). Official sources linked.

## 1. Structure & registrations
- [ ] **Business structure** 👩‍⚖️ — sole trader is cheapest to start; a **Pty Ltd company**
  is usually recommended once inventory, contracts and brand IP exist (asset separation).
  Decide with an accountant. [business.gov.au — business structures](https://business.gov.au/planning/business-structures-and-types/business-structures)
- [ ] **ABN** — free via [Australian Business Register](https://abr.gov.au). Required for
  suppliers, Shopify Payments, wholesale.
- [ ] **Business name** — register via [ASIC](https://asic.gov.au) unless trading under a
  company name that matches. (~$45/1yr, $105/3yr — confirm current fees.)
- [ ] **Company registration** (if Pty Ltd) 👩‍⚖️ — via ASIC (~$597 — confirm), plus
  director ID for James via [ABRS](https://www.abrs.gov.au).

## 2. Brand protection
- [ ] **Trademark** 👩‍⚖️ — search [IP Australia Australian Trade Mark Search](https://search.ipaustralia.gov.au/trademarks/search/quick);
  file classes **25** (clothing) + **35** (retail). TM Headstart pre-assessment ~$330/class
  + registration; attorney adds ~$700–1,500 but materially reduces risk. **Do this before
  printing labels.** All name checks in NAME_OPTIONS.md are preliminary only.
- [ ] **Domains** — tidestate.com.au requires an ABN (auDA rule). Register .com + .com.au
  + defensive variants day one.

## 3. Money & compliance
- [ ] **Bank account** — separate business account (mandatory for companies, sane for
  everyone). Consider a sub-account for GST/tax set-asides.
- [ ] **Bookkeeping** — Xero/QuickBooks/Rounded from day one; connect Shopify via app.
  Engage a bookkeeper/accountant 👩‍⚖️ by BAS #1.
- [ ] **GST** — registration **required at $75k turnover** (12-month projection counts);
  registering earlier lets you claim input credits on inventory/setup — usually smart
  here 👩‍⚖️. [ATO — GST registration](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/gst-excise-and-indirect-taxes/gst/registering-for-gst)
- [ ] **Import duty/GST** 👩‍⚖️ — most apparel imports: 5% duty on customs value + 10% GST
  at the border; confirm HS codes with a licensed customs broker. [ABF — importing](https://www.abf.gov.au/importing-exporting-and-manufacturing/importing)

## 4. Selling rules
- [ ] **Australian Consumer Law** — consumer guarantees are mandatory and sit *on top of*
  our 30-day change-of-mind policy: refunds/replacements for faulty goods can't be
  excluded; no "no refunds" signs. [ACCC — consumer guarantees](https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/buying-products-and-services/consumer-rights-and-guarantees)
- [ ] **Returns policy** — publish clearly (site prototype includes it): 30-day
  change-of-mind on eligible full-price items + statutory rights unaffected.
- [ ] **Pricing** — display GST-inclusive prices; no fake "was/now" pricing; scarcity/
  stock claims must be true (ACCC actively enforces false-scarcity cases).
- [ ] **Privacy** — collect only needed data; publish a privacy policy (placeholder in
  prototype 👩‍⚖️ before launch). [OAIC — privacy for business](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-guidance-for-organisations-and-government-agencies)
- [ ] **Spam Act 2003** — express consent for email/SMS marketing, identify sender,
  functional unsubscribe. [ACMA — spam](https://www.acma.gov.au/avoid-sending-spam)
- [ ] **Influencer disclosure** — gifted/paid content must be disclosed (AANA code, ACCC
  guidance) — baked into CREATOR_PROGRAM.md.

## 5. Product compliance
- [ ] **Care labelling** — mandatory standard for clothing: permanent, legible care
  instructions (based on AS/NZS 1957). [ACCC Product Safety — care labelling](https://www.productsafety.gov.au/product-safety-laws/safety-standards-bans/mandatory-standards/care-labelling-for-clothing-textile-products)
- [ ] **Fibre content** — accurate composition labelling (consumer law: no misleading
  claims; state e.g. "100% combed cotton" truthfully per mill certs).
- [ ] **Country of origin** — if claiming "Made in Australia" or similar, must meet
  origin rules; imported goods labelled accordingly 👩‍⚖️. [ACCC — country of origin](https://www.accc.gov.au/business/advertising-and-promotions/country-of-origin-claims)
- [ ] **No unsubstantiated claims** — COASTFORM described as construction standard only;
  no performance/antibacterial/UPF claims without test evidence.

## 6. Operations
- [ ] **Supplier contracts** 👩‍⚖️ — written specs (MANUFACTURING_BRIEF.md), payment terms
  (30/70), IP clauses (our artwork/patterns remain ours), defect remedies.
- [ ] **Insurance** — product liability + public liability (events!) + stock cover;
  broker quote before first event/first import 👩‍⚖️.
- [ ] **Shipping** — AusPost MyPost Business / Shippit / Sendle rates comparison;
  publish delivery timeframes honestly.
- [ ] **Terms & privacy pages** 👩‍⚖️ — replace prototype placeholders with
  lawyer-reviewed versions before launch.
