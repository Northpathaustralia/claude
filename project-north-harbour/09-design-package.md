# Design Package — Interiors, Equipment, Landscape & Schedules

**Companion to:** 03 Facility Design Brief · Concept Drawing Set (S/RF/C/A/L sheets) · Visualisation Suite.
**Design language:** *Coastal Brutalism × Warm Minimalism* — off-form concrete and charred timber given warmth by travertine, walnut, brass and abundant planting. The reference set is Lanserhof Tegernsee (clinical-calm wellness), Aman (material restraint), Life Time Athletic (operational scale), Equinox (lighting theatre) and Six Senses (biophilic recovery). The building is designed as a **destination**, not a tenancy.

> **Status — concept design.** This package fixes the design *intent* to a level a client can approve and a QS can price. It is not statutory construction documentation; NCC-compliant working drawings, structural/services engineering and certification are produced by the appointed licensed consultants in the pre-development phase.

## 2. Room-by-room interior design

### Arrival & reception (260m²)
Double-height (7m) volume; **6m solid-walnut reception monolith** with backlit brass lettering and a living green wall behind. Honed travertine floor, a suspended brass-ring feature light (3m dia.), concierge stools in tan leather. Digital: two 55" portrait check-in displays flush-mounted in walnut; a discreet 98" "club pulse" display showing live class/occupancy heat-map. Scent + sound zoned (signature cedar-citrus, 55–60dB curated playlist). Artwork: a commissioned 4m coastal abstract by a Gold Coast artist.

### Café & juice bar (180m² + 40m² terrace)
Licensed, dual-facing (street + club). **Terrazzo-topped bar** with brass foot-rail, walnut-slat ceiling raft, banquette seating in bouclé + tan leather, marble-look bistro tables (60 covers). Specialty espresso (La Marzocco), cold-press juice, protein kitchen. Pendant cluster over the bar; festoon over the terrace. This is the club's "third place" — designed to hold members between sessions and to trade to passers-by.

### Retail (90m²)
Gallery-style, not shop-shelf: backlit walnut display bays, a central brass table for supplement/apparel hero product, recovery-tech vignette (percussion, compression, red-light devices). Integrated with reception sightline for impulse capture.

### Strength & performance halls (700 + 450 + 520 + 180m²)
7m clear-span, blackened-steel exposed ceiling, board-formed concrete perimeter, seamless rubber floor in graphite with zone-coloured platforms. **The 9m glazed "engine-room" window** frames the free-weights floor to Hope Island Road — the building's billboard. Circadian lighting runs cool-bright (4500–5000K) at peak. Chalk-permitted powerlifting annex on an **acoustically isolated floating slab**. Mirror runs are framed in blackened steel; motivational graphic supergraphics in brand-neutral typography.

### Functional / HYROX arena (520m²)
30m sled/prowler track inlaid in the floor, wall-mounted rig spanning 24m, tiered comp-standard ergs. Event-mode lighting (DMX-controllable colour wash) so the hall converts to a spectator arena for HYROX-affiliate events. Scoreboard display wall.

### Group fitness + cycle (180 + 120m²)
Studio: sprung timber-look floor, full-height fluted-glass frontage, tunable colour-wash. Cycle: theatre-raked, immersive front projection wall + surround audio, individual bike telemetry to member app; near-black finish for immersion.

### Recovery centre (520m²) — the signature space
Spa-grade, barefoot-rated micro-cement throughout, in-slab heating, tumbled-stone feature walls, dimmable warm lighting (2700K) with fibre-optic "starlight" ceiling over the plunge court. Contents: **2 communal + 2 private cold plunges**, **magnesium mineral pools** (warm), **2 traditional saunas** (one panoramic to a fern courtyard) + **1 infrared suite**, steam room with chromotherapy, a contrast-therapy deck, a **12-station compression-boot lounge** in leather recliners, and a silent "float" quiet room. Biophilic courtyard visible from the saunas. This is the space a competitor cannot replicate without $30m and two years.

### Pilates (340m²) & yoga (160m²)
Reformer studios A (28 beds) + B (16 beds): warm oak-look floor, timber-slat acoustic ceiling, full-height glazing to the members' terrace, individual bed lighting. Premium reformers (Align-Pilates / BASI-grade). Yoga: hot-capable (radiant + humidity control), preserved-moss acoustic wall, sunrise-programmable lighting.

### Cardio mezzanine (450m²)
Overlooks the strength hall from Level 1 — the club's best sightline. Connected cardio ecosystem (integrated streaming, virtual coaching), stretch/mobility deck with turf and a rig, blackened-steel balustrade with brass handrail.

### Members' lounge, co-working & content studios (220 + 90m²)
Co-working: booth seating, high-speed wifi, phone booths, a boardroom-grade meeting room for corporate members. **Podcast studio** (acoustically treated, 2-3 seat, broadcast lighting) + two content-creation rooms (cyclorama wall, ring + key lighting) — a genuine differentiator for the creator-economy membership and for the operator's own marketing.

### Luxury change & bathrooms (320m²)
Hotel-grade: fluted-oak full-height lockers with app-unlock, stone-topped vanities with individual grooming stations, private WC/shower cubicles (no gang showers), towel service, hairdrying bar with premium tools, amenity dispensers (branded refillables). Underfloor heating. Separate premium "Diamond" change suite.

### Kids club (160m²) & allied health (300m²)
Kids club: licensed ~40, soft-fall zones, sensory play, secure airlock entry from carpark, parent-view glazing, own amenities. Allied health: 8 consult rooms + a rehab gym with a window onto the main floor (the referral flywheel), leased to a physio/sports-medicine group — reception-independent after-hours access.

*(Rolls up into the Phase 6 cost plan's ~$3.2m equipment + tech lines after tender competition and vendor-finance treatment.)*

### Equipment-layout logic
- **Cardio on the mezzanine + perimeter** — best views, drives the "sightline" experience, keeps the loud strength core central.
- **Free weights + platforms in the naturally-lit engine-room** — the hero zone facing the street.
- **Plate-loaded in the middle band** — highest throughput, flanked by selectorised for accessibility/new-member comfort.
- **Coaching sightlines:** the PT/coaching desk sits at the hinge of strength + functional halls with a clear line to platforms and rig.
- **Accessibility:** inclusive selectorised stations, 1200mm circulation aisles, an accessible change/shower suite, and lift access to all mezzanine equipment.
- **Maintenance & future-proofing:** 10% floor slack retained; power/data floor-boxes on a grid so equipment can be re-zoned without builder works; a signed refresh reserve in opex.

## 6. Sustainability & building performance

- **350kW rooftop solar + battery**, targeting a high NABERS-equivalent operational rating; heat-pump pool/space heating (no gas base case).
- **Rainwater harvest** to a 100kL tank for irrigation + WC flushing; WSUD bioretention train.
- **High-air-change, dehumidified HVAC** zoned per activity (the single most under-budgeted system in gyms — carried properly in Phase 6), with heat recovery on pool/steam exhaust.
- **Electrification-ready**, EV-charging, end-of-trip facilities (60 bike spaces) leveraging rail-station adjacency.
- **Circadian lighting + biophilia** as measurable wellbeing features, not just aesthetics — part of the premium-membership value story.
