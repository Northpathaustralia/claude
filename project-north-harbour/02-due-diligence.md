## 2. Planning & zoning

- **Scheme:** Gold Coast City Plan (current version incl. "Our City Our Plan" amendments). Hope Island Central sits in precinct-based controls descending from the Hope Island Local Area Plan (Precinct 4 "Hope Island Central", historically zoned RD1 with mixed-use centre outcomes); the western gateway corridor spans **Mixed Use zone / Centre zone / Low impact industry pockets (Oxenford side)**.
- **Use definition:** a gym is **"Indoor sport and recreation"** under the City Plan. In Mixed Use and Centre zones this is typically **code assessable** — no public notification, no third-party appeal rights — provided height/parking/design codes are met. In residential zones it becomes impact assessable: **avoid residential-zoned lots.**
- **Height:** corridor mapping generally 2–3 storeys (9–15m) `[CONFIRM ☐ City Plan interactive mapping for the chosen lot]`. Our design needs ~12–14m for a 2-level club with mezzanine — inside envelope.
- **Site cover / setbacks:** big-box + parking at 10,000m² implies ~35–45% site cover — comfortably code-compliant.
- **Parking rate:** City Plan Transport Code (9.4.13) rate for indoor sport & recreation `[CONFIRM ☐ exact table rate]`; industry-standard planning assumption **1 space / 20–25m² GFA** → 5,500m² GFA ⇒ **220–275 spaces**. Benchmark parcel fits ~230 at-grade + 60 under-croft. Traffic engineer to negotiate a reduced rate citing 24/7 demand spread + rail station adjacency.
- **Relevant overlays to clear:** Flood (see §3), Coastal erosion (unlikely this far inland), Acid sulfate soils (likely mapped — low-lying estuarine), Airport OLS (Gold Coast/Heliport — non-constraining at this height), Transport noise corridor (Hope Island Rd frontage → AS3671 façade treatment), Biodiversity/waterway buffers on canal-adjacent lots.
- **Infrastructure charges:** City of Gold Coast Charges Resolution (No.1) 2023 — indoor sport & recreation is a charged use per m² GFA plus impervious area stormwater charge. `[ALLOWANCE]` **$1.9m–$2.6m** carried in the cost plan; ☐ obtain a formal charges estimate pre-contract; negotiate offsets for trunk works.

## 3. Flood, stormwater & coastal hazard — **the defining constraint**

- Hope Island is a low-lying estuarine island; historic development was enabled by an engineered navigable canal system draining to Coombabah and Saltwater Creek estuaries (Hope Island LAP).
- Council's **draft Flood overlay update** (interactive mapping) now embeds +0.8m sea-level rise to 2100 and +10% rainfall intensity — expect **designated flood levels and fill requirements to rise** on any current acquisition.
- Design responses priced in Phases 4/6: **finished floor level at DFE + 500mm freeboard** `[ASSUMPTION: ~+1.0–1.5m of engineered fill across building pad, ~15,000–20,000m³ import]`, flood-immune electrical rooms at upper level, at-grade parking accepted as flood-compatible, on-site detention + bio-retention to achieve no-worsening discharge.
- ☐ Site-specific flood study by RPEQ civil engineer against both current and draft overlays (this is the single highest-value DD item — **contract must be conditional on it**).
- **Acid sulfate soils:** presumed present below ~5m AHD. Management plan, lime-treatment allowance for bulk earthworks priced in Phase 6.

## 6. Traffic engineering

- Hope Island Rd is **state-controlled** west of the island: access via new signalised/left-in-left-out arrangement requires **TMR concurrence** — engage TMR pre-lodgement. Prefer a lot with secondary local-street frontage for full-movement access.
- Peak gym generation ~350–450 vtph at 5–7pm `[RPEQ traffic report ☐]`; post-Connector spare capacity on the corridor is favourable but must be modelled with 2036 volumes.
- Parking: 290 bays incl. 8 PWD, 20 EV-charging, 40 staff/overflow; one-way circulating aisle; drop-off for kids club; bus/coach bay; 60 bicycle spaces + end-of-trip (station adjacency).

## 7. Environment, noise, neighbours

- Transport-noise corridor overlay on frontage → glazing/façade acoustic spec (positive: constant road noise masks gym noise).
- 24/7 operation near future residential: locate outdoor training + carpark circulation away from residential boundaries; acoustic fence to any residential interface; music systems with limiters (standard DA conditions).
- Lighting: AS4282 obtrusive-light compliance; signage illumination curfew per code.
- No mapped remnant vegetation expected on a cleared corridor lot ☐; canal-adjacent lots trigger waterway buffer setbacks — prefer non-canal lots.

## 8. Construction methodology & program (summary)

Piled foundations → engineered pad → **structural steel portal frame** (27–36m clear spans over gym floor), precast/tilt concrete to podium and wet areas, Kingspan-type architectural cladding + curtain-wall entry statement. Pools as insitu shells early works.
**Program:** DD + contract 4 months → DA (code assessable) 4–6 months → detailed design/tender 5 months (overlap) → construction 14–16 months → fitout/commissioning 3 months. **Site secure → doors open ≈ 26–30 months** (base), 22 months best case.
