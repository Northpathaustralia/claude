Project North Harbour · Visualisation Suite

The Experience

Concept visualisations of Australia's next flagship wellness & performance precinct — the arrival, the halls, the recovery house and the social heart, rendered in the project's material language of concrete, timber, brass, stone and water.

About these images. These are stylised concept visualisations — composed illustrations that fix the atmosphere, materials, lighting and spatial intent of each space. They are not photographs and not photorealistic CGI. The photorealistic render pack (12 hero images) is produced by an architectural visualisation studio from the concept design, per the Phase 4–5 brief (~$35k); this suite is the art-direction that briefs it. Operator identity is shown as a neutral "North Harbour" placeholder.

The complete visualisation programme (photorealistic) covers 40+ viewpoints across exterior, arrival, every training and wellness zone, recovery, studios, lounge, kids club, outdoor training and Stage-2 expansion — the shot list is scheduled in the Facility Design brief. These stylised concept illustrations establish the art direction. Prepared for Project North Harbour; private and confidential; not for circulation without written permission.
