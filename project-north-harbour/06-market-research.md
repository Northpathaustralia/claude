# Phase 9 — Market Research & Competitive Gap Analysis

**Catchment:** Hope Island 4212 + 10–15 min drive (Helensvale, Oxenford, Coomera/Upper Coomera, Paradise Point/Runaway Bay, Sanctuary Cove). Desk research July 2026; mystery-shop visits and review-mining refresh recommended at DA stage.

**Structural finding:** within 4212 itself there is **no full-service club of any tier** — the island's ~18,600 residents (growing to 20k+) currently drive 10+ minutes to a Westfield or settle for boutiques. Nobody in the entire northern corridor offers big-box training + spa-grade recovery + Pilates + allied health + café under one roof. The nearest true analogues (TotalFusion-style precinct clubs) are in Brisbane and the southern Gold Coast.

## 2. Review mining — recurring complaints across incumbent clubs (Google/Yelp themes)

1. **Peak-hour overcrowding and equipment queues** (all big-box comps) → our answer: 480-concurrent design capacity + occupancy heat-map in app.
2. **Parking friction at Westfield clubs** → 290 dedicated bays at the door.
3. **Dated/tired amenities, cold change rooms** → hotel-grade wet areas, towel service.
4. **"Recovery" = one infrared sauna** → 520m² recovery centre.
5. **Creche hours too short for working parents** → kids club aligned to class-peak windows incl. Saturday mornings.
6. **Boutique members paying twice** (gym + Pilates + recovery at 3 venues ≈ $90–130/wk) → Diamond tier consolidates at $47.95/wk. **This is the single biggest wallet-capture play.**

## 3. What members wish existed (survey themes to validate with 400-person catchment survey at DA stage)
- One membership covering gym + reformer + recovery
- Somewhere to work/meet between sessions (remote-worker corridor)
- Serious lifting environment that doesn't feel hostile to women and older members
- Physio/massage on-site with gym-floor integration
- A café worth staying at (social fitness — the "third place")

## 4. Merse Coomera interaction protocol
- **Product separation:** Merse = standalone luxury bathhouse ritual (2–3hr visits, day-spa price architecture). North Harbour recovery centre = training-integrated recovery (30–60min, membership-bundled). Different occasions, different price metabolism.
- **Funnel design:** Merse guests receive North Harbour trial passes and vice-versa; shared CRM; corporate wellness packages sell both.
- **Cannibalisation cap:** modelled at <8% of recovery-line revenue (recovery is $0.62m of $10.5m — bounded exposure).

## 5. Gap analysis verdict

| Demand segment | Currently served by | Gap |
|---|---|---|
| Affluent 45–75 wellness (largest 4212 cohort) | Sanctuary Cove CC (partially) | **WIDE OPEN** |
| Serious strength 18–45 (corridor) | World Gym Coomera | Partially served — win on amenity + recovery |
| Reformer Pilates (booming, all ages) | 2 capacity-constrained boutiques | **WIDE OPEN at scale** |
| Families (kids club + parents train) | Goodlife x2 | Win on hours, quality, parking |
| Corporate/remote workers | Nobody | **UNCONTESTED** |
| Recovery/longevity consumers | Merse (from Oct 2026, Coomera only) | Own it on-island first |

**Conclusion:** the northern Gold Coast has volume competitors and one resort club, but **no integrated premium flagship**. First mover on the gateway corridor takes a defensible position that in-centre incumbents structurally cannot copy (they cannot add land, parking, pools or 24/7 independence inside a Westfield).
