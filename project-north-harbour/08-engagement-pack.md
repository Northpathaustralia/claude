# Engagement Pack — Approach, Protection & Execution

This document closes the gap between "a great package" and "a deal in motion": how to approach the operator safely, protect the idea, procure the consultants, and run the data room.

## 1. Draft cover letter — operator approach

> **Private & Confidential — for the attention of the CEO / Head of Property, Fitness Cartel Australia**
>
> Dear [Name],
>
> I am writing to introduce **Project North Harbour** — a confidential, fully-researched development opportunity for a flagship health club and wellness precinct at the Hope Island western gateway on the northern Gold Coast.
>
> In brief: the wealthiest fast-growing catchment on the northern Gold Coast (median household income $1,934/week, +26.7% population since 2021) has no full-service club of any tier, and two pieces of state infrastructure — the Coomera Connector and the new Hope Island rail station — switched on at its gateway within the last seven months. We have completed a weighted multi-site analysis, pre-feasibility due diligence, concept design for a 6,200m² integrated club/recovery/Pilates/allied-health precinct, a Class 5 cost plan (~$47m TDC) and a 10-year financial model showing ~$4m mature EBITDAR under an OpCo/PropCo structure that would require less than $5m of operator capital.
>
> We believe this is the natural Gold Coast statement asset in your journey to 60 clubs by 2030, and it is deliberately structured so the operator's capital commitment is limited to fitout and equipment against a long triple-net lease.
>
> I would welcome a 45-minute meeting to walk your team through the investment memorandum. The full package is available under NDA.
>
> Yours sincerely,
> **James Forycki** · Project North Harbour

**Sequencing note:** consider securing the site option *before* the operator meeting. Whoever controls the land controls the negotiation; presenting with an executed option converts this from "an idea they could pursue themselves" into "a deal they can only do with you."

## 2. Confidentiality & IP protection protocol

| # | Measure | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | NDA before disclosure | Mutual NDA (2-year term, non-circumvention clause) signed before the memorandum or site identity is shared with any operator, agent, or capital party. Non-circumvention is the critical clause — it stops a recipient going direct to the landowner. |
| 2 | Codename discipline | "Project North Harbour" in all documents; the specific corridor/lot is disclosed only after NDA execution. |
| 3 | Numbered copies | Watermark each issued PDF with recipient name + date; keep an issue register. |
| 4 | Trademark discipline | No operator name in any public document, domain, business name, ASIC registration or social media. Operator brands appear only inside confidential evaluation documents as prospective-partner references. |
| 5 | Site control first | The enforceable moat is the option contract, not the idea. Prioritise it. |
| 6 | Advisor engagement letters | Ensure consultant IP in surveys/designs is assigned to the project entity. |

## 3. Project entity & structure checklist

- [ ] Incorporate SPV (e.g. "North Harbour Developments Pty Ltd") + discretionary trust structure per accountant's advice — before signing the option.
- [ ] Pre-development budget facility ($350k) committed.
- [ ] Buyer's agent engaged for off-market corridor sweep (fee ~1–1.5%).
- [ ] Solicitor (property + construction) engaged; option contract template ready.
- [ ] Accountant/tax advisor: GST, margin scheme, Div 43/40 depreciation strategy.

## 5. Data room index (for operator & capital parties, post-NDA)

1. Investment memorandum + deck (this package)
2. Site: title searches, option contract, survey, planning certificate
3. Technical: flood study, geotech, traffic, services advice
4. Design: architect concept set, area schedule, staging plan
5. Financial: QS cost plan, 10-year model (native spreadsheet), sensitivity workbook
6. Market: catchment report, competitor audit, survey results
7. Legal: SPV structure chart, NDA register, consultant engagement letters
8. Correspondence: council pre-lodgement minutes, TMR position, Energex/utility advice
