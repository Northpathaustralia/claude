## Learning outcomes (what she can DO after)
1. Follow a named 5-step clean method from memory.
2. Apply eyeshadow without fallout ruining her under-eyes.
3. Set up her few essential tools/products (no over-buying).
4. Fix the 5 most common mistakes.
5. Get a result that holds up in person, on camera, and in AU heat.

## The "Clean Method" (the spine — 5 steps)
**P-B-C-A-S:** **P**rep → **B**ase the lid → **C**ontrol (catch fallout) → **A**pply shadow → **S**et & finish. Every lesson maps to a step; every downloadable reinforces it.
