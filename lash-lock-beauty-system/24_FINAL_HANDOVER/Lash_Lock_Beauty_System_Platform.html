1 · Platform at a glance

A working Gold Coast makeup artist teaches a clean, repeatable eye-makeup method — the un-glamorous professional steps that make everyday makeup look good in person, on camera, and in real Australian heat — paired with the Lash Lock tool and one-page checklists.

The white space

Own "the clean method"

AU makeup education is dominated by $2,500–$5,000 career certificates; social tutorials are free but unstructured. The $19–79 focused micro-class tier is wide open. Kat owns method + honesty + Australian conditions — a moat US tool brands and big schools can't copy.

The engine

Tool ↔ class reinforce

The class is the honest Lash Lock demo; the tool makes the method effortless. Value-to-product ratio held at 4:1 so it never feels like an ad channel. Real-footage-first; AI for B-roll only.
