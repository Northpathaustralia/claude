- **Sun (2–3h):** edit the week's 7 assets from batch footage; write captions; make covers; schedule.

- **Fri (30 min):** log metrics in the dashboard; mark keep/iterate/retire; pick next week's A/B.
- Stories most days: polls ("hooded or not?"), behind-the-scenes, comment call-outs, countdown to launch (Wk4).
- ⚠️ items publish only after product tests/compliance clear; if not ready, swap in a non-product edu asset.
