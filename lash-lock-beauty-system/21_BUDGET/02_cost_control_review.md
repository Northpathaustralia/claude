## Lowest-cost credible workflow
Phone + real footage + window light + natural voice + free editing + hosted course/checkout + free-tier email + organic-first + manual bundle fulfilment at low volume + a handful of AI B-roll clips. **Pre-first-sale cash outlay ≈ $55–170.**

## Caps & rules
- **AI-generation cap:** ~{$30}/mo to start; only hero product/lifestyle/transition B-roll; max {3} regenerations/shot then fall back to real.
- **Paid-ad test cap:** start {$10–30}/day; kill a variant that can't beat CPA < contribution after a fair test window.
- **Stop-loss:** if 7-day paid CPA > contribution margin with no improving trend → pause paid, return to organic.
- **Upgrade triggers:** LED (daylight failing), email paid tier (>1k list), Higgsfield Plus (weekly AI B-roll), VA (support > owner capacity), ads (2 organic winners + known margin).
