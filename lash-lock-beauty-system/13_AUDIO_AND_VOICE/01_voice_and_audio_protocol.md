# 13 · Voice & Audio Protocol

**Default: Kat's real recorded voice.** Generated voice/lip-sync only where an on-camera generated performance is unavoidable, and only after passing the checks below.

## The safe premium workflow
1. Write the final script (short spoken lines).
2. Kat records on her phone in a quiet, soft-furnished room (bed/wardrobe = natural sound treatment).
3. Record **three takes** of every line: **Calm Expert**, **Friendly Conversational**, **Higher-Energy Promotional**.
4. Select the most believable take (believable > perfect).
5. Light noise clean only — **keep breaths and humanity.**
6. Use the real voice as VO over B-roll.
7. Use lip-sync only when a generated on-camera performance is truly needed.
8. Compare generated mouth movement **frame by frame**.
9. Replace any questionable speaking shot with real footage or VO-over-B-roll.

## Kat voice guide
- **Accent:** natural Australian female — relaxed, not broad-ocker, not put-on.
- **Register:** warm, calm-confident, a pro friend at the mirror.
- **Pace:** unhurried; let key words land; natural micro-pauses.
- **Energy by content type:** Educational = Calm Expert. Story/BTS = Friendly Conversational. Ads/launch = Higher-Energy (still believable, never American-infomercial).

## Energy scale (1–5)
1. Intimate/reassuring (mature-skin, bridal nerves) · 2. Calm teaching (core lessons) · 3. Friendly upbeat (most reels) · 4. Bright/promotional (ads, launch) · 5. High excitement (rare; reveals only). **Default to 2–3.**

## Delivery notes (say-it-like-this)
- Hooks: drop the preamble; first word is the problem/payoff.
- Emphasise the *verb of the step* ("**catch** the fallout", "**set** before you smile").
- Smile on the friendly lines — it's audible.
- Trail off naturally on the honest note ("...it won't fix everything, but—").

## Voice rules (hard no)
No robotic TTS · no generic American sales voice · no fake emotional performance · no unnatural speed · no excessive vocal fry · no mispronounced AU terms · no voice that doesn't match the brand · **no cloned voice without clear consent.**

## Lip-sync rejection checklist (reject if ANY true)
☐ mouth drifts from audio · ☐ frozen/glassy eyes · ☐ unnatural teeth/tongue · ☐ inconsistent accent · ☐ robotic cadence · ☐ no natural blink/breath. → replace with real footage.

## Audio quality checklist (every asset)
☐ Voice clearly above music (music ducked) · ☐ no clipping/peaking · ☐ consistent loudness across cuts · ☐ no distracting room echo/hum · ☐ real product sounds preserved · ☐ deliberate silence before reveals · ☐ exported clean-master audio kept separately.
