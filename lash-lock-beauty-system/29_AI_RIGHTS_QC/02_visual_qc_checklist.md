# 29 · AI Visual & Audio QC Checklist

Run on **every** AI-generated clip before it's used. Any ✗ = regenerate / simplify / replace with real footage. Reviewer signs each.

## Anatomy & realism
☐ Hands correct (5 fingers, no fusion/extra) ☐ Realistic finger joints ☐ Eyes natural (no warped pupils, no dead stare) ☐ Eyelashes plausible (not impossible) ☐ Teeth natural/symmetrical ☐ Lips natural ☐ Hair natural movement (no melting) ☐ Skin texture real (not plastic/over-smoothed)

## Continuity & identity
☐ Kat's identity stable across shots ☐ Jewellery unchanged ☐ Nails unchanged ☐ Wardrobe consistent ☐ Makeup continuity holds

## Product
☐ Product shape matches real reference ☐ Colour correct ☐ Logo stable + correct placement (no drift/duplication) ☐ Packaging accurate ☐ Correct scale/proportion ☐ Not floating; obeys gravity

## Scene physics
☐ Reflections plausible ☐ Shadows consistent with light ☐ No background morphing ☐ No object duplication ☐ No flicker/strobing ☐ No warping between frames

## Text & artefacts
☐ No accidental subtitles/gibberish text ☐ No watermark ☐ No stray artefacts

## Audio (if present)
☐ Lip sync matches every syllable (no drift) ☐ Accent consistent + Australian where required ☐ Voice natural (not robotic) ☐ Natural breaths/pauses ☐ No audio drift vs picture ☐ Music ducked under any dialogue

## Compliance (from `01_...`)
☐ Consent/rights on file for any likeness/voice/music ☐ Not presenting AI as physical proof ☐ Not implying a real customer ☐ AI disclosed where required ☐ Only ✅-register claims spoken/shown

## Decision
PASS → use · REGENERATE (note fix) · SIMPLIFY (split shot / remove dialogue / voice-over) · **REPLACE WITH REAL FOOTAGE** (default for any interaction/proof shot). Log: asset ID · reviewer · date · decision.
