# 29 · AI Video — Rights, Consent & Compliance Rules

Governs all Higgsfield/AI production. Binding rules — an asset that breaks any of these is rejected.

## Rights & consent
| Asset | Rule |
|---|---|
| Kat's likeness | Used only with her signed consent (`28_...03`). Reference images she approves only. |
| Kat's voice (clone/transform) | Only with explicit signed consent. Prefer her real recorded voice. |
| Customer images/video | Never used without a signed photo/video release. |
| Music | Licensed / platform library / royalty-free with commercial rights on file. No ripped tracks. |
| Stock assets | Commercial licence retained. |
| Third-party people/brands | Not depicted without rights. No competitor branding. |
| Uploaded references | Treat as confidential; don't upload others' PII or private supplier docs to AI tools. |

## Truth-in-advertising rules (hard prohibitions)
- ❌ AI-generated **product performance** presented as physical proof (fallout capture, longevity, etc.). Proof = **real footage only** (Gate A).
- ❌ AI-generated **people implied to be real customers**.
- ❌ **Synthetic testimonials** of any kind.
- ❌ **Fake before/after** or restaged/relit results.
- ✅ AI allowed for: non-proof B-roll, product beauty shots (geometry locked to a real reference), lifestyle ambience, transitions — clearly not standing in for evidence.

## Disclosure
- Disclose AI-generated imagery of people/scenes per platform + AU expectations ("includes AI-generated visuals").
- Never let AI imply live footage of Kat without disclosure.

## Product accuracy
- Product geometry, colour, logo must match the **verified real reference**. Any drift = reject/regenerate or shoot real.
- No product claims in AI shots beyond ✅ register items.

## Data retention (AI tools)
- Delete Kat's trained models/reference uploads when the project ends or on her request.
- Keep a record of what was uploaded and its consent basis.

## Rejection triggers → see `02_visual_qc_checklist.md`
Any realism failure, geometry drift, uncanny performance, or missing consent/rights = do not publish; regenerate, simplify, or replace with real footage.
