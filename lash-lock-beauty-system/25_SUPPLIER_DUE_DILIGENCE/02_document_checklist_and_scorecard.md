## Response scorecard (decision)
For each material item, rate: **A** adequate · **C** conditional · **Q** needs clarification · **T** independent testing required · **R** reject.

| Item | Rating | Reason | Action |

**Overall decision rule:**
- All safety/compliance/IP/insurance items = **A** → **Accept supplier.**
- Minor gaps only → **Accept with conditions** (document conditions + deadline).
- Safety/material unclear → **Independent testing required** before any claim/shipment.
- Missing insurance or IP warranty, or refusal on safety → **Reject / find alternative** (do not ship).

## Fallback sourcing plan (if current supplier can't evidence)
1. **Independent test:** send samples to an accredited AU/NZ testing lab for material/skin-contact/restricted-substance screening. Budget + timeline as a launch-gate task.
2. **Alternative suppliers:** shortlist 2–3 makers of reusable silicone eye tools who *can* provide SDS + compliance + insurance (compare cost, MOQ, lead time, evidence quality).
3. **Interim:** continue selling the **class** (not the tool); market the tool only once evidenced.
4. **Never:** ship an unverified skin-contact product or state material/safety claims without evidence.

**Confidentiality:** store all supplier documents in a secure, access-controlled drive — never commit them to git.
