## Formal email
> **Subject: Product compliance & specification request — Lash Lock ({your business name})**
>
> Hi {supplier contact},
>
> We're preparing to sell your {product/SKU} in Australia under our brand "Lash Lock" and need to complete standard supplier due diligence before we can market or ship it. Could you please provide the following for this product:
>
> 1. Full material composition (all components, incl. any coatings/colourants).
> 2. Safety Data Sheet (SDS) where applicable, and any skin-contact suitability statements.
> 3. Confirmation of restricted-substance compliance (e.g. free of restricted phthalates/heavy metals) and any certificates.
> 4. Manufacturing location and your quality-control process.
> 5. Product dimensions and tolerances, and batch/lot identification method.
> 6. Recommended cleaning method, storage, and expected reuse lifespan.
> 7. Packaging composition.
> 8. Confirmation you hold the IP rights to the design/logo, or that our branding won't infringe third-party rights.
> 9. Product-liability insurance confirmation (insurer + coverage).
> 10. Your defect, recall and change-notification process.
>
> We treat these as confidential. Happy to sign a mutual NDA if preferred. Could you send what's available within {10 business days}? If any item isn't available, please say so directly so we can arrange independent testing.
>
> Thanks,
> {Name}, {Business}, {phone}
