## B. Material & safety facts to confirm (get from supplier + physical check)
- [ ] Exact material (e.g. medical-grade silicone? food-grade? verify supplier spec sheet)
- [ ] Dimensions (mm) — front / back / thickness
- [ ] Weight
- [ ] Colour(s) — with a colour reference photo under neutral light
- [ ] Logo — exact placement, colour, whether embossed/printed
- [ ] Cleaning method the supplier certifies (soap & water? boil? no?)
- [ ] Reuse lifespan the supplier will stand behind
- [ ] Any allergen info from supplier (do NOT claim "hypoallergenic" without a certificate)
- [ ] Country of manufacture
- [ ] Whether it's latex-free (only claim if certified)

## C. CLAIMS BANNED until evidence exists (per §1)
❌ Medical / therapeutic (e.g. "de-puffs", "reduces wrinkles", "soothes") — unless a specific, evidenced, compliant claim
❌ "Guaranteed" results · ❌ "Waterproof" · ❌ "Hypoallergenic" · ❌ "Allergy-free" · ❌ "Non-toxic/food-grade" (unless certified) · ❌ Sustainability/eco claims (unless certified) · ❌ Any performance %/stat

## D. Safe-to-say-now language (use until verification completes)
- "A reusable tool I use to help keep eye makeup **controlled**."
- "I use it to help **catch fallout** so it doesn't land on finished under-eye makeup." *(only publish once test #1–2 pass)*
- "Here's **how I use it** — watch what happens." *(let the uncut demo do the claiming)*
- Frame as *"the tool + the method"* — the method carries the promise, the tool is shown, not over-claimed.

## E. Australian advertising-compliance note (not legal advice)
AU consumer law (ACL) prohibits misleading/deceptive conduct and unsubstantiated claims. Therapeutic-type claims can bring a cosmetic under TGA scrutiny. **Route the final claim list past a qualified AU adviser before launch** (→ `23_OWNER_VERIFICATION`). Keep evidence files for every claim you publish.
