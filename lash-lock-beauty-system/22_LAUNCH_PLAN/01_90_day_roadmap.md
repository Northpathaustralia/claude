# 22 · 90-Day Growth Roadmap

Sequenced so nothing depends on unverified claims until they're cleared. The step-by-step "dumb-down" execution guide is in `02_action_plan.md` (Batch 08).

## Days 1–30 — Verify, build, warm up
- **Wk1:** Complete Lash Lock testing + get supplier spec (Owner Verification #1–2). Book AU-compliance review (#3). Lock final promise/claims.
- **Wk1–2:** Batch shoot (course + 30+ organic assets). Set up host, checkout, email, lead magnet.
- **Wk2–4:** Post daily (calendar Wk1–3). Grow list via free Method Card. Establish metric baselines. Build & QC the mini-course + downloadables.
- **Gate to launch:** claims verified ✅ + compliance ✅ + course tested end-to-end + a small list.

## Days 22–37 — Soft then public launch
- **Soft launch (Day ~22):** open to email list + engaged followers; gather first real testimonials (consented); fix funnel friction; confirm delivery/checkout works.
- **Public launch (7-day, ~Day 30):** run launch calendar Wk4 + email sequence D. Any deadline must be real. Watch CPA, add-to-cart, refunds.

## Days 38–60 — Optimise & introduce paid
- Double down on winning hooks/covers (dashboard). Retire losers.
- Launch small paid tests (AD1/AD2 cold → AD5/AD4 retarget), one variable at a time; scale only when CPA < contribution.
- Ship the **Fallout-Proof bundle ($69)** once product/claims verified; add order bump + upsell live.
- Publish honest-failure & AU-heat proof to deepen trust.

## Days 61–90 — Expand the ladder & systemise
- Add the **Even-Eyeliner add-on** and begin the **flagship ($147)** build from best-performing lessons.
- Repurpose top reels into carousels/Pinterest/YT; start a light weekly email/newsletter.
- Consider consented **student results** as social proof engine.
- Decide on international-English expansion *only if* AU metrics are healthy.
- Re-run red-team + council (Batch 08) on real data; update strategy.

## Standing weekly rhythm (all 90 days)
Batch-edit Sun · post daily + reply first hour · Fri metrics review + keep/iterate/retire · monthly re-baseline & scorecard. Protect against Kat-burnout: 1 batch shoot covers weeks; never trade honesty/quality for volume.

## KPIs (set your own targets from baselines — no invented numbers)
Reach & saves (top-funnel health) · list growth · LP click-rate · class conversion · bundle attach-rate · CPA vs contribution · refund rate · repeat/upsell rate.
