# 02 · Brand Positioning (foundational)

## Positioning statement
**For** time-poor Australian women (and beginner/aspiring makeup lovers) who are tired of messy, complicated, doesn't-work-on-me makeup,
**Kat** is the Gold Coast working makeup artist who teaches a **clean, repeatable eye-makeup method** — the un-glamorous professional steps that make everyday makeup look good in person, on camera, and in real Australian heat —
**unlike** expensive career certificates or endless free trend-chasing tutorials,
**because** she pairs a named method with the exact tool (Lash Lock) and one-page checklists that make it repeatable at home.

## Brand purpose
Help ordinary women feel *put-together and confident* with makeup that actually behaves — without the mess, the overwhelm, or a $3,000 course.

## Audience promise
"In under an hour, you'll learn a clean eye-makeup method you can repeat every day — no fallout ruining your under-eyes, no confusing pro techniques, no wasted products."

## Value proposition (one line)
**Professional results, minus the mess and the mystery.**

## The one thing to be known for (white space)
The **"clean method"** — controlled, no-mess, no-fallout eye makeup that holds up *in Australian conditions*. Not glam. Not trends. **Method + honesty + AU climate credibility.**

## Positioning guardrails (never drift from these)
1. **Method over looks.** Always teach a repeatable *process*, not a one-off *look*.
2. **Honest over hype.** Under-claim the tool, over-deliver the proof — including honest "where it doesn't help" content.
3. **Specific over general.** Name the eye/skin/scenario (hooded, mature, bridal, heat).
4. **Tool serves the teaching.** Lash Lock appears because the *method* uses it — not as an ad bolt-on.
5. **Australian.** AUD, AU spelling, AU climate, AU lifestyle. This is a moat US brands can't copy.

## What Kat credibly does better (evidence-based)
- **Professional experience** — a real working MUA, not a faceless page.
- **Demonstration quality** — split-face, real-time, close-up proof.
- **Teaching clarity** — one named finite method + downloadable tools.
- **Product integration** — tool and class reinforce each other.
- **Gold Coast / AU conditions** — heat & humidity longevity nobody else owns.
- **Human trust** — honest failure testing, real face, real hands.
