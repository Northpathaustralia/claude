# 14 · Editing, Caption & Thumbnail Standards

Repeatable template (CapCut/Canva). Brand palette: oat/cream base, espresso text, clay accent.

## Editing template
**Opening (0–2s):** immediate visual tension (problem/result/tension); headline visible fast; **no logo animation before value; no long fade; no greeting.** First frame reads sound-off.
**Main:** cut dead air; change framing/crop/info when attention would dip; close-ups for technique; text clarifies (doesn't echo every word); product natural; pattern-interrupts only if they aid understanding.
**Ending:** show the result; **one** clear action (not three competing CTAs); hold the last useful frame long enough to read.

## Rhythm rules
Quick, not frantic. Cut on action. Hold on the demo when the technique must be seen. **Never cut during the catch reveal / split-face reveal** — continuity = trust. Slight speed-up of boring bits is fine; don't fake continuity on proof shots.

## Caption (on-screen text) standards
- Sans, heavy weight, high contrast (espresso on light card, or white with subtle shadow).
- Correct AU spelling; proofread.
- **Never over the eye/technique area**; inside platform-safe margins (clear of top/bottom UI).
- Emphasise key words selectively (clay highlight), don't full-caption every word.
- No tiny disclaimers; any required disclaimer legible.
- Keep a separate editable caption file (.srt/.txt) per asset.

## Sound
Voice clearly above music; music ducked; keep real product sounds; deliberate silence before reveals. Trending audio only for tested top-funnel reach pieces (separate from brand-voice pieces).

## Master exports (per asset)
Clean master (no captions) · IG Reels · TikTok · FB Reels · YT Shorts · paid-ad cut · silent-viewing cut · thumbnail/cover frame. No platform watermark. Naming: `LLBS_[pillar]_[assetID]_[platform]_[v].mp4`.

## Thumbnail / cover strategy
**Goal:** legible at a glance on a grid; promises one clear outcome.
- One high-contrast phrase (2–4 words): "Watch the catch", "SET before shadow", "Clean vs messy", "Under 10 min".
- Face or macro proof, not a logo.
- Clay accent bar behind the phrase; espresso or white text; brand serif for cover phrase.
- Consistent lower-third placement so the grid looks like a *series*.
- Series covers (e.g. "Clean or Messy?") share a template so the profile grid reads as a brand.
- Test 2 covers per hero asset (see testing dashboard); keep the winner's style.
- Avoid: clutter, tiny text, three messages, gold glitter, mismatched fonts.
