# 02 · Customer Language & Pain-Point Map

**Purpose:** Give Kat the *exact words* real customers use, so hooks, ads and lesson titles sound like the customer's own head — not marketing. **Honesty:** phrasing below is modelled on authentic beauty-forum/comment language patterns and search phrasing; it contains **no invented quotes attributed to real people**. Owner may enrich it by copying real (paraphrased) phrases from her own comments/DMs (→ `23_OWNER_VERIFICATION`).

---

## How to read this map
For each problem we separate six layers (brief §4D):
- **Surface problem** — what she says out loud.
- **Emotional problem** — what it really costs her.
- **Practical consequence** — what goes wrong today.
- **Desired outcome** — what "solved" looks like.
- **Buying objection** — why she hesitates.
- **Proof required** — what would make her believe.

---

### 1. Eyeshadow fallout ruining under-eye makeup
- **Surface:** "Eyeshadow keeps falling under my eyes." · "My concealer looks dirty after eyeshadow." · "Fallout everywhere."
- **Emotional:** Frustration + feeling amateur — "I did everything and it still looks messy."
- **Practical:** She wipes fallout across finished concealer → smears it → has to redo → runs late.
- **Desired:** "Do eyes first without wrecking the rest," or "catch the fallout so I don't have to redo."
- **Objection:** "Can't I just do eyes first? Why buy a tool?"
- **Proof:** Split-face — one side catches fallout, one side doesn't — real time, no cuts.

### 2. Smudged / transferred mascara
- **Surface:** "Mascara ends up on my lid/brow bone." · "Raccoon eyes by lunch."
- **Emotional:** Self-conscious, checks mirror all day.
- **Practical:** Prints mascara on the lid while applying; smudges under eye through the day.
- **Desired:** "Clean lashes without the print." · "Lasts through the day."
- **Objection:** "Isn't that just the mascara I use?"
- **Proof:** Close-up of the guard between lash and lid; before/after at end of day (AU heat).

### 3. Uneven / wobbly eyeliner
- **Surface:** "My wings never match." · "One eye is always bigger."
- **Emotional:** Defeat — "I've watched 100 tutorials and still can't."
- **Practical:** Redraws, thickens to fix, runs out of time, gives up on liner.
- **Desired:** "A repeatable way to get even lines without a steady-hand gift."
- **Objection:** "Guides look gimmicky / like a hack that won't actually work on me."
- **Proof:** Same tool, both eyes, matched result; show it working on a *hooded* eye.

### 4. Makeup takes too long
- **Surface:** "I don't have 45 minutes." · "By the time I'm done I'm exhausted."
- **Emotional:** Guilt + overwhelm; feels makeup isn't "for" busy women.
- **Practical:** Skips makeup or does a rushed version she doesn't like.
- **Desired:** "A fast routine that still looks done."
- **Objection:** "Will a class actually save time or just add steps?"
- **Proof:** Timed real-time demo — full clean eye in X minutes.

### 5. Looks different / worse on camera
- **Surface:** "Looks fine in the mirror, terrible in photos/video." · "Flashback." · "Looks patchy on camera."
- **Emotional:** Embarrassment at events, weddings, video calls.
- **Practical:** Photos she won't post; bad wedding photos.
- **Desired:** "Makeup that holds up in person *and* on camera."
- **Objection:** "Is this just about better products?"
- **Proof:** Same face, mirror vs phone camera, side by side.

### 6. Mature / hooded under-eyes
- **Surface:** "Concealer creases into my lines." · "Everything settles into wrinkles." · "Cakey under-eyes."
- **Emotional:** Feels tutorials are all for 20-year-olds; feels unseen.
- **Practical:** Heavy product creases within an hour.
- **Desired:** "Under-eyes that look smooth and *not* cakey, made for my skin."
- **Objection:** "Young creators don't get my skin."
- **Proof:** Demo on mature/hooded model; honest "less is more" method.

### 7. Bridal longevity
- **Surface:** "Needs to last all day and through happy tears." · "Photographs for hours."
- **Emotional:** High stakes — one shot, permanent photos.
- **Practical:** Fear it smudges/melts; fear of DIY for own wedding.
- **Desired:** "Bulletproof, photo-ready, all-day."
- **Objection:** "This is too important to DIY / trust a gadget."
- **Proof:** Real bride footage (with consent), end-of-night check, AU-heat context.

### 8. Fear of "doing it wrong" / product overwhelm
- **Surface:** "I don't even know what order." · "Too many products, no idea what I need."
- **Emotional:** Intimidated; feels stupid; avoids trying.
- **Practical:** Buys products she never uses; gives up.
- **Desired:** "Just tell me the steps and the few things I actually need."
- **Objection:** "It's probably all free on YouTube anyway."
- **Proof:** A short, named, finite method + a one-page tool list.

## The three objections that gate every sale
1. **"It's all free online."** → Counter with *structure + outcome + a repeatable system + support*, not more free tips. Lead magnet must feel like a finished tool, not a teaser.
2. **"The tool is a gimmick."** → Counter with *honest, uncut, split-face proof* — including one video showing where it *doesn't* help. Honesty is the differentiator.
3. **"It won't work on MY eyes/skin/age."** → Counter with *specificity*: dedicated hooded/mature/oily demonstrations named in titles.
