## Teleprompter script format
Short lines, one idea per card, / marks for breath, **bold** = emphasis. Derived from `07_LESSON_SCRIPTS` — keep spoken, not written-essay.

## Model/talent release
Use `28_PRIVACY_DATA/03` (photo/video + test-participant). No footage of a person used without a signed release on file.

## Trackers (sheets)
**Asset tracker:** `ID | title | type | Real/AI | status(script/shot/edit/QC/scheduled/live) | claims-checked | owner | due`.
**Editing tracker:** `ID | rough | captions | cover | exports(IG/TT/YT/ad/silent) | QC pass | scheduled`.
**Content-approval checklist (per asset before publish):** ☐ claims ✅ only ☐ consent/rights ☐ AI QC pass (if AI) ☐ captions correct ☐ CTA single ☐ mobile-safe text ☐ disclosure if needed.
