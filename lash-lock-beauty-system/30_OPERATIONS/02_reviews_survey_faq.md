# 30 · Review-Request System, Feedback Survey & Master FAQ

## Review-request system
- **Trigger:** P6 email (Day 8) + in-course completion prompt.
- **Ask:** "One line on what changed for you — and a photo if you're happy (optional)." Link to a short form.
- **Consent:** every published review needs a signed testimonial release (`28_...03`). No release = don't publish.
- **Use:** only real, honest, released reviews (`26_...03`). Store on secure drive.
- **Cadence:** monthly, feature 1–2 (with permission).

## Customer feedback survey (post-completion, ~5 Qs)
1. Did the class help you get cleaner eye makeup? (Yes / Somewhat / No)
2. What was the single most useful step?
3. What was confusing or missing?
4. How long until you felt in control? (1st try / 2–3 / more)
5. Would you recommend it? (0–10) + why.
→ Feeds: creative angles, lesson fixes, claim honesty check (if many say "didn't help," soften the promise).
