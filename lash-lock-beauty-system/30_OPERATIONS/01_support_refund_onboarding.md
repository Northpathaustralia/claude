# 30 · Customer Support, Refund Workflow & Onboarding

## Customer onboarding (post-purchase)
1. Payment confirmed (server-side webhook) → access auto-granted.
2. **P1 + P2 emails** fire (`16_EMAIL_FUNNEL`): receipt + "start here".
3. Course landing: Lesson 0 + Method Card download visible first.
4. Bundle buyers: shipping confirmation + care guide (⚠️ once verified) + tracking.
5. Day 1/3/6 nurture nudges.

## Refund workflow
1. Request in → verify order + date within policy window ({14} days).
2. Digital: approve satisfaction refund unless clear abuse; process in payment platform; revoke access; send confirmation macro.
3. Physical (bundle): hygiene item — faulty/not-as-described covered by ACL; otherwise per policy. Arrange return only if required.
4. Log every refund (reason) → feeds product/creative improvement.
5. Never argue statutory rights; escalate edge cases to owner.

## Support macros (edit tone to brand — warm, plain)
- **Access issue:** "So sorry! Here's your access link again: {link}. If it still won't open, tell me your device/browser and I'll sort it today. — Kat's team"
- **Refund request:** "No problem at all — I've refunded your class ({order}). It'll show in {3–5} business days. If anything about it didn't land, I'd genuinely love one line of feedback. 🤍"
- **"Does it work on my eyes?":** "Yes — the method flexes for hooded/mature/oily eyes; Lessons 3 & 5 cover the tweaks. Tell me your eye type and I'll point you to the exact bit."
- **Shipping delay:** "Thanks for your patience — your Lash Lock is on its way, tracking: {link}. Any issues when it arrives, reply here."
- **Product question (pre-verification):** stick to safe interim wording (`04_...04`); never claim material/safety not yet verified.
- **Reaction/irritation report:** "I'm really sorry — please stop using it and {advice}. Can you tell me what happened? We take this seriously." → log as incident (`30_...03`), consider refund, review claim.

## SLA
Reply within 1 business day; refunds within policy; log everything. Templates live in the helpdesk, not just here.
