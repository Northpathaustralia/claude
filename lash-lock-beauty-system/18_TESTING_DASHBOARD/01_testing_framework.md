# 18 · Test-and-Learn Framework

**Principle:** test one variable at a time; establish *your own* baselines (no invented universal benchmarks). Decide with data, not vibes.

## Variables to test (one at a time)
Hook · first frame · presenter vs voice-over · problem-led vs result-led · short vs medium length · product shown early vs late · demo vs testimonial · price framing · CTA · thumbnail/cover · caption opening.

## Metrics to track
3-sec hold · avg watch time · completion % · rewatches · saves · shares · comments · profile visits · link/LP clicks · add-to-cart · purchase conversion · cost per LP view (paid) · CPA · refund rate · support questions.

## Establish baselines (first 2–3 weeks)
Post consistently, log everything, then compute *your* median 3-sec hold, completion, save-rate, LP click-rate. Those medians become your bar. "Good" = beats your own median; re-baseline monthly.

## Spreadsheet schema (one row per asset)
`Asset | Date | Platform | Pillar | Hook# | Creative type | Reach | Views | 3s hold% | Avg watch | Completion% | Saves | Shares | Comments | Profile visits | LP clicks | Add-to-cart | Sales | Spend | Revenue | CPA | Notes | Next action`

Ad-test tab adds: `Variant | Variable tested | Impressions | CTR | CPC | CPM | LP view cost | ROAS`.

## Decision rules
| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| Low 3-sec hold, rest fine | **Change hook / first frame**, keep body |
| Good hold, low completion | **Recut middle** (tighten, add reframes) |
| High saves/shares, low clicks | **Change CTA / add link sticker**, keep creative |
| High clicks, low add-to-cart | **Fix landing page**, not the ad |
| High add-to-cart, low purchase | **Fix checkout / price framing / trust** |
| High purchase, high refund | **Fix expectation/claim in creative**; check product |
| Beats baseline on all | **Keep; make 2–3 variations; scale carefully** (paid) |
| Below baseline 2+ tries | **Retire**; harvest any good B-roll |

## Cadence
Weekly review (Fri): tag each asset Keep / Iterate / Recut / Change-hook / Change-offer / Retire / Scale. Monthly: re-baseline; re-score the "materially-better" scorecard (`02_POSITIONING`) with real numbers.

## Guardrails
Don't scale on vanity metrics (views) alone — scale on saves/shares + CPA. Don't kill a creative on one post. Don't change two variables at once. Keep a "winners" folder of proven hooks/covers/prompts.
