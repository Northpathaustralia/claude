# 26 · Disclosure, Testimonial, Before/After & AI-Content Policy (DRAFT — AU review)

Provisional; confirm with adviser (ACCC guidance on testimonials/endorsements; platform rules). Not legal advice.

## Testimonials
- **Only real, genuine, consented.** No invented, incentivised-without-disclosure, or edited-to-mislead testimonials.
- Get a signed **testimonial release** (`28_PRIVACY_DATA/03_...`) before publishing any customer words/photos.
- If a testimonial describes a result, it must be **typical or clearly qualified** ("results vary"); keep the evidence on file.
- Disclose any material connection (gift, discount, affiliate).

## Before/after & demonstration content
- Same person, same conditions, **honestly captured** — no relighting/retouching that changes the result, no restaging a fake outcome.
- Uncut on the reveal (split-face / catch). Timing and heat/temp figures must be the **actual** ones filmed.
- Label conceptual/visualised shots as such; never present an AI visualisation as physical proof.

## Influencer / creator collaborations
- Require clear disclosure (#ad / "paid partnership" / gifted) per ACCC + platform rules.
- Brief creators to use only ✅ claims from the claim register; provide the safe-wording list.
- Keep a record of what each creator was asked to say.

## AI-generated content disclosure
- Where a video contains **AI-generated imagery of a person or scene**, disclose appropriately (e.g. "includes AI-generated visuals") per evolving platform/AU expectations.
- **Never** use AI to fabricate a person implied to be a real customer, a real testimonial, or real product-performance proof.
- Kat's AI likeness/voice (if ever used) only with her signed consent (`28_PRIVACY_DATA/03_...`), and disclosed where a viewer could reasonably think it's live footage.

## Privacy in content
- No customer's face/name published without written consent.
- Blur/anonymise third parties captured incidentally.

## Quick pre-publish gate (every asset)
☐ All claims are ✅ in the register ☐ Any testimonial is real + released ☐ Before/after honest + uncut reveal ☐ Material connections disclosed ☐ AI visuals disclosed + not fabricating proof/people ☐ No prohibited (therapeutic/hypoallergenic/guarantee) wording.
