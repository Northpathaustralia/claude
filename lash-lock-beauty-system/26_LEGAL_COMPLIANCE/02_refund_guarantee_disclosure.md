## Guarantee wording (draft) — NOT a results guarantee
> We stand behind the class with a satisfaction refund. We **don't** promise specific results — everyone's skin, eyes and practice are different, and results vary. What we promise is a clear, honest method taught by a working makeup artist.

## Consumer-guarantee note (ACL)
Under the ACL, goods/services carry non-excludable guarantees (acceptable quality, fit for purpose, as described). Do **not** use wording that appears to waive these. Reviewer to confirm exact mandatory phrasing.

## Mandatory disclaimers to include
- "Results vary with practice; this is educational content."
- "Lash Lock is a cosmetic accessory, not a medical device."
- "Individual skin may react differently — discontinue use if irritation occurs." *(only after Gate A confirms no incidents; reviewer to confirm)*
- Country-of-origin / material labelling as advised.

## Prohibited in refund/guarantee context
No "money-back if it doesn't work" results guarantee · no "risk-free" if not literally true · no wording that misrepresents statutory rights.
