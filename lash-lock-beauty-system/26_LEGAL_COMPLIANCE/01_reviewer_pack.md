## Claims flagged by us as high-risk (please prioritise)
- Any "hypoallergenic / non-toxic / skin-safe / de-puffs / soothes / anti-ageing" wording — we have **removed** these pending your view; confirm prohibited or conditions.
- Durability/lifespan numbers — pending supplier evidence.
- "Lasts all day / in the heat" longevity — we intend to state only actually-filmed hours/temp.
- Guarantee vs satisfaction refund — we prefer a simple satisfaction refund, not a results guarantee.

## Questions requiring a written answer
1. Which listed claims may we use as-is, with conditions, or not at all?

4. Required testimonial substantiation + influencer disclosure + AI-generated-content disclosure?
5. Any mandatory product-safety, labelling, or country-of-origin duties for this item?
