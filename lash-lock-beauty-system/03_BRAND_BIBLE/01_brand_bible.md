# 03 · Brand Bible — Voice, Visual & Creative Direction

**Production default (owner decision):** **Real footage first.** Kat films demos and talking-head on iPhone; Higgsfield is used only for B-roll, product beauty shots, transitions and polish. All eye/hand/product contact is always real footage.

---

## 1. Brand foundations
- **Purpose:** Help ordinary Australian women feel put-together and confident with makeup that actually behaves — no mess, no overwhelm, no $3,000 course.
- **One-line value prop:** *Professional results, minus the mess and the mystery.*
- **Known for:** the **clean method** — controlled, no-fallout eye makeup that holds up in Australian conditions.
- **Tagline options (pick one; owner to trademark-check):** "Clean makeup, made simple." · "The method, not just the look." · "Makeup that behaves."

## 2. Brand personality
Warm · credible · practical · calm-confident · a little dry-funny · generous. **She's the pro friend who tells you the truth and shows you the step nobody else does.** Not: salesy, hype-y, perfectionist, intimidating, trend-chasing.

Personality dials (0–10): Warmth **8** · Authority **8** · Playfulness **5** · Polish **7** · Edginess **2** · Aspiration **6** (relatable-aspiration, not unattainable).

## 3. Voice guide
**Sentence style:** short, spoken, second-person ("you"), present tense. Say it the way Kat would say it standing next to you at the mirror.

**Voice principles:**
1. Lead with the problem or the payoff — never a preamble.
2. One idea per piece.
3. Show, then name the step. ("Watch this. That's the control step.")
4. Admit trade-offs — it builds trust ("This won't fix everything, but it fixes the fallout.")
5. End with one clear next action.

**Words Kat USES:** clean · controlled · fallout · under-eyes · the step · repeatable · looks done · holds up · in the heat · what you actually need · without redoing · here's why · watch this.

**Words Kat AVOIDS:** game-changer · viral · miracle · flawless (overused) · guaranteed · #1 · obsessed · girlies · "Hi guys" · anti-ageing · slay · dermatologist-approved / hypoallergenic / waterproof (unless verified).

**Never:** fake urgency ("only 3 left!!"), fake stats, fake testimonials, over-claiming the tool.

## 4. Visual identity direction
- **Feel:** premium, feminine, modern, real, clean, warm, cinematic-but-honest. Australian daylight, not studio-plastic.
- **Skin:** real texture visible. **No** excessive smoothing, no plastic AI skin, no impossible lashes.
- **Colour palette:**
  - Base: warm off-white / oat (#F4EEE6), soft sand (#E4D6C3)
  - Ink: espresso brown (#3B2E26) for text (softer than black)
  - Accent: muted terracotta / clay (#C1745A) — warm, AU, not "beauty pink"
  - Secondary accent: sage/eucalyptus (#8A9A83) — subtle AU nod, used sparingly
  - Metal (sparing): brushed champagne, never shiny gold glitter
- **Typography:**
  - Headlines: a warm high-contrast serif (e.g. *Fraunces*, *Canela*-style) — premium, editorial.
  - Body/captions: a clean humanist sans (e.g. *Inter*, *Aeonik*-style) — legible at speed, sound-off.
  - On-screen caption rule: sans, heavy weight, high contrast, never over the eye area.
- **Avoid (per brief):** gold-glitter luxury clichés, fake clinics/labs, floating products, warped packaging, generic luxury B-roll with no purpose, robotic presenters, unrealistic before/afters.

## 5. Makeup & wardrobe direction (Kat on camera)
- **Makeup:** Kat wears the *clean method* she teaches — polished but attainable. Neutral warm eye, groomed brows, glowy-not-greasy skin, soft everyday liner. Consistency matters more than variety.
- **Wardrobe:** solid, muted tones from the palette (oat, clay, cream, soft sage); scoop/round necklines that don't crowd the face; minimal jewellery (small studs, one thin necklace) — kept **consistent within a batch shoot** for continuity.
- **Nails:** neutral/short — they're in every close-up. Keep colour constant across a shoot.

## 6. Set-design direction (3 repeatable sets — detailed in Continuity Bible)
1. **Kat's beauty station** (teaching/talking-head).
2. **Clean product demo table** (top-down + macro tool shots).
3. **Premium lifestyle vanity/bathroom** (lifestyle B-roll, "getting ready").

## 7. Lighting direction
- **Default:** big soft key (window or one soft LED) at ~45°, slightly above eye line; white reflector fill opposite. Soft shadows. Clean catchlights in the eyes.
- **Macro/demo:** even, shadow-controlled light so fallout/texture reads truthfully (don't hide the proof).
- Colour temp consistent (~4800–5200K); never mix warm tungsten + daylight in frame.

## 8. Music & sound design
- **Music mood:** warm, modern, understated — soft acoustic/lo-fi/neo-soul beds; **always ducked under dialogue.** No aggressive trending-audio that undercuts credibility (use trending audio only for pure-reach top-funnel pieces, tested separately).
- **Sound design:** keep real product sounds (brush taps, cap clicks) — they signal authenticity. Use silence deliberately before a reveal.

## 9. Editing rhythm & camera language
- **Rhythm:** quick but not frantic. Cut on action; reframe/crop when attention would dip; hold on the demo when the technique needs to be *seen*.
- **Camera language:** stable (tripod) for teaching; controlled push-ins/macro for proof; the **split-face** is the signature framing.
- Full standards in `14_EDITING_SYSTEM/` (Batch 06).

## 10. Signature visual devices (ownable)
1. **The Split-Face** — messy side vs clean side, real time, uncut on the reveal. *The* brand device.
2. **The Step Stamp** — each method step appears as a numbered clay-coloured tag ("STEP 2 · CONTROL").
3. **The Catch Reveal** — macro of what the tool catches before it reaches the concealer.
4. **The Heat Check** — end-of-day timestamp in AU-context videos ("6 hrs · 31°C").
5. **The Honest Note** — a recurring "here's where it *won't* help" beat that builds trust.

## 11. Signature series names (repeating formats)
- **"Clean or Messy?"** — split-face proof series.
- **"The Step You're Skipping"** — one pro micro-step per episode.
- **"Fix My Eye"** — diagnose a common viewer mistake.
- **"Does It Hold?"** — AU heat/longevity tests.
- **"Makeup Bag Truth"** — audits: what you actually need.
- **"Bride, Do Your Own"** — DIY bridal micro-lessons.

## 12. Rules for showing Lash Lock naturally
1. Show it **mid-method**, doing its job — never as a bolt-on "and here's my product" ad.
2. Let the **uncut demo** make the claim; keep spoken claims within the verified list only.
3. Include the **Honest Note** — at least one video shows a limitation. Trust > hype.
4. Never over-handle/hero-spin the packaging in a way that looks like a TV-shopping ad.
5. Every 4–5 pieces of pure-value content : ~1 that features the tool prominently (avoid feeling like an ad channel).
