# 10. Offers & Bundles

AOV math: hero products sit at A$30–65. Goal: lift AOV from ~A$45 to A$60–75 so ads can
be profitable at a A$20–25 CAC. Every offer below exists to serve that number.

## Core bundles (permanent collection)

### 1. The Starter Wink — A$69 (value A$99.85, save A$30)
- **Includes:** Chop Boss + SnapStrain + SnipSnip herb scissors.
- **Why it works:** the three most universal problems (chopping, draining, herbs) in one
  box; the natural "I'm new here" purchase; margin stays >60% because two items are cheap.
- **Target buyer:** first-time visitor from a chopper or strainer video.
- **Hook:** "New here? This is the holy trinity. Start where everyone starts."

### 2. The Best Sellers Box — A$119 (value A$164.80, save A$45)
- **Includes:** Chop Boss + RollSharp + Garlic Grenade + SnapStrain.
- **Why:** captures the "just give me the good stuff" buyer; biggest cash order at launch.
- **Target:** returning visitors, email list, gift-to-self buyers.
- **Hook:** "Everything with a five-star cult following. One box. Done."

### 3. The Gift Wink — A$89 (value A$118.85, save A$29)
- **Includes:** FlipGrind duo + ProbeMate thermometer + gift box & card (Lola/Mia-written
  card messages to choose from).
- **Why:** solves "I don't know what to get them"; grinders + thermometer are the two most
  gifted kitchen items; the written-card touch is shareable.
- **Target:** gift buyers (huge in Q4, Mother's/Father's Day, weddings, housewarmings).
- **Hook:** "For the person who says 'don't get me anything' and means the opposite."

### 4. The Couples/New Home Bundle — A$139 (value A$196.75, save A$57)
- **Includes:** Best Sellers Box + FlipGrind duo.
- **Why:** wedding/engagement/moving-in gift registry alternative; highest AOV in store.
- **Target:** 28–45 gift buyers, "first home" audiences.
- **Hook:** "They have a couch and a lease. Now give them a kitchen that flirts back."

### 5. The Meal Prep Bundle — A$79 (value A$107.80, save A$28)
- **Includes:** Chop Boss + SnapStrain + Garlic Grenade + oil sprayer.
- **Why:** rides the permanent meal-prep trend; January hero offer ("New year, new prep era").
- **Target:** fitness/organisation audience, Sunday-prep TikTok viewers.
- **Hook:** "Sunday prep in 20 minutes. Your Wednesday self says thanks."

### 6. The Kitchen Reset — A$75 (value A$97.80, save A$22)
- **Includes:** ScrubBot + SnackLock 2-pack + SnapStrain.
- **Why:** CleanTok crossover audience; strong for renters/bond cleans; refreshes the
  catalogue for people who already bought cooking tools.
- **Target:** cleaning-content audience, existing customers (email exclusive first).
- **Hook:** "For kitchens with a past. Total amnesty. Fresh start."

## Launch & conversion offers

### 7. Limited-time launch offer — "The First 500 Winks"
- First 500 orders get: 15% off sitewide (code **WINK15**) + free surprise gift (herb
  scissors or sealer — cheap, high perceived value) + founding-customer status (early
  access to future drops).
- **Why:** genuine, countable scarcity (an order counter, not a fake timer). Founding
  status feeds the future VIP club.
- **Copy:** "We just opened the doors. First 500 kitchens get 15% off, a surprise gadget,
  and permanent bragging rights. Counter's live. 😉"

### 8. Free shipping threshold — A$60
- Set just above the hero-product price point (A$49.95) so nearly every buyer adds one
  small item (SnapStrain A$16.95, SnipSnip A$19.95) to qualify. Announce in the top bar,
  show a progress bar in cart ("A$10.05 away from free shipping — the herb scissors are
  *right there*").

### 9. Buy-more strategy — "The More the Merrier"
- Sitewide automatic tiers: 2 items = 10% off · 3 items = 15% off · 4+ = 20% off.
- Works because margins are 65–75%; a 20% discount on a 4-item cart still beats a
  full-price single-item cart on absolute profit and on shipping efficiency.
- **Copy:** "Gadgets get lonely. 2 = 10% off · 3 = 15% · 4+ = 20%."

### 10. Cart upsell (in-cart, one click)
- Rules: cart contains Chop Boss → offer SnapStrain at A$12 (29% off). Cart contains
  RollSharp → offer cut-glove +A$12. Any cart under A$60 → offer SnipSnip to unlock free
  shipping. One offer max — decision fatigue kills carts.
- **Copy:** "Pairs deliciously with… (and unlocks free shipping, just saying)."

### 11. Post-purchase upsell (after payment, before thank-you page)
- One-click add without re-entering payment: 50% off a second Garlic Grenade, or A$19
  ProbeMate with any A$80+ order. Target 15–20% take rate; this is pure-margin AOV.
- **Copy:** "Order locked in! One more thing — because you clearly have taste: [offer].
  One click, ships in the same box, gone in 10 minutes."

### 12. Abandoned cart offer (email/SMS — full flow in `08-email-sms.md`)
- Sequence: 1h reminder (no discount) → 24h social proof + FAQ (no discount) → 48h
  **WINKBACK10** 10% code with 48-hour expiry (real expiry, enforced).
- **Why staged:** most carts recover without a discount; training customers that
  abandoning = coupon erodes margin. The 10% is the closer, not the opener.
- **SMS copy:** "Lola here 👋 your cart's getting cold and the Chop Boss doesn't deserve
  that. 10% says you finish the job: WINKBACK10 (48h) → [link]"
