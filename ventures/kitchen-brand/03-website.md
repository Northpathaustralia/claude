## Website design direction

**Colour palette**
- Cream base `#FFF6EC` (warm, appetizing, not clinical white)
- Ink charcoal `#2B2623` (text — softer than pure black)
- Tomato coral `#FF5A45` (primary CTA — appetite + energy; use for every buy button, nothing else)
- Butter yellow `#FFD166` (accents, badges, highlights)
- Sage `#9CB89C` (secondary sections, trust blocks)
- Blush `#F7C5BA` (sister-content sections)
Rule: coral = action only. If everything is loud, nothing is.

**Fonts:** Display — a rounded confident serif or chunky rounded sans (e.g. *Recoleta* /
*Fraunces* for charm, or *Poppins SemiBold* for the budget option). Body — *Inter* or
system stack. Two families max. Big sizes, generous line height, short lines.

**Logo:** wordmark "Whisk & Wink" with the ampersand or the "i" dotted by a tiny winking
face / whisk that doubles as a closed winking eye. One-colour versions required (packaging,
favicon). Skip paid design at launch: wordmark + wink glyph is achievable in Canva; budget
A$50–150 on a designer at month 2–3.

**Hero & sister imagery:** golden-hour warm kitchen, shallow depth of field, real mess
(flour, herbs) — lifestyle, not showroom. Generate the sisters with a locked character
reference (consistent faces across all assets — tools: Midjourney character reference,
Flux/LoRA character training, or HeyGen/Arcads-style avatar video). Style rule: creator
selfie energy, natural skin texture, no plastic-doll rendering — uncanny kills conversion.

**Product imagery:** 1) clean cutout on cream, 2) in-hand action shot, 3) results shot,
4) GIF/video loop of the money moment, 5) "what's included" flat lay. Never raw AliExpress
photos — reshoot/regenerate everything; it's the single biggest trust signal in dropshipping.

**Icons:** rounded-line icon set, 2px stroke, butter-yellow filled circles behind. Same set
everywhere (shipping truck, shield, chat bubble, wink).

**Mobile-first layout rules:** thumb-reach sticky add-to-cart on product pages; hero
message readable in 2 seconds; sections max 60% viewport height; every image lazy-loaded
and compressed (target <2.5s LCP — speed is conversion).

**Trust elements:** payment icons at checkout (Apple Pay/Google Pay/PayPal/Shop Pay —
enable all), guarantee badge near add-to-cart, delivery estimate near add-to-cart,
review stars above the fold, "human support" link in footer. Checkout: no surprise
costs — show shipping early; that's the #1 abandonment cause.

**Homepage wireframe (mobile):**
`Announcement bar → Hero (loop video + H1 + CTA) → Social proof strip → Best sellers
(2×2) → Problem/solution (3 swipe cards) → Video reel embed → Meet the sisters (photo +
3 lines + CTA) → Bundles (3 cards) → Why us (4 icons) → Reviews carousel → FAQ accordion
→ Final CTA → Footer (nav, legal, email capture "Get the weekly wink 😉")`

**Product page wireframe (mobile):**
`Gallery (video first) → Title + stars + price + shipping ETA → Sticky Add to Cart →
Benefit bullets (3–5) → Sister quote block → How it works (3 steps) → What's included →
Reviews → FAQ accordion → Guarantee banner → Cross-sell ("Pairs deliciously with…") →
Recently viewed`
