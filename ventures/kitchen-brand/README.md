# Whisk & Wink — Complete Launch Playbook

A launch-ready ecommerce business plan: unique kitchen utensils and smart kitchen gadgets,
sold via dropshipping, fronted by two sister brand characters (Lola & Mia) who make cooking
content that's funny, charming, and impossible to scroll past.

## 1. Executive Summary

**The business:** Whisk & Wink is a personality-led kitchen gadget store. Instead of the
usual faceless dropshipping site, the brand is fronted by two sisters in their early 20s —
Lola (blonde, bold, cheeky) and Mia (brunette, sweet, clever) — who demo products, roast
each other, and turn kitchen problems into 20-second comedy. The store sells proven,
high-demo-value gadgets (viral choppers, rolling sharpeners, clip-on strainers, auto
stirrers) at 3–4x markup, fulfilled by dropship suppliers so there's zero inventory risk.

**Why it wins:** Kitchen gadgets are one of the most reliable impulse-buy niches — visual,
satisfying, problem-solving, giftable. But 95% of kitchen stores are interchangeable. The
moat here is not the products; it's the *characters*. People follow people. A brand with
faces, banter, and running jokes gets cheap organic reach, better ad hook rates, higher
trust, and repeat customers. The sisters are AI-generated virtual creators presented in a
warm, human, personality-driven way — with light-touch disclosure that keeps the brand
ethical and ad-platform safe (see `10-risks-roadmap.md`; this is non-negotiable and it
does not hurt performance — the biggest virtual influencers are all disclosed).

**The model:** Shopify (or Basic) + CJdropshipping/Zendrop fulfilment + Klaviyo email +
organic TikTok/Reels/Shorts as the primary engine + small, disciplined Meta/TikTok ad
tests. Launch cost: roughly **A$300–700 total** before scaling ads.

**Targets (realistic):**
- Month 1: store live, 30+ organic videos posted, first 30–100 orders, find 1–2 winning creatives.
- Month 2: scale winning ad sets, AOV ≥ A$55 via bundles, email flows producing 15–25% of revenue.
- Month 3: 1–2 hero products carrying the account, private-label evaluation for the #1 seller.

**Headline economics (typical hero product):** cost ~A$14 landed → sell A$49.95 →
gross margin ~A$32 → target blended CAC ≤ A$20 → bundles lift AOV to A$60–75.
