# 12–13. Paid Ads Plan & Organic Content Plan

## 12. Paid ads plan (minimal budget, maximum signal)

### Philosophy
Organic finds the winners; paid scales them. Never put a creative into paid that hasn't
shown organic signal (watch time, saves, comments) unless you're seeding launch data.
Budget discipline beats budget size.

### Campaign structure — Meta (Facebook + Instagram)
```
Campaign 1: TOF Testing (CBO off, ABO) — A$30–50/day total
├── Ad set A: Broad AU, 22–55, no interests (Advantage+ audience) — 3 creatives
├── Ad set B: Interest stack "cooking, kitchen gadgets, meal prep" — same 3 creatives
└── Ad set C: Broad, creative test lane — 3 NEW creatives rotated weekly
Campaign 2: Retargeting (start week 2+) — A$10–15/day
├── Ad set: 7-day site visitors + cart abandoners — social proof & objection ads
└── Ad set: 30-day video viewers (75%) + IG/FB engagers — bundle offers
Campaign 3 (month 2+): Advantage+ Shopping consolidation — winners only
```
- **Objective:** Purchase conversions from day one (not traffic, not engagement — those
  optimize for clickers, not buyers).
- **Pixel + Conversions API** both installed before spending a dollar.

### Campaign structure — TikTok
```
Campaign 1: Smart Performance / manual CBO — A$20–30/day
├── Ad group: Broad AU 22–55 — 3–4 Spark Ads (boosting the organic posts that worked)
└── Ad group: Creative test lane — new hooks weekly
```
- Use **Spark Ads** (boost the actual organic posts) — they keep social proof (likes/
  comments) attached and consistently outperform dark ads at this budget level.

### Daily budget recommendation (total ecosystem)
- Weeks 1–2: A$40–60/day (Meta-heavy). Weeks 3–4: A$60–90/day shifting into winners.
- Hard floor rule: every ad set needs ≥ A$10/day or it never exits learning.
- Monthly cap while testing: ~A$1,500–2,000. Do not exceed until a creative hits the
  scale criteria below.

### Creative testing plan
- Test **hooks first, products second**: same body footage, 3 different first-3-seconds.
- 3 new creatives/week minimum into the test lane. Kill-or-keep every 3–4 days.
- Angles to rotate per product: problem/solution · demo-only ASMR · sister comedy skit ·
  testimonial-style · gift angle · comparison/test.
- Naming convention: `product_angle_hook_v1` (e.g. `chopboss_problem_tears_v2`) so
  reporting stays readable.

### Audience testing
- At this budget, audience testing is mostly a distraction: **broad + great creative
  wins**. Test only: Broad vs one interest stack vs one lookalike (purchasers, once
  ≥100 purchases exist). Let creative do the targeting.

### Retargeting setup (10 ad concepts below)
- Windows: 7-day cart/checkout abandoners (highest bid), 14-day product viewers,
  30-day video viewers/engagers. Exclude purchasers (180 days) from all prospecting.
- Frequency cap watch: if frequency >4 and CTR halves, refresh creative or narrow window.

### Winning creative criteria (scale it when…)
- Hook rate (3-sec views/impressions) ≥ 30% · Hold rate (ThruPlay) ≥ 10%
- CTR (link) ≥ 1.5% Meta / ≥ 1% TikTok · CPA ≤ A$25 on hero products, ≤ A$35 on bundles
- ROAS ≥ 2.0 at test spend (break-even is ~1.4–1.6 at our margins)

### When to kill an ad
- A$30 spent, zero adds-to-cart → kill. · 2× target CPA spent, zero purchases → kill.
- CTR < 0.8% AND hook rate < 20% after 1,000 impressions → kill (creative is dead on
  arrival; don't wait for conversions to confirm).

### When & how to scale
- Vertical: +20–30% budget every 48–72h on the winning ad set (bigger jumps reset learning).
- Horizontal: duplicate winner into Advantage+ / new ad set at 2× budget; clone the
  winning hook onto 3 new bodies.
- Never touch a winning ad set intraday. Judge on 3-day rolling ROAS, not single days.

### Best-performing elements (build creatives from these)
- **Hooks:** problem-first ("you've been doing X wrong"), timer/challenge, "wait for it,"
  price-anchor ("cheaper than one takeaway"), sister-conflict cold open.
- **CTAs:** "Tap to fix dinner forever" · "Get yours — 30-day guarantee" · "Free AU
  shipping over $60" · "Gift-ready in one click" · (retargeting) "Still thinking about it?
  It's still thinking about you."
- **Angles by product:** Chop Boss = time/tears · RollSharp = transformation ·
  SnackLock = "sounds fake, watch" · FlipGrind/ProbeMate = gift/status ·
  ScrubBot = disgust→satisfaction · Stir Crazy = lazy-genius comedy.

### Ad copy examples (primary text)
1. "One press = a diced onion. No tears, no board, no 20-minute prep. 30-day guarantee
   because we know you'll keep it. 🧅😉"
2. "Your knives aren't bad — they're neglected. 60 seconds with RollSharp and the tomato
   won't know what hit it. Watch till the slice."
3. "We shook a resealed chip bag over Lola's head. Nothing fell out. SnackLock: airtight
   and smug. Free AU shipping over $60."

## 13. Organic content plan

### TikTok (primary engine)
- **Cadence:** 1–2 posts/day for the first 60 days (volume finds the algorithm), then
  1/day sustained. Post 11am–1pm and 6–9pm AEST to start; let analytics correct you.
- **Mix (weekly):** 4 product demos/hacks · 3 banter/trend/skit · 2 satisfying/ASMR ·
  1 gift/bundle · 1 series episode.
- **Series (appointment viewing):** "Mum Reviews" (weekly) · "We Tested It So You Don't
  Have To" (weekly) · "Which Sister Are You?" (fortnightly) · "Kitchen Reset Sundays."
- Reply to top comments with video replies — cheapest reach multiplier on the platform.
- TikTok Shop AU: join as soon as volume justifies it; affiliate commission opens the
  UGC floodgates.

### Instagram Reels + feed
- Repost native cuts (no TikTok watermark), 1 Reel/day, 3 grid posts/week (product
  glamour, meme, review screenshot), Stories daily (polls: "Lola's method or Mia's?",
  behind-the-scenes, restock alerts). Broadcast channel for VIP drops in month 2+.
- Bio link: Linktree-style page → bestsellers, bundles, latest-video product.

### YouTube Shorts
- Repost the 10 best performers/week. Shorts has the longest shelf life and feeds search
  ("knife sharpener test"). Zero extra production cost — pure upside.

### Facebook page + Reels
- Repost Reels; FB is where the 40+ gift-buyer lives — "Mum Reviews" and gift angles
  over-index here. Join/organically participate in AU cooking & meal-prep groups as the
  brand page where group rules allow (never spam).

### Pinterest (sleeper channel for this niche)
- 5 pins/week: meal-prep rainbow fridge, gift guides, kitchen-reset checklists, each
  pinned to SEO-titled boards ("Kitchen gadget gift ideas Australia"). Pinterest traffic
  compounds for months and converts absurdly well on gift bundles in Q4.

### SEO (compounding, start week 1, expect results month 3+)
- Blog = "The Saucy Sisters Sessions." 1 post/week, 800–1,200 words, sister voice:
  - "Best kitchen gadgets under $50 (tested, timed, argued over)" — commercial
  - "How to sharpen a knife at home without a whetstone" — RollSharp intent
  - "10 meal prep hacks that take under 10 minutes" — traffic + bundle links
  - "Kitchen gift guide: what to buy every person you know" — Q4 magnet
- Product pages: unique copy (done — see `04-product-pages.md`), descriptive alt text,
  FAQ schema on FAQ blocks, review schema via Judge.me. Site speed budget: LCP <2.5s.

### Influencer / UGC strategy
- Month 1: seed 10–15 micro creators (5k–50k, AU food/home niche) with free product for
  content rights (gifting-for-usage agreement in writing — usage in ads is the point).
- Month 2: pay the 2–3 whose content performed (A$100–300/video + affiliate code).
- Month 3: TikTok Shop affiliates at 15–20% commission — let volume come to you.
- Always secure **whitelisting/Spark codes** so their posts can run as ads from their
  handles — outperforms brand-handle ads consistently.
