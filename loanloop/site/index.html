How it works

No developer needed. No app to log into all day. It just runs.

1. Paste one snippet

Copy a single embed code onto your homepage, a landing page, or your email signature link.

2. Visitors self-serve

They enter income, debts and deposit and instantly see an indicative borrowing range — always shown as a range, always with the required disclaimer.

3. You get the lead

Every submission lands in your dashboard and, once connected, your inbox and CRM — automatically, with zero manual work.
