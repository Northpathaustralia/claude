# Target market

## Primary ICP

**Independent Australian mortgage/finance brokers**, sole operators or small brokerages (1–10
brokers), who:

- Already have a website but generate little inbound business from it.
- Are members of the MFAA or FBAA (a usable acquisition channel — see `ACQUISITION_STRATEGY.md`).
- Spend money on other lead sources (Google Ads, comparison-site referrals, portal leads at
  $30–$150 per lead) and would recognise the cost comparison immediately.
- Are not developers — the entire pitch depends on "paste one snippet, done."

There are roughly 20,000+ active credit representatives / mortgage brokers in Australia (per
industry association membership data), most operating small, website-having, marketing-light
practices. Even a low single-digit percentage is a viable initial market for a $0–$99/mo product.

## Secondary ICPs (post-MVP expansion, not built yet)

- Buyer's agents and mortgage aggregator franchise networks (bulk/white-label deals).
- Financial planners who touch borrowing capacity as part of broader advice (need extra care —
  higher compliance bar, likely requires legal review before targeting).
- Real estate agencies wanting a "what could I borrow" widget as a lead magnet for listings pages.

## Explicitly not the target (for now)

- Consumers directly (LoanLoop sells to brokers, not to borrowers — the borrower is the broker's
  lead, not LoanLoop's customer).
- Large banks/lenders — enterprise sales cycle and compliance requirements are a different
  business entirely.

## Why brokers say yes

1. **Zero build cost to them** — they already have a website; this is one paste, not a project.
2. **Cost per lead is the natural comparison point** — Free tier costs $0 and caps at 20 leads/mo;
   Pro at $49/mo beats almost any paid lead source once it delivers more than 2–3 leads/month.
3. **No compliance exposure added** — the tool never gives credit advice or promises outcomes, so
   it doesn't create new obligations under their existing Australian Credit Licence (see
   `COMPLIANCE_AND_PRIVACY.md`).
