AMAZON BOOK DESCRIPTION (paste into KDP "Description")
--------------------------------------------------

Everyone carries something we cannot see.

In a warm little forest lives a tiny echidna named Pip — a little friend who
notices things others sometimes miss. One morning, Pip discovers something
amazing: every animal in the forest is carrying a backpack. Not a backpack
you could touch. These backpacks were invisible.

Kangaroo's looks light. Birdie's looks tiny. But Koala's backpack looks very
heavy indeed, and Koala sits alone, not climbing, not playing.

Through small, gentle moments — helping Wombat pick up dropped berries,
listening when Birdie is too shy to sing, noticing that a very bright smile
can still be carrying something heavy — Pip discovers a simple truth:
kindness makes backpacks lighter.

The Invisible Backpack is a warm, gentle picture book about empathy,
emotional awareness and everyday kindness, perfect for children aged 3 to 7
and for the grown-ups who read with them.

This heartfelt story helps little readers understand that:
- Everyone is carrying something, even when we can't see it
- Small acts of kindness really do make a difference
- Noticing a friend, and simply being kind, matters

Because we may not see what someone is carrying, but we can always choose
to be kind.

Includes a "For Parents & Teachers" page with gentle discussion questions
for talking about feelings with your child.

RECOMMENDED KINDLE PRICE
--------------------------------------------------
Launch price: USD $3.99 (AUD ~$5.99)
- Sits in Amazon's 70% royalty band ($2.99-$9.99)
- Note: Kindle delivery fees apply per megabyte on the 70% plan, and
  picture books have large image files. Compare royalties at 70% vs 35%
  in the KDP pricing screen and pick whichever earns more per sale.
- Optional launch tactic: $0.99 for the first week to gather early
  reviews, then raise to $3.99.

ISBN: none required. Kindle ebooks do not need an ISBN, and Amazon
assigns its own ASIN automatically. A paperback edition (if pursued
later) gets a free KDP-assigned ISBN. Do NOT invent or reuse any
ISBN or barcode.

James Forycki is an Australian author who writes gentle, heartfelt
stories for young children. Inspired by the animals and quiet corners

The Invisible Backpack is his debut picture book.
