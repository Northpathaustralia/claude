AMAZON BOOK DESCRIPTION (paste into KDP "Description")
--------------------------------------------------

Everyone carries something we cannot see.

Deep in the Australian bush, a small echidna named Pip discovers something
amazing: every animal is carrying an invisible backpack. Some are light and
bouncy. Some are very, very heavy.

Wombat's day feels too hard. Birdie is too scared to sing. Kangaroo hides her
worries behind the biggest smile in the bush. And gentle Koala is carrying
the heaviest backpack of all.

When a storm rolls in and Koala is left alone in the rain, Pip does the
bravest, kindest thing a friend can do. He sits down beside him and says
two small words: "I'm here."

The Invisible Backpack is a warm, gentle story about empathy, emotional
awareness and quiet kindness — perfect for children aged 3 to 7, and for
every grown-up who has ever needed a friend in a storm.

This heartfelt Australian picture book helps little readers understand that:
- Everyone is carrying something, even when we can't see it
- We don't have to fix our friends — being there is enough
- No one has to carry their backpack alone

Because we may not see what someone is carrying, but we can always choose
to be kind.

Includes a "Note for grown-ups" with a simple conversation starter for
talking about big feelings with your child.

RECOMMENDED KINDLE PRICE
--------------------------------------------------
Launch price: USD $3.99 (AUD ~$5.99)
- Sits in Amazon's 70% royalty band ($2.99-$9.99)
- Note: Kindle delivery fees apply per megabyte on the 70% plan, and
  picture books have large image files. If the final file is large,
  compare royalties at 70% vs 35% in the KDP pricing screen and pick
  whichever earns more per sale.
- Optional launch tactic: $0.99 for the first week to gather early
  reviews, then raise to $3.99.

ISBN: none required. Kindle ebooks do not need an ISBN, and Amazon
assigns its own ASIN automatically. Do NOT invent an ISBN or barcode.

AUTHOR BIOGRAPHY (paste into Author Central / About the Author)
--------------------------------------------------
James Forycki is an Australian author who writes gentle, heartfelt
stories for young children. Inspired by the animals and wide skies of
the Australian bush, James writes about the big feelings little people
carry — and the small, everyday kindnesses that make them lighter.
The Invisible Backpack is his debut picture book. He hopes it helps
at least one small reader (and one grown-up) feel a little less alone.
