## 6. MASTER PAGE SPECIFICATIONS (Pages 1–32)

For every page: final wording, illustration description, characters, expressions, backpack appearance, and text position. These specs are what each supplied image will be audited against — and what any replacement image must be generated from.

### Global style rules (apply to every page)

- **Style:** gentle Australian watercolour picture-book style, soft edges, warm natural light
- **Pip:** small brown echidna, large expressive brown eyes, natural spikes, always wearing the same **teal-blue backpack** (solid, opaque, small front pocket)
- **Koala:** grey, gentle, quiet; his **invisible backpack** is drawn as a faint, semi-transparent teal outline (the visual device for "invisible")
- **Kangaroo:** female, warm golden-brown, friendly face — identical in every appearance
- **Wombat:** brown, round, gentle
- **Birdie:** small blue Australian bird (fairy-wren-like) — same species, colour and markings on every page
- **Invisible backpacks on other animals:** always the same faint translucent-teal outline treatment
- **Text:** large rounded child-friendly font, high contrast against the background, generous space, never within 10% of any page edge (safe zone for trimming/screens)
- **Never:** humans, dogs, mice, substitute birds, random new characters, ISBNs, barcodes, publisher logos, "prototype" wording, or visible AI text artefacts
