# The Invisible Backpack — Beginner's Guide to Publishing on Amazon KDP

Written for a complete beginner. No technical jargon. Follow in order.

## Before you start
You cannot publish yet — 8 pages (23–30) still need real illustrations.
See `The_Invisible_Backpack_Page_Order.md` for exactly which ones and why.
Everything below is ready to use the moment those pages are finished.

## Step 1 — Create your KDP account
1. Go to kdp.amazon.com and sign in with (or create) an Amazon account.
2. Fill in your author/publisher details and address.
3. Complete the "tax interview" — as an Australian author you'll fill in
   an Australian tax form. This is standard and takes about 10 minutes.
   Australia has a tax treaty with the US, so less tax is withheld from
   your royalties. This guide can't give tax advice — if unsure, ask an
   accountant.
4. Add your Australian bank details so Amazon can pay your royalties.

## Step 2 — Create the Kindle eBook listing
1. From your KDP dashboard, click **Create** → **Kindle eBook**.
2. Enter:
   - Title: `The Invisible Backpack`
   - Subtitle: `A story about kindness, empathy and the things we cannot see.`
   - Author: `James Forycki`
3. Paste the description from `The_Invisible_Backpack_KDP_Metadata.txt`.
4. Enter the 7 keywords and 3 categories from that same file.
5. Confirm this is NOT a public domain work, and you own the rights.

## Step 3 — Upload your interior file
1. Under "Manuscript," upload your finished, print-ready book file
   (once pages 23–30 are complete — see `The_Invisible_Backpack_Master.pdf`
   once it exists).
2. KDP will process it and generate a preview automatically.

## Step 4 — Upload your cover
1. Upload your square cover image (from page 1 of the book).
2. Make sure it's at least 2,048 x 2,048 pixels, JPG format.

## Step 5 — Use the Online Previewer
1. Click "Preview" and page through the ENTIRE book on the phone,
   tablet, and Kindle e-reader views.
2. Check every page for: text cut off, blurry images, wrong reading
   order. If anything looks wrong, go back and fix the source file,
   then re-upload — never publish with a page you haven't personally
   looked at.

## Common upload errors and fixes
- **"Image resolution too low"** — your page needs to be closer to
  300 DPI. Re-export the artwork at a higher pixel size.
- **"File too large"** — compress images (keep quality above 90%) or
  reduce page count of very heavy image files.
- **"Fonts not embedded"** — if you build the PDF from a design tool,
  make sure "embed fonts" is turned on in the export settings.

## Step 6 — Publish the Kindle version
1. Set your price (see recommendation below).
2. Choose "70% royalty" if eligible (usually is, for $2.99–$9.99 books).
3. Choose territories — "All territories" is simplest for a new author.
4. Click Publish. It typically goes live within 24–72 hours.

## Step 7 — (Optional but recommended) Order a paperback proof
1. From the same KDP title, click **Create paperback**.
2. KDP paperback gives you a FREE ISBN automatically — do not buy or
   invent one yourself.
3. Upload the same interior (formatted for print - check trim size and
   bleed) and a full wraparound cover (front + spine + back). KDP's
   cover calculator tells you the exact spine width based on page count.
4. Order a physical proof copy before making the book public — always
   check a real printed copy before telling anyone it's available.

## How royalties work (plain English)
- Kindle: Amazon pays 70% of the sale price (minus a small delivery
  fee based on file size) for books priced $2.99–$9.99. Below or above
  that range, you get 35%.
- Paperback: Amazon pays a royalty based on price minus printing cost
  (printing cost depends on page count and whether it's colour).
  Amazon shows you the exact number before you set your price.
- Royalties are paid roughly 60 days after the end of the sales month.

## Suggested pricing
- **Kindle launch price:** $3.99 USD (see metadata file for reasoning)
- **Paperback price:** typically $9.99–$14.99 USD for a 32-page colour
  picture book, once you see your actual printing cost in KDP.
- **Long-term:** many picture books settle around $4.99 Kindle /
  $12.99 paperback once reviews build up.

## Kindle Unlimited — is it right for you?
Kindle Unlimited (via "KDP Select") pays you per page read instead of
per sale, and requires exclusivity to Amazon for 90 days at a time.
For a first picture book, it can help with discoverability, but it
means you can't sell the ebook on Apple Books, Kobo, etc. during that
time. A common beginner approach: launch on Amazon only via KDP Select
for the first 90 days to build reviews, then decide whether to renew
or go wide.

## A simple Australian tax reminder
As an Australian author selling through a US company, you'll have
some US tax withheld unless you complete the tax interview correctly,
and your Australian royalty income will need to be declared to the
ATO at tax time. This guide is not tax advice — a bookkeeper or
accountant familiar with self-published authors can help you get this
right the first time.
