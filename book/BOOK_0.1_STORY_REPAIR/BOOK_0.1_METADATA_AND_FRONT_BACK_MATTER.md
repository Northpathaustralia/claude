> **For Parents & Teachers**
> Everyone carries an invisible backpack — feelings, worries and hard

> • What might someone carry inside their invisible backpack?

## Back-cover copy (final)
> Everyone carries something we cannot see.
>
> In a warm little forest, a tiny echidna named Pip notices what others
> miss — that every friend carries an invisible backpack. Wombat's feels
> heavy with a hard day. Birdie's holds the fear of getting it wrong.
> Kangaroo hides hers behind a tired, bouncing smile. And gentle Koala
> carries the heaviest backpack of all.
>
> Pip learns that kindness is not always about fixing a problem.
> Sometimes it is helping. Sometimes it is listening. Sometimes it is
> simply staying.
>
> Because everyone carries something — and no one should have to carry
> it alone.
>
> A gentle Australian story about empathy, feelings and belonging, for
> children aged 3–7. Includes discussion questions for parents and teachers.
