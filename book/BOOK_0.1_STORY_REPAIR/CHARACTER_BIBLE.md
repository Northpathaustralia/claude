# The Invisible Backpack — Character Bible (Book 0.1)
Consolidated from the verified continuity sheet + Book 0.1 art. This is
the single source of truth for every character across the 40-page edition.
Any new/replacement page MUST conform to this.

## PIP — the echidna (protagonist)
- Small brown echidna; **not** a hedgehog. Rounded, compact, bipedal.
- Honey-tan fur; golden-tan spines darkening to brown at the tips, on
  head and back only (never face/chest/belly).
- Long smooth brown-black snout; small dark nose tip.
- **Large round warm-brown eyes** with white catchlight — carries all
  emotion; never small or generic.
- Solid **opaque teal-blue backpack**, two straps, front flap, single
  **gold buckle button**. The only "clothing" Pip ever wears.
- Personality (visual): gentle, observant, unhurried. Never runs or
  shouts. Defined by attention and empathy ("Pip noticed things others
  sometimes missed").
- Arc: notices feelings → learns backpack size can mislead → helps
  (Wombat) → listens/accepts (Birdie) → lets someone stop performing
  (Kangaroo) → learns he cannot remove pain, only stay (Koala).

## BIRDIE — the small blue bird
- Small plump songbird (blue-wren type); dusty **sky-blue** head/back/
  wings/tail, **pale cream** throat/chest, small dark beak, dark round eyes.
- Faint translucent pale backpack outline (not solid).
- Tiny — perch-sized, ~Pip's head. Male ("he").
- Personality: shy, tender; courage shown by lifting head / one tiny note.
- **ART WATCH:** on the "sang one tiny note / then another" page, the
  singer must clearly be Birdie (blue bird), not Pip.

## KOALA — gentle, the emotional climax
- Grey, soft, rounded, quiet. Carries the heaviest invisible backpack.
- His backpack must **never vanish or be thrown away** — it stays heavy
  but feels lighter because he is not alone. Emotional realism is the
  whole point.

## KANGAROO — female, warm golden-brown
- Friendly natural muzzle; **no human teeth**, no uncanny grin.
- In Book 0.1 she wears a teal backpack (echoing Pip) once revealed —
  reinforcing "everyone carries something."
- Arc: performs (bounces, tired smile) → admits fear ("scared I'll
  fall / won't be enough") → allowed to rest ("You don't have to bounce
  to be loved").

## WOMBAT — brown, round, gentle
- Simple, warm. His arc is the simplest kindness: practical help
  (dropped berries) makes his backpack a little lighter.

## BACKPACK METAPHOR RULES (enforce across all pages)
1. **Pip's** backpack: solid opaque teal + gold buckle — real/physical, the anchor.
2. **Others'** invisible backpacks: soft, pale, semi-transparent outline
   — the visual device for hidden feelings.
3. Heavier feeling → backpack looks larger/darker; lighter → smaller/paler.
4. **Never** show a character physically removing, dropping, or throwing
   away an emotional backpack — feelings aren't "thrown away," they're
   shared. (Book 0.1's later pages trend toward more solid-looking packs;
   keep them symbolic, never discarded.)
5. No character ever becomes a different species, human, or wears clothing
   (Pip's backpack excepted).
