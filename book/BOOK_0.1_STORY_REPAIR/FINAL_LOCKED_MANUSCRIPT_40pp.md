4. **Dedication** — For every little heart carrying big feelings.
5. In a warm little forest lived a tiny echidna named Pip.
6. Pip noticed things others sometimes missed.
7. One morning, all the animals walked to the clearing.
8. Kangaroo bounced. Koala wandered. Wombat shuffled. Birdie fluttered.
9. Then Pip noticed something strange.
10. Everyone was carrying a backpack.
11. Not a backpack you could touch.
12. These backpacks were invisible.
13. Kangaroo's backpack **seemed** light. She laughed and leaped.
14. Birdie's backpack **seemed** tiny. He sang sweet songs.
15. But Koala's backpack looked very heavy.
16. Koala sat alone under the gum tree. He did not climb. He did not play.
17. Pip asked softly, "Are you okay, Koala?"
18. "I think so," said Koala. But his voice sounded small.
19. At lunch, Wombat dropped his berries. They rolled into the dirt.
20. Pip helped pick them up, one by one.
21. "Thank you," whispered Wombat.
22. His backpack looked a little lighter.
23. Pip blinked. Kindness makes backpacks lighter.
24. Near the river, Birdie sat quietly. He was not singing.
25. "Someone laughed when I sang the wrong note," Birdie said.
26. "I like your song," said Pip. "Even the wobbly parts."
27. Birdie sang one tiny note. Then another.
28. His backpack grew lighter too.
29. Later, Pip saw Kangaroo bouncing high. But her smile looked tired.
30. "Is your backpack heavy too?" Pip asked.
31. "Sometimes I'm scared I'll fall," whispered Kangaroo. "Sometimes I'm scared I won't be enough."
32. "You don't have to bounce to be loved," Pip said. They sat still together.
33. **[STORM BRIDGE — canonical, from James's book]** Then dark clouds rolled in. Rain fell hard. Thunder rumbled.
34. Everyone ran to the hollow log. Everyone except Koala.
35. Pip walked into the storm and sat beside him. "I'm here," he said.
36. "My backpack feels too heavy today," Koala whispered.

> **EDITORIAL DECISION (13 Jul 2026):** James's book already contained a
> storm bridge for pages 33–36. It is adopted as canonical over the
> earlier "sunny" bridge I drafted when those pages weren't yet visible.
> Reasons: (1) page 38's "When the clouds cleared" REQUIRES the storm to
> make sense; (2) the storm climax ("Pip walked into the storm… 'I'm
> here'") is more powerful; (3) it passes the trauma-informed safety
> gate (gentle art, brave/warm beat, reassuring resolution). The sunny
> alternate pages are archived, NOT used. Do not include both.
37. One by one, the others joined them. No one let Koala carry it alone.
38. When the clouds cleared, Koala smiled. "It still feels heavy, but lighter than before."
39. **For Parents & Teachers**
    - What do you think an invisible backpack means?
    - What might someone carry in their backpack?
    - How did Pip help his friends?
    - What did Pip do when he could not make Koala's feelings disappear?
    - What kind thing could you do for someone today?
    *Notice. Ask. Listen. Stay. And when feelings feel too big, tell a trusted grown-up.*
40. **Everyone carries something.** / **No one should have to carry it alone.**
    *The Invisible Backpack is a gentle story about kindness, empathy and the feelings we cannot always see. Join Pip the little echidna as he learns that everyone carries something — and small acts of kindness can help lighten the load.*
