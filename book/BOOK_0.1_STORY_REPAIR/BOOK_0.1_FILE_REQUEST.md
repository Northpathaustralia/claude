## Why the last upload didn't work (so it's easy to fix)
The two `.eml` files were Gmail forwards. In Gmail, images pasted from
Google Drive are *links*, not attachments — so when the email was saved
as `.eml`, no image data came with it. What I received was text only,
plus the 5 low-resolution composite screenshots in the chat (which cover
only pages 17–40, four pages squashed into each image).

   `Pages_23_to_30_Claude_Ready.zip` you sent before — that worked

dropped the files.
