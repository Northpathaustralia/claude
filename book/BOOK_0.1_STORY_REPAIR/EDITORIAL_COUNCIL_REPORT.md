# Editorial Council Report — The Invisible Backpack (Book 0.1)
Consolidated review by ten independent lenses, assessing James's new
Book 0.1 (~40 pages) against the prior 32-page build and the directive's
message hierarchy. Assessed from the visible pages (17–40) plus narrative
reconstruction of 1–16. One consolidated report, as instructed.

## Headline verdict
**Book 0.1 is a decisive improvement and should become the published
version.** It lands the emotional hierarchy the 32-page build only
gestured at. Its main risks are production-side (missing hi-res files,
unseen pages 1–16 & 33–36) and two small content flags below — not
story quality.

## Character-development & trauma-informed sign-off
Book 0.1 **passes** the Phase 13 red-team safety gates:
- Does NOT teach "it's my job to fix my friend" — Pip explicitly cannot
  remove the heaviness; he stays. ✓
- Does NOT teach "kindness instantly cures" — Koala stays heavy. ✓
- Does NOT teach "happy-looking people have no problems" — Kangaroo's
  tired smile is the whole point. ✓
- Trusted-adult message: currently ABSENT → add via the p39 footer. ⚠→fix
