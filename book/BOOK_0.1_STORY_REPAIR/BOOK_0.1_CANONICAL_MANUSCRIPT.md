## BACK MATTER (39–40) — CONFIRMED from Book 0.1
- **39** **For Parents & Teachers** — (Book 0.1 shows four questions:)
  - What do you think an invisible backpack means?
  - What might someone carry in their backpack?
  - How did Pip help his friends?
  - What kind thing could you do for someone today?
  **Council upgrade (Phase 8), recommended:** add a fifth question and a
  footer to serve the theme and safety:
  - *What did Pip do when he could not make Koala's feelings disappear?*
  - Footer: *Notice. Ask. Listen. Stay. And when feelings feel too big,
    tell a trusted grown-up.*
- **40** Everyone carries something. Be kind.
  *The Invisible Backpack is a gentle story about kindness, empathy and
  the feelings we cannot always see. Join Pip the little echidna as he
  learns that everyone carries something — and small acts of kindness
  can help lighten the load.*
  **Council note (Phase 9):** the primary message is stronger as the
  full couplet. RECOMMEND the closing line read:
  *"Everyone carries something. No one should have to carry it alone."*
  (retain "Be kind." above the description if desired).
