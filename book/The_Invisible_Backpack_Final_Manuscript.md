**Page 3**

In a sunny corner of the Australian bush lived a little echidna named Pip. Pip had bright brown eyes, tiny brown spikes, and a teal-blue backpack he took everywhere.

---

**Page 4**

One morning, Pip noticed something amazing. Every animal in the bush was carrying a backpack — a backpack nobody else could see.

---

**Page 5**

Some invisible backpacks looked light and bouncy. Some looked very, very heavy.

---

**Page 6**

Koala walked slowly under the gum trees. His shoulders drooped. His invisible backpack looked heavier than anyone's.

---

**Page 7**

"Are you okay, Koala?" asked Pip.

"I'm fine," said Koala quietly.

But Pip wasn't so sure.

---

**Page 8**

Wombat sat by his burrow with a big sigh.

"Everything feels too hard today," he said.

---

**Page 9**

So Pip stayed and helped, one small stick at a time. Wombat smiled. His invisible backpack felt a little lighter.

---

**Page 10**

High in a wattle tree sat Birdie, very still and very quiet.

"I'm scared to sing," Birdie whispered. "What if I get it wrong?"

---

**Page 11**

"I'll listen," said Pip. "I already like your song."

Birdie took a big breath — and sang.

---

**Page 12**

Boing! Boing! Kangaroo bounced by with the biggest smile in the bush.

---

**Page 13**

But Pip looked a little closer. Kangaroo's invisible backpack was full of worries. Even a big smile can carry something heavy.

---

**Page 14**

"It's okay to feel wobbly sometimes," said Pip gently.

Kangaroo stopped bouncing and gave Pip a real, warm smile.

---

**Page 15**

That afternoon, the sky turned grey. The wind whooshed through the gum trees. A storm was coming.

---

**Page 16**

Thunder rumbled. Rain came down — plip, plop, SPLASH! All the animals hurried home.

---

**Page 17**

But someone was missing.

"Where is Koala?" said Pip.

---

**Page 18**

Koala sat all alone under the old gum tree. The rain fell and fell. His invisible backpack had never felt so heavy.

---

**Page 19**

"It's too much," whispered Koala. "It's all just too much."

---

**Page 20**

Pip walked out into the storm, one small step at a time.

---

**Page 21**

Pip sat down beside Koala in the rain.

"I'm here," said Pip.

That was all. And it was enough.

---

**Page 22**

Pip didn't try to fix anything. Pip just stayed — close, and warm, and quiet.

---

**Page 23**

Then, through the rain, came Wombat. He sat down on Koala's other side.

---

**Page 24**

Birdie fluttered down and nestled on Koala's shoulder. Kangaroo hopped over and sat close too.

---

**Page 25**

Nobody could take Koala's invisible backpack away. But somehow, with his friends beside him, it felt lighter.

---

**Page 26**

"I thought I had to carry it all by myself," said Koala softly.

"You never have to," said Pip. "Not ever."

---

**Page 27**

Slowly, the storm drifted away. The clouds opened, and warm golden light spilled over the bush.

---

**Page 28**

The friends walked home together, side by side, splashing through the puddles.

---

**Page 29**

Pip thought about backpacks — the ones we can see, and the ones we can't. Everyone is carrying something.

---

**Page 30**

We may not see what someone is carrying, but we can always choose to be kind.

---

**Page 31**

And in a sunny corner of the Australian bush, five friends carried their backpacks together.

The End

**Page 32 — A note for grown-ups**

A note for grown-ups

Everyone carries an invisible backpack — worries, big feelings, hard days. Children carry them too.

Ask your child: "What's in your invisible backpack today?" Then simply listen.

You don't have to fix everything. Sometimes "I'm here" is enough.
