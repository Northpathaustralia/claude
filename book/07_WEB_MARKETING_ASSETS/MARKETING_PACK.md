## 7. 30-DAY LOW-COST LAUNCH PLAN

**Week 1 — Foundations (30–60 min/day)**
- D1: Publish Kindle. Order paperback proof. Set up Author Central.
- D2: Website live; add Amazon link everywhere.
- D3: Personal FB/IG announcement (the highest-converting post you'll
  ever make — make it personal, not salesy).
- D4: Email/DM 10 friends & family with the direct review-request text.
- D5: Join 3 Australian parenting/teacher Facebook groups; read rules.
- D6: First TikTok using the 30-sec script.
- D7: Rest / respond to comments.

**Week 2 — Educators**
- D8–12: 5 teacher-outreach emails per day (local kinders, primary
  schools, school counsellors, childcare centres).
- D13: Post the Parents & Teachers discussion questions as an IG carousel.
- D14: Offer the Teacher Kit in 2 teacher Facebook groups (where allowed).

**Week 3 — Community**
- D15–19: 3 parent-influencer DMs per day (micro-influencers,
  1k–20k followers, AU-based).
- D20: Library outreach — email 5 local libraries re: local-author stock.
- D21: Second TikTok/Reel (read one page aloud).

**Week 4 — Reviews & consolidation**
- D22–26: Follow up gently with everyone who received a copy.
  Target: 10+ honest Amazon reviews by day 30.
- D27: "One month of Pip" thank-you post + best reader photos (with permission).
- D28: Evaluate: which channel produced sales? Double down there.
- D29: Pitch one local paper / community newsletter with the press release.
- D30: Plan month 2 (consider Amazon Ads only AFTER 10+ reviews).

**Do NOT spend on ads in month 1.** Proof copies, reviews and direct
outreach outperform paid ads for a debut picture book at this stage.
