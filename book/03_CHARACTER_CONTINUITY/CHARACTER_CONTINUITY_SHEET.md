# The Invisible Backpack — Character Continuity Sheet
Built entirely from direct visual inspection of the 22 approved pages
(primary references: pages 3, 5, 6, 8, 9, 13, 21, 22, 31 for Pip;
pages 8, 14 for Birdie). Nothing here is invented — every detail below
is describing what is already on an approved page.

---

## PIP (the echidna)

**Body shape:** Small, round, compact bipedal echidna. Stands upright
on two short legs. Chubby rounded torso, no visible neck — the head
sits directly forward of the shoulders.

**Face shape:** Rounded head tapering into a long, slender snout. The
snout is smooth (no spines), warm brown-black, ending in a small dark
rounded nose tip.

**Eyes:** Very large relative to the head, round, warm brown iris,
black pupil, with a small bright white catchlight. This is Pip's most
important feature — the eyes carry his entire emotional expression and
must never be drawn small, narrow, or "cute-generic."

**Nose:** Long tapered snout, smooth brown-black surface (not spiny),
small rounded dark nose tip at the end.

**Spines:** Golden-tan at the base, deepening to darker brown at the
tips, covering the head (forming a soft "mohawk" silhouette from the
forehead back), shoulders, back, and rear only. Never on the face,
chest, belly, arms, or front of legs — those areas are short soft
tan-brown fur.

**Feet:** Small, stubby, tan-brown, with tiny dark rounded claws.
Always bare (no shoes).

**Colour palette:** Warm honey-gold to tan-brown fur, spine tips
deepening to a soft brown (never black-and-white, never grey, never
orange/red).

**Relative size:** The smallest of the main character group. Roughly
knee-height to Wombat and Koala; about a third of Kangaroo's standing
height. Birdie, when perched at Pip's eye level, is roughly the size
of Pip's head.

**Typical expressions:** Soft closed-mouth smile with warm eyes
(content); wide-eyed with a raised paw near the chin/mouth (curious /
noticing something, see page 9); a gentler, slightly softened smile
with relaxed brow (warmth toward a friend, see page 21).

**Personality communicated visually:** Gentle, quietly observant,
unhurried. Pip is never shown running, shouting, or wide-mouthed —
his kindness reads through stillness and attentive eye contact.

**Backpack (Pip's — always physical, not invisible):**
- Solid, opaque **teal-blue** fabric (a slightly textured/mottled
  watercolour teal, not flat colour)
- Small canvas-style backpack, two shoulder straps
- One front flap pocket with a single small **gold/brass buckle
  button**
- Worn high on the back, nestled between/over the spines
- This is the ONE piece of "clothing" Pip ever wears — no hats, no
  scarves, no shirts

### Features that must NEVER change
- Warm tan-brown fur with darker brown spine tips (never grey, never
  black-and-white, never a different species)
- Large round warm-brown expressive eyes
- Long smooth brown-black snout
- Solid opaque teal-blue backpack with gold buckle button, worn on the
  back
- Small, rounded, bipedal echidna body — never a dog, never a human,
  never a mouse, never a wallaby, never any other animal

## BIRDIE (the small blue bird)

**Species appearance:** A small stylised Australian songbird (closest
real-world reference: a blue wren / blue robin type), not a specific
named species — soft and rounded, storybook-simplified, not
photorealistic.

**Body shape:** Small, plump, rounded bird body. Perches with feet
gripping a branch.

**Feather colours:** Soft dusty sky-blue on the head, back, wings and
tail. Pale cream-white throat and chest. A subtle warm tan/gold accent
at the wing-tip feathers.

**Beak:** Small, short, dark grey-black, gently pointed. Shown open
when singing, closed when quiet/shy.

**Eyes:** Round, dark brown-black, with a bright white catchlight —
matching the same large-expressive-eye style as Pip.

**Wings:** Folded neatly at the sides when perched, showing layered
blue feathers with soft darker-blue scalloped detail; can be shown
slightly spread/fluttering if the scene calls for motion.

**Relative size beside Pip:** Tiny — roughly the size of Pip's head,
easily fits on a branch at or slightly above Pip's eye level. Never
drawn larger than a small songbird.

**Typical expressions:** Head tilted back, beak open, small music
notes floating near the beak (singing, joyful — see page 14); small
body slightly hunched, head tucked low, feet close together (shy,
quiet, sad).

**Personality communicated visually:** Gentle and a little shy;
courage shows through posture (opening up/lifting the head) rather
than big gestures.

**Backpack:** Same "invisible backpack" treatment as the other
supporting characters — a soft, pale, semi-transparent glassy-white
outline of a small backpack shape with visible strap and buckle
linework, sitting on his back, NOT a solid colour.

### Features that must NEVER change
- Soft dusty sky-blue plumage with pale cream chest (never a
  kookaburra's brown/white banding, never an owl's mottled brown,
  never a cockatoo's white/yellow crest)
- Small, plump, rounded songbird body — always small enough to perch
  comfortably in one hand
- Same large dark expressive eyes as the rest of the cast
- Translucent pale "invisible backpack" outline, never a solid
  physical bag

## SUPPORTING NOTE — the "invisible backpack" visual rule
Every character except Pip wears a **translucent, glassy, pale
white/light-blue outline** backpack (visible strap lines and a buckle
shape, but see-through) — this is the story's visual device for
"backpacks nobody else can see." Pip's backpack is the one exception:
solid opaque teal, because it is real and physical. This rule must
carry into every replacement page — Birdie's backpack in pages 23–30
should be the same soft translucent outline style seen on page 14 and
page 8, not a solid colour.
