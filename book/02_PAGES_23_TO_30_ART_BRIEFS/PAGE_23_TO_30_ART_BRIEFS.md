**Shared technical spec for all 8 pages:**
- Format: square, 1:1 aspect ratio
- Recommended generation size: 2048×2048 px minimum (matches the
  approved pages' proportions; higher native resolution than the
  existing 1254×1254 approved pages is fine and preferred — the whole
  book can be upscaled together later for print. Do not generate at a
  lower resolution than 1254×1254.)
- Full-bleed illustration — art fills the entire square, no borders,
  no frame, no white margin baked in
- Safe margin: keep essential story detail (faces, hands, key action)
  at least 8% in from every edge (about 165px on a 2048px canvas) —
  this protects against trim variance
- NO text, letters, numbers, or speech bubbles rendered inside the
  image — text is added afterward using the book's existing typesetting
- NO page number baked into the art — added afterward, bottom-right,
  matching the existing style
