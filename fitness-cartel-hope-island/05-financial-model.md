# Phase 7 — Business Model & Financial Analysis

**Method:** bottom-up membership build calibrated against (a) ~15% national membership penetration, (b) Fitness Cartel Maroochydore's claimed 5,000+ members, (c) a 10-minute catchment of ~65,000–75,000 people growing >3% p.a. All dollar figures AUD ex-GST, FY of first full trading year unless noted. **This is a pre-feasibility model; the bankable model is a monthly 10-year three-statement model built after site control.**

**The model's centre of gravity is membership count.** Below ~3,000 mature members the project underperforms institutional hurdles; above ~3,800 it clears them comfortably. That is why Phase 1's dual-catchment site selection and Phase 4's daytime-yield design are not aesthetic choices — they are the underwriting.

## 5. De-risking the ramp
1. **Pre-sale target 1,400 foundation members** (Maroochydore-style launch playbook; foundation pricing locked for 24 months) — decision gate: if pre-sale <900 at month −2, delay opening opex ramp.
2. **Pre-leased allied health** (physio group, sports medicine) signed before construction completes — income from day one and referral flywheel.
3. **Corporate anchors:** Sanctuary Cove employers, marine precinct, hospital (from 2027), schools staff — 400-seat pipeline.
4. **Merse cross-sell:** Merse Coomera (Oct 2026) becomes a funnel, not a competitor: reciprocal upgrade paths between spa-only and club Diamond.
