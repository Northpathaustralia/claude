01

The Opportunity

Not a gym — a precinct. A 6,200 m² flagship integrating a serious training floor, spa-grade recovery, 44-bed reformer Pilates, allied health, licensed café and co-working on ~10,000 m² of freehold at the Hope Island Road western gateway.

Three clocks started in the last seven months

  • Dec 2025 — Coomera Connector (M9) Stage 1 North opened, a second motorway spine immediately west of Hope Island.
  • May 2026 — Hope Island rail station opened off Hope Island Road, serving Hope Island, Helensvale and Oxenford.
  • Oct 2026 — Merse Wellness Spa (Fitness Cartel's sister brand) opens at Coomera, proving corridor wellness demand.

Underwritten three ways

  • Operating business — 4,070 mature members at $36.10 blended weekly yield.
  • Real estate — freehold gateway corridor land beside new rail, with pad-site land bank.
  • Operator-agnostic design — a wellness precinct any premium operator could run; the value doesn't die with one tenant. That is what makes it lendable.
02

Why Hope Island — Honestly

Affluent, booming — and older than you think. Median age 50, ~28% over 65. That kills the case for a discount box and makes the case for premium wellness.

Verified
  • Population 14,665 (2021) → ~18,585 (Feb 2026); growth led by interstate migration (ABS / AreaSearch).
  • Median household income $1,934/wk — well above the QLD median. Unemployment 2.4%.
  • 10-min catchment adds Helensvale, Oxenford, Coomera, Paradise Point — ~68,000 people, growing >3% p.a. (catchment figure to be confirmed by drive-time study).
  • ~15% of Australians hold gym memberships; adult gym participation ~28% and rising (AusPlay / IBISWorld).

What the demographic actually buys

Recovery, reformer Pilates, strength-for-longevity, allied health, social amenity — the highest-margin lines in the industry, bought by people with daytime availability. The design allocates ~35% of floor area to these lines and monetises the 10am–3pm trough every big-box operator writes off. The serious 700 m² iron floor still anchors the corridor's 25–45 market driving in from Oxenford and Coomera.

We tested the counter-case: a value gym here would be wrong. A premium precinct is the only format this data supports — and the southern Gold Coast, where that format already exists, is contested. The north is not.

Development Cost — $52.4m → $47m

Class 5 elemental estimate (±20–25%), SEQ Q3-2026 rates, value-engineering path identified.

Recommended structure — OpCo/PropCo: PropCo funds land + building (~$47m ex-equipment) against a 15+5+5-year triple-net lease at $2.3m p.a. with 3% escalators; Fitness Cartel OpCo funds equipment (<$5m) and operates. FC gets a flagship for under $5m of capital; the partner gets an institutional-grade national-brand covenant.
