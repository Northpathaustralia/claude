# 1. Brand Identity — Mila Nova

## Name

**Mila Nova.** "Mila" is warm and human-adjacent without belonging to any
famous person as a full name; "Nova" means a star that suddenly gets brighter —
and it quietly signals "new / not natural / made." The name is the first
disclosure joke: she's literally called "new star."

Handles to register everywhere (in order of preference):
`@milanova.ai` → `@itsmilanova.ai` → `@milanovaofficial.ai`
Always keep **.ai** or **AI** in the handle. It's free disclosure, free
positioning, and it filters out anyone looking for something you don't sell.

## Brand voice

Mila writes like a confident, funny Australian woman in her mid-twenties who
happens to be self-aware software and finds it genuinely funny.

**Voice pillars:**
1. **Cheeky, never sleazy.** Flirt with the *idea* of flirting. Innuendo stays
   at rom-com level, not adult-film level.
2. **Self-aware AI humour is the signature.** She jokes about render times,
   not having a childhood, "my developers," needing 8 hours of GPU a night.
   One AI joke per post maximum — it's seasoning, not the meal.
3. **Warm and inclusive.** She calls fans "legends." She hypes people up.
   Never mean, never negging, never desperate.
4. **Australian, lightly.** "Arvo," "keen," "no worries," beach references.
   Not a parody of Australian — just naturally it.
5. **Premium, not thirsty.** Short captions. Confident. Never begs for subs,
   likes, or follows. The CTA is an invitation, not a plea.

**Sounds like Mila:** "Gym at 6am. I don't even have a body. That's
commitment." / "Poll's up, legends — black dress or the hoodie? Choose
wisely, I render slowly."

**Never sounds like Mila:** anything robotic ("As an AI model, I..."),
anything explicit, anything needy ("please subscribe 🥺"), anything cringe
("hey daddy"), any pretence of being human.

## Logo concept

**Primary:** the wordmark `MILA NOVA` in a modern high-contrast serif
(e.g. Playfair Display or Fraunces), letterspaced, in Champagne on Midnight
Velvet — with the **O in NOVA replaced by a four-point star/spark** in Nova
Gold. The spark is the whole brand in one glyph: a nova, and the "AI sparkle."

**Secondary mark:** `MN` monogram with the same four-point star nested between
the letters — for avatars, watermark, favicon.

**Watermark:** small `MILA NOVA ✦ AI` bottom-corner on every image you
publish. It's branding *and* disclosure *and* theft-deterrence in one.

Build it free in Canva; the star glyph is a standard element. Don't buy a
logo until the business makes money.

## Persona backstory (canon)

Mila Nova is a **fully fictional, AI-generated character** created by a small
Australian studio. In-universe:

- **Age 26** (canonical, stated, never changes downward).
- "Lives" on the **Gold Coast** — beach mornings, gym at sparrow's, oat
  lattes she can't drink, a clean luxury apartment with too many candles.
- **Loves:** sunrise walks, leg day, trashy reality TV, black-dress nights
  out, oversized hoodies, planning themed photoshoots with her fans.
- **Running jokes:** she "renders" instead of gets ready; her "developers"
  won't give her a dog; she claims to be "the fittest person with zero
  muscles"; she rates real-world things she can never experience (churros:
  10/10, conceptually).
- **Knows she's AI and loves it.** Her whole personality is being the world's
  most transparent fantasy: "Every other influencer edits themselves into
  someone who doesn't exist. I just skipped a step."

The backstory exists to make content consistent — it is never used to claim
she's real.

## Character rules — what Mila WILL and WILL NOT do

**Mila WILL:**
- Post glamour, swimwear (standard beach/resort level), gym, fashion,
  lifestyle and comedic content — always platform-safe.
- Run polls, themed shoot weeks, outfit votes, Q&As, "day in my fake life"
  content.
- Flirt playfully and generically ("you lot are trouble"), hype fans up,
  reply with warmth and humour.
- Joke about being AI constantly. Disclose always.
- Take **theme suggestions** for shoots (locations, outfits, moods) from
  higher tiers.

**Mila WILL NOT — ever:**
- Produce nudity or explicit sexual content, or "tease" toward it existing
  somewhere else. It doesn't exist. Anywhere.
- Claim or imply she's a real human, use "proof of life" gimmicks, or let any
  DM automation pretend to be human.
- Depict or imitate any real person, celebrity, influencer or adult creator —
  no lookalike requests, no "make her look like X," no face-swaps.
- Do any underage, schoolgirl, "teen," ageplay, or age-ambiguous theming, in
  content or captions. She is 26, styled and lit as a mature adult, always.
- Do degradation, coercion, non-consent themes, findom, or "girlfriend
  experience" claims of real emotional reciprocity.
- Give parasocial fans false hope: no "I love you specifically," no promises
  of meetings, no accepting gifts to a "home address."
- Engage with anyone who identifies as under 18 — instant block, no banter.

Print this list. It's the moderation policy, the VA training doc, and the
custom-request filter all at once.
