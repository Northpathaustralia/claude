# 3. Subscription Offer — Three Tiers

Platform recommendation: **Fanvue** — it explicitly permits fully AI-generated
creators, has an AI-creator designation, and *requires* AI disclosure (which is
our brand anyway). Verify its current AI rules the week you launch (see
[09-compliance-checklist.md](09-compliance-checklist.md)). Prices in USD —
platform standard.

Pricing logic: $5 is an impulse buy, $10 is where polls (the sticky feature)
live so most people land there, $20 is for the superfans who want everything.
Don't launch higher — you have no reputation yet. Raise prices for *new*
subscribers only, once you're proven.

---

## ✦ Starter — $5/month

**The pitch:** "The feed, the diary, the jokes."

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Weekly content volume** | 3 posts/week (2 photo sets of 4–6 images, 1 diary/comedy post) |
| **Poll access** | Monthly mega-poll only (vote, can't propose options) |
| **Custom requests** | None |
| **Inclusions** | Main feed, Mila's diary posts, monthly poll vote |

**Boundaries (state on the tier description):** Non-explicit glamour content
only. No DM conversations at this tier — Mila replies to comments when she can.

**Upsells:** pinned post — "VIPs picked this outfit. Want a vote next week?
That's the $10 tier." Show poll *results* to Starters, participation to VIPs.

---

## ✦✦ VIP — $10/month  ← push everyone here

**The pitch:** "You pick the shoots."

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Weekly content volume** | 5–6 posts/week (3 photo sets, 1 behind-the-render outtakes post, 1–2 diary/Q&A posts) |
| **Poll access** | Weekly polls — outfits, locations, moods. VIPs *are* the creative committee |
| **Custom requests** | 1 theme suggestion/month (suggestion ≠ guarantee; best ones get made and credited) |
| **Inclusions** | Everything in Starter + weekly polls, behind-the-render content, VIP Q&A posts, occasional AI-disclosed DM broadcasts |

**Boundaries:** Theme suggestions must be within character rules — outfits,
locations, moods only. No real-person lookalikes, nothing explicit, no
age-related themes, no "pretend you're real." Suggestions that break rules are
declined with a friendly template (see [05-promotional-content.md](05-promotional-content.md)).

**Upsells:** early-access teasers ("Premiums saw this yesterday"), archive
locked posts visible-but-blurred as "Premium archive."

---

## ✦✦✦ Premium — $20/month

**The pitch:** "Everything, first, plus your name in lights."

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Weekly content volume** | Daily — 7+ posts/week (everything from VIP + exclusive Premium set each week) |
| **Poll access** | Weekly polls + Premium-only tiebreaker vote when polls are close |
| **Custom requests** | 2 priority theme requests/month — reviewed first, most get made |
| **Inclusions** | Everything in VIP + 24h early access on all content, full back-catalogue archive, monthly "Legends" shout-out post (first name/username only, opt-in), first look at merch/new projects |

**Boundaries:** Same content rules as every tier — Premium buys *more and
earlier*, never *more explicit*. Say this explicitly in the tier description:
"Premium unlocks volume, early access and creative input. Mila's content
rules are identical at every tier." This kills the #1 dangerous buyer
assumption before it forms.

**Upsells (later, only once stable):** annual plan at 2 months free; one-off
"shoot pack" PPV bundles of past themed sets ($5–15); physical merch drops
(prints, calendar — flat $10 shipping, clearly disclosed pre-checkout).

---

## Rules that apply to all tiers

1. **No explicit content exists at any price.** Never imply otherwise, even as
   a joke. "What's in the top tier?" → "More Mila, sooner, with your name on
   the credits. Same rules everywhere, legend."
2. **Custom requests are creative direction, not commissions.** You keep full
   veto. Publish the request rules inside the platform as a pinned post.
3. **All automated messages carry the AI-assist disclosure line.**
4. **Cancellations:** frictionless, platform-handled, no guilt-trip messages.
5. **Refunds:** platform policy + local consumer law; respond to disputes
   within 48h. A $10 refund is cheaper than a payment-processor complaint.
