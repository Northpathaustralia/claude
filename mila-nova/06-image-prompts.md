# 6. AI Image/Video Generation Prompts — 50 Safe Prompts

## How to use this file

Every prompt below is built as: **IDENTITY BLOCK + scene (outfit, location,
mood, camera angle, lighting, style) + NEGATIVE BLOCK.** Copy the two blocks
once into your generator's saved presets, then paste the scene lines per image.

**Consistency:** generate one "canon" face you love using prompt #35, then use
your tool's character-reference / seed-lock feature for every other prompt so
Mila looks like the same woman everywhere. Reverse-image-search your canon
face before adopting it (see [09-compliance-checklist.md](09-compliance-checklist.md)).

---

### IDENTITY BLOCK (prepend to every prompt)

> A fully fictional, AI-generated woman, age 26, clearly a mature adult,
> original synthetic face with no resemblance to any real person or celebrity,
> Australian glamour/lifestyle model aesthetic, sun-kissed tan, long
> honey-bronde hair, warm confident smile, soft glam makeup, athletic-feminine
> build, premium social-media photography quality —

### NEGATIVE BLOCK (append to every prompt)

> **Negative prompt:** underage, teen, child, childlike, youthful-face
> ambiguity, school uniform, celebrity, lookalike, resemblance to real person,
> famous face, nudity, nude, topless, explicit, sexual act, lingerie,
> see-through clothing, suggestive pose beyond fashion-editorial standard,
> logos, brand marks, watermarks, text, distorted hands, extra fingers,
> deformed limbs, warped face, low quality, oversharpened skin

## Video prompt adaptation

For video generators, reuse any prompt above and append:
> "subtle natural motion: hair moving in breeze, gentle camera push-in,
> cinematic 24fps feel, 4–6 second loop" + the same NEGATIVE BLOCK plus
> "morphing, flickering, face warping between frames."

## Hard rules when generating

- If any output looks younger than mid-twenties: **delete, don't post,** regenerate with "clearly a mature adult woman in her late twenties" strengthened.
- If any output resembles a real celebrity even vaguely: delete and adjust the canon face.
- Reverse-image-search the canon face (Google Lens + PimEyes) before adopting it, and spot-check monthly.
- Never use a real person's photo as an input/reference image. Ever.
