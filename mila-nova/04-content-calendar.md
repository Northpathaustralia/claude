# 4. 30-Day Launch Content Calendar

Structure: **Week 1** introduce the character and the joke. **Week 2** build
the world (beach/gym/apartment/night-out). **Week 3** make it interactive
(polls drive everything). **Week 4** convert (fan-directed shoot reveal +
subscription push).

Rules for every single post:
- AI disclosure in caption or platform AI label switched ON (both where possible).
- Nothing explicit; swimwear at standard beach level; Mila always styled/lit as a mature adult.
- One CTA per post, max.

**Hashtag sets (rotate, don't paste all):**
- **SET A (AI/character):** `#aimodel #aiinfluencer #virtualinfluencer #aiart #digitalcharacter #aigenerated`
- **SET B (glam/lifestyle):** `#glamour #lifestyle #beachlife #gymfit #luxurylifestyle #ootd`
