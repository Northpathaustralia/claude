# Mila Nova — AI Glamour Character Business Blueprint

**"Not real. Still your favourite distraction."**

A complete, ready-to-build blueprint for a transparent, compliant AI-character
fan-club brand. Mila Nova is a **fully fictional, AI-generated 26-year-old
glamour/lifestyle character** — never presented as a real person, always
disclosed as AI, strictly 18+, strictly non-explicit.

## The one-line business model

Post free, funny, glamorous AI-character content on social media → send people
to a landing page → convert them to a paid subscription on an AI-friendly
platform (Fanvue) with three tiers ($5 / $10 / $20).

## Non-negotiable rules (read before anything else)

1. **Mila is fictional and AI-generated. Always say so.** Every profile, every
   platform, pinned where possible: *"AI-generated fictional character. 18+
   only. No real person is depicted."*
2. **She is 26. She always looks and reads as a mature adult.** No teen/school
   themes, no age ambiguity, ever.
3. **No real person is ever copied, referenced, face-swapped or "inspired by."**
   Her face is generated, locked, and checked against reverse-image search.
4. **No explicit content.** Flirty, glamorous, funny, suggestive at most —
   swimwear/gym/lifestyle level, platform-safe everywhere.
5. **No chatbot deception.** Automated or AI-assisted messages identify
   themselves. Nobody is ever told they're talking to a real woman.
6. **The fantasy is the transparency.** Mila jokes about being AI. That's the
   brand. The audience is in on it — that's why it works and why it's safe.
