# 7. Marketing Strategy & Growth Plan

The funnel, in one line:
**TikTok/IG/X reach → Instagram profile (home base) → landing page → email + Fanvue subscription.**

Everything below serves that line. If an activity doesn't move someone one
step down the funnel, skip it.

---

## Platform playbooks

### Instagram (home base)
- **Role:** the polished portfolio + link hub. This is where people decide you're premium.
- **Cadence:** 4 feed posts/week (2 carousels, 2 reels), stories most days, weekly story poll.
- **Rules:** turn on Instagram's AI-content label on every post; disclosure line in bio permanently; 18+ signal in bio. Keep everything comfortably within IG's nudity rules — beach-standard swimwear max, no "link in bio for spicier" implications (that's what gets AI accounts flagged).
- **Growth levers:** reels for reach, carousels for saves, polls for repeat visits. Reply to every comment in the first hour after posting, in voice.

### TikTok (reach engine)
- **Role:** top of funnel. Comedy-first — the "self-aware AI" angle is inherently TikTok-shaped.
- **Cadence:** 1/day ideally, 4/week minimum. Slideshows count and are cheap to make.
- **Rules:** use TikTok's AI-generated content label (mandatory for realistic AI media); keep it comedic and lifestyle — TikTok is the strictest platform on suggestive content, so lead with funny, not glam.
- **Growth levers:** hooks in the first 1.5 seconds (use the 20 hooks list), trending audio on slideshows, reply-to-comment videos.

### X (personality engine)
- **Role:** voice showcase + the only major platform relaxed about adult-adjacent brands. Great for the dry one-liners.
- **Cadence:** 1–2 posts/day (one image post, one text joke), quote-repost fan reactions.
- **Rules:** mark the account's media settings appropriately; keep the actual content non-explicit anyway (processors and advertisers see your whole footprint). No hashtags — they hurt reach on X.
- **Growth levers:** text jokes travel further than images here; polls are native and free engagement.

### Reddit-style communities
- **Role:** high-intent niche discovery. AI-art and virtual-influencer communities *want* this content.
- **How:** find subreddits/communities about AI influencers, AI art, and virtual characters. **Read each community's rules first** — many ban promotion outright. Post the *behind-the-render* content (process, fails, prompts-adjacent talk) rather than glam spam; that's what these communities upvote.
- **Cadence:** 1–2 genuinely good posts/week + real participation in comments. One helpful comment a day beats five link-drops.
- **Never:** drop subscription links in communities that don't allow it, ever. One spam report can nuke the account you need for months.

### SEO landing page
- **Role:** convert + capture email + own your audience off-platform.
- **Do:** host the landing page on your own domain (milanova.ai or similar); title/meta around "AI glamour model," "AI influencer fan club," "virtual model subscription"; add a small blog later (one post/month: "How Mila is made," "This month's fan-directed shoot") — process content ranks and builds trust.
- **Age-gate stays on**, disclosure stays in the footer, always.

### Email list
- **Role:** the asset nobody can ban. Priority from day one.
- **Mechanic:** "Free render Friday" — one wallpaper + a 100-word week recap, every Friday, forever. Simple, wanted, cheap.
- **Cadence:** weekly only. Sales pushes max once a month (launch, price change, theme premiere).
- **Tool:** MailerLite or similar free tier. Double opt-in ON, 18+ checkbox at signup.

### Paid ads (only if compliant — check first)
- Meta/TikTok ads for adult-adjacent subscription brands are high-risk: expect rejections even for tame creative. **Don't spend money here before $1k/month organic.**
- If/when you try: promote the *email freebie* ("free AI art wallpaper weekly"), never the subscription directly; use the tamest lifestyle creative; disclose AI in the ad; one small $5/day test, watch for rejections, stop at the first account warning — ad-account bans contaminate everything linked to them.
- X ads are more permissive but low quality. Fanvue's internal discovery is effectively free "ads" — optimise your profile there first.

### Referral strategy
- **Fan referrals:** monthly "bring a legend" mechanic — subscribers who refer a friend (platform referral link if available, or a code word in the join note) get their name on the Legends wall + one bonus theme-request. Costs nothing, feels premium.
- **Creator collabs:** cross-promo with *other disclosed AI creators* (not real-person adult creators — audience and compliance mismatch). Joint polls ("Mila vs. X: who wore the hoodie better") are easy, funny, and swap audiences.
- **Affiliate (later):** Fanvue has referral/affiliate mechanics — verify current terms when you're big enough for it to matter.

## First 100 fans (weeks 1–6)

Goal: 100 paying subscribers ≈ $800–1,000/month at expected tier mix.

1. Run the 30-day calendar exactly as written. Consistency IS the strategy.
2. Post to Fanvue from day 1 even with zero social following — its internal discovery surfaces new AI creators, and an empty feed converts nobody later.
3. Founding-member price lock (announced week 3, honoured forever) creates the only urgency you'll ever need.
4. Personal-feeling (AI-disclosed) welcome message to every single subscriber within 12 hours. Retention starts at hello.
5. Convert commenters: anyone who comments 3+ times gets a friendly reply pointing at the poll ("you clearly have opinions — the fan club votes weekly, just saying").
6. One behind-the-render post/week in AI communities. This is where the first 30 fans actually come from — they're already AI-curious.

**Watch:** follower→landing-page click rate and page→subscribe rate. If clicks are high and subs are low, the page is the problem. If clicks are low, the bio/CTA is the problem.

## First $1,000/month (months 2–4)

Tier mix at ~120 subs typically lands ~$1,050 (60 Starter / 45 VIP / 15 Premium).

1. **Double down on what the data says.** By week 6 you'll know your one over-performing format (usually either comedy slideshows or fan-directed shoots). Make 2× more of it.
2. **Push the VIP tier in all conversion messaging** — polls are the sticky product; Starters churn, VIPs stay.
3. **Add one PPV bundle/month** (a past themed set, $5–10) for existing subscribers — pure margin, no new content.
4. **Start collabs** with 2–3 similar-size disclosed-AI creators.
5. **Churn watch:** message (AI-disclosed) lapsed subscribers once, warmly, no guilt: "the polls miss you, legend."
6. Raise Starter to $6–7 for new subscribers only once conversion is proven.

## First $10,000/month (months 5–12)

~700–900 subscribers, or fewer with stronger tier mix + PPV. This is a real
content operation now.

1. **Systematise:** batch-generate 2 weeks of content ahead; document the weekly engine so a VA can run scheduling and first-pass DMs (with your scripts + the character rules taped to their monitor).
2. **Second character or second language?** No — depth before width. A second persona doubles workload and halves quality. Instead: video content (AI video is the growth unlock), longer themed "seasons," merch drop #1 (prints/calendar, flat $10 shipping, disclosed).
3. **Annual plans** (2 months free) to pull cash forward and cut churn.
4. **Owned audience:** by month 6 the email list should be 3–5× the subscriber count. Email is the hedge against any platform ban.
5. **Paid ads experiment** now — and only now — per the rules above.
6. **Weekly numbers review** (see [10-launch-guide.md](10-launch-guide.md)): subs, churn, tier mix, top content, revenue/sub. Kill what doesn't work monthly.

## What you never do at any stage

- Buy followers or engagement (kills reach permanently, looks exactly as fake as it is).
- Mass-DM strangers. That's spam, and it's the fastest route to a ban.
- Follow/unfollow games.
- Imply explicit content exists anywhere. It doesn't, and hinting otherwise is both a lie and a platform-policy time bomb.
- Panic-post during a slow week. Slow weeks are normal; the engine wins on months, not days.
