# 9. Compliance Checklist

Print this. Every item is binary — done or not done. Anything unchecked is a
launch blocker (🚫) or a weekly duty (🔁).

## A. AI disclosure 🚫
- [ ] Standard disclosure block ("AI-generated fictional character. 18+ only. No real person is depicted.") in: Instagram bio, TikTok bio, X bio, Fanvue profile, landing page header badges AND footer, email footer.
- [ ] Long-form disclosure on landing page footer and Fanvue About (wording in [01-brand-identity.md](01-brand-identity.md)).
- [ ] Platform AI-content labels switched ON per post: Instagram AI label, TikTok AI-generated label, Fanvue "AI-Generated" callout (required by Fanvue's rules).
- [ ] Watermark `MILA NOVA ✦ AI` on published images.
- [ ] 🔁 Spot-check 5 recent posts weekly: is disclosure visible on each?

## B. 18+ labelling & age-gating 🚫
- [ ] "18+" in every bio and profile.
- [ ] Age-gate interstitial live on landing page (already built into [landing-page/index.html](landing-page/index.html)).
- [ ] Email signup includes 18+ confirmation text.
- [ ] Subscription platform's own age verification active (a reason Fanvue > DIY).
- [ ] Character canonically 26; styling, lighting and captions always read mature adult.
- [ ] 🔁 Any generated image that could read as younger than mid-20s: deleted before posting, never published.
- [ ] Policy written down: any user who states or appears to be under 18 is blocked immediately, no engagement.

## C. Platform rules 🚫→🔁
- [ ] Read, this month, the current AI-content policy of: Fanvue ([AI guidelines](https://www.fanvue.com/blog/ai-content-guidelines), [help article](https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/9538738-is-ai-content-allowed-on-fanvue), [community guidelines](https://legal.fanvue.com/community-guidelines)), Instagram/Meta, TikTok, X, and each Reddit-style community before posting there.
- [ ] Confirmed the subscription platform explicitly allows fully-AI creators (Fanvue does; OnlyFans-style platforms generally require a verified real person — do not use those).
- [ ] Checked AI-generator ToS permits commercial use of outputs.
- [ ] 🔁 Re-check all platform AI policies monthly (they change fast); note the date checked in the records log.

## D. No real-person likeness / no celebrity resemblance 🚫
- [ ] Canon face generated from text only — **no real photo ever used as input or reference**.
- [ ] Canon face reverse-image-searched (Google Lens + a face-search engine) before adoption; screenshots of clean results saved.
- [ ] 🔁 Monthly re-check of the canon face against face search.
- [ ] Standing rule: all lookalike/"make her like [person]" requests declined with the template in [05-promotional-content.md](05-promotional-content.md).
- [ ] Negative prompts include celebrity/real-person exclusions on every generation (built into [06-image-prompts.md](06-image-prompts.md)).

## E. No non-consensual / coercion / degradation themes 🚫
- [ ] Character rules doc ([01](01-brand-identity.md)) explicitly bans non-consent, coercion, degradation themes — in imagery, captions, and replies.
- [ ] Theme-request filter includes these categories as auto-decline.

## F. No chatbot deception 🚫
- [ ] Every automated or AI-assisted message ends with the AI-assist disclosure line.
- [ ] No claims of being human, no reciprocal-romance claims, no promises to meet.
- [ ] FAQ answers "who am I talking to?" honestly (already on the landing page).
- [ ] If a VA later handles DMs: trained on the character rules; same disclosure applies.

## G. Record keeping 🔁
- [ ] One folder (see structure in [10-launch-guide.md](10-launch-guide.md)) storing: generation prompts/settings for the canon face, face-search screenshots + dates, platform-policy check dates, published-content archive, decision log for anything borderline (what was declined/deleted and why).
- [ ] Records backed up off-device (cloud + local).
- [ ] Business registered / income declared per Australian requirements (ABN, tax) — ask an accountant once revenue starts.

## H. Content review before publishing 🔁 — the 60-second gate, EVERY post
1. Mature adult, unambiguously? (face, styling, caption)
2. Zero resemblance to any real person?
3. Non-explicit and within the strictest platform's rules (assume TikTok)?
4. AI disclosure present (label or caption)?
5. No implied explicit content anywhere ("you know where to find more…")?
6. Hands/anatomy clean enough that it doesn't go viral for the wrong reason?
7. Would I be comfortable if this exact post were quoted in a news article? 
- Fail any → don't post. No exceptions for schedule pressure.

## I. Refund policy 🚫
- [ ] Refunds page states: platform refund policy applies + Australian Consumer Law rights are unaffected.
- [ ] Support email answered within 48h; refund disputes resolved generously (a $10 refund is cheaper than a chargeback strike).

## J. Privacy policy 🚫
- [ ] Privacy policy page live before the email form goes live: what's collected (email), why (newsletter), tool used (e.g. MailerLite), unsubscribe anytime, contact address, AU Privacy Act basics. Use a reputable generator, then read it.
- [ ] Double opt-in enabled on email list.

## K. Terms page 🚫
- [ ] Terms of service live: 18+ requirement, fictional-character disclosure, licence (content for personal viewing, no redistribution), custom-request rules and creator veto, subscription/billing handled by platform, no guarantees of specific content volume beyond stated tiers, governing law (Queensland/Australia).

## L. DM boundaries 🚫
- [ ] Published DM rules (pinned post on platform): in-character fun, AI-assisted and labelled, no explicit talk, no real-world meeting talk, no personal-data requests in either direction, harassment/underage = instant block and report.
- [ ] Over-attachment response ready: kind, honest, includes "I'm fiction" and, where warranted, a gentle pointer to real-world support.
- [ ] Never solicit or store subscribers' personal details beyond what the platform provides.
