Commission & Trail Reconciliation Checker

Find out what your aggregator owes you before you send the query email.

How this works: everything runs in this browser tab. Your loan book and statement never leave your device — there is no server, no upload, no analytics in this tool. Results are a reconciliation calculation, not financial or credit advice, and not a guarantee your aggregator owes you anything — always verify a flagged discrepancy with your aggregator before acting on it. Some aggregator agreements restrict re-sharing commission schedules with third parties; this tool only processes data on your own device, but check your agreement before forwarding exported statements to anyone else.
