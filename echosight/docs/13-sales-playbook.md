# EchoSight AI — Sales Playbook

Council owners: Sales Director, Customer Success Lead, CEO (founder-led enterprise year 1–2).

---

## 1. Motions

| Motion | Segment | Owner | Cycle |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Self-serve | Creators, small brands | Product (PLG) | Minutes–days |
| Sales-assisted | Agencies, mid brands ($199–$1k/mo) | AE (from M9) | 2–4 weeks |
| Enterprise | Multi-brand orgs ($18k+/yr) | Founder → AE+SE | 6–12 weeks |

PQL definition (routes to sales-assisted): workspace with ≥ 3 connected accounts, or agency-kind signup, or white-label/report usage in trial, or API key created. PQLs get a human email within 4 business hours.

## 2. Discovery framework (agencies/brands)

Open: "Walk me through what happens between a client's post going live and the client understanding how it did."

1. **Reporting load:** Who writes the monthly commentary? Hours per client? (quantify: hours × rate = the number we beat)
2. **Creative decisions:** How do you decide what to make next? What happens when a client asks *why* something flopped?
3. **Client retention:** Ever lost a client over "results"? What would have saved it?
4. **Stack:** What's in the toolchain today; what does it *not* answer?
5. **Success definition:** If this works, what's different in 90 days?

Qualification: MEDDICC-lite (Metrics, Economic buyer, Decision process, Pain, Champion, Compliance requirements).

## 3. Demo flow (20 min, "their data or nothing close to it")

1. Pre-load a lookalike niche account (or connect theirs live — 4 min backfill on a small account).
2. **Moment 1 (min 3):** open their best and worst recent post side-by-side — read the *why* teardown aloud.
3. **Moment 2 (min 8):** Pre-Flight a rough cut they brought; show scores + the 3 fixes; re-score an edit live.
4. **Moment 3 (min 14):** generate the white-label monthly report draft in 60 seconds — "this is your analyst's Tuesday, done."
5. Close with the accountability page (predicted vs. actual) — "we show you when we're wrong."

## 4. Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
| --- | --- |
| "AI scores are guesswork" | "Every score carries a confidence grade and we publish predicted-vs-actual accuracy per account — here's ours. Judge us on the reconciliation, not the promise." |
| "We have Sprout/Later" | "Keep it — they schedule and report *what*. We explain *why* and what to make next. Agencies run us alongside; the report commentary alone pays for it." |
| "Is this against Instagram's rules?" | "The opposite — official API only, Meta-reviewed app, and we publicly commit to never claiming private-data access. The tools that break rules get accounts banned; that's exactly what we're not." |
| "Price" | Reframe to analyst-hours (report automation) or one retained client. Annual = 2 months free; agencies: per-account marginal price drops with volume. |
| "Data security" | Security one-pager: SOC 2 roadmap, AU/EU data residency, encryption, no data sales, DPA on request. |
| "Predictions might not fit my niche" | "Confidence grades tell you exactly when we're extrapolating. In-niche accuracy improves as your account history syncs — typically within two weeks." |

## 5. Enterprise playbook

Land: one brand/region pilot (90 days, success criteria written into the order form — e.g. engagement-quality +15%, reporting hours −50%). Expand: additional brands, API/BI integration, custom model tuning. Standard artefacts ready: security questionnaire pack (CAIQ-lite pre-filled), DPA, SLA (99.9%, support tiers), procurement-friendly annual invoicing. Pilot pricing: full rate with pilot-exit ramp — never free (protects value perception).

## 6. Onboarding & success

- **Self-serve:** activation checklist in-app (connect → first teardown → first Pre-Flight → first experiment); day-3 and day-10 lifecycle emails with the user's own numbers ("your saves baseline is X — here's this week's experiment").
- **Agency:** 45-min guided setup (accounts, team roles, report template branding); success plan with QBR cadence ≥ $500/mo.
- **Health scoring:** usage (weekly insight actions), breadth (features touched), outcomes (baseline movement) → churn-risk playbooks (CS outreach with a specific win to deliver, not a check-in).
- **Renewal/expansion:** QBR deck auto-drafted from the customer's own report data; expansion triggers surfaced to CS (account cap hits, credit exhaustion, API interest).
