# EchoSight AI — Marketing Playbook

Council owners: Growth Marketing Director, Brand Strategist, Copywriter, CRO Expert. Red-teamed: round R3 (channel realism, CAC discipline, launch-risk).

---

## 1. Strategy in one paragraph

We sell *understanding*, so the marketing **is** the product demo: public, evidence-rich teardowns of why specific Reels performed, published where creators already learn (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, newsletters). Every channel funnels to one PLG motion — free account → first teardown in minutes → shareable Report Card → upgrade. Paid media is a scaling amplifier only after organic proves message-market fit (CAC discipline gate: no paid scale until blended payback < 12 months).

## 2. Funnel & metrics

`Visitor → Signup (target 8% LP conv.) → Activated: first teardown viewed (60%) → Habit: 3 sessions in wk1 (40%) → Paid (6% of free within 60d) → Retained (see churn targets) → Advocate (Report Card shares, referrals)`

North-star metric: **Weekly Insight Actions** (teardowns viewed + recommendations adopted) — correlates with retention, honest about value delivered.

## 4. Launch sequence

1. **M5 Closed beta:** 200 hand-picked creators (mix of niches), white-glove onboarding, weekly feedback loop; capture 15 quantified case studies (e.g. "saves +42% in 6 weeks").
2. **M6 Waitlist warm-up:** public teardown engine running; waitlist with referral-position mechanics; partner creators seeding Report Cards.
3. **M7 Product Hunt:** Tuesday launch; assets: 90s demo video, interactive sandbox (analyse a sample Reel without signup), founder story ("we built the anti-spy-app"); community mobilised; goal top-3 of day.
4. **M7+2wk AppSumo/LTD decision:** run only if beta CAC targets missed; capped 1,000 codes, Starter-equivalent tier only (protects ARPU; red-team R3-4 caps LTD exposure).
5. **M8 "Prediction Accountability" campaign:** publish our predicted-vs-actual accuracy publicly each month — the marketing claim competitors can't copy without our data.
