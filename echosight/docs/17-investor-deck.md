**1 · Title**
**EchoSight AI — Know why. Before you post.**
The content-intelligence platform for Instagram. Seed round · 2026.

**2 · Problem**
**Creators are flying blind on a $250B attention market.**
Instagram tells 200M professional accounts *what* happened — never *why*, never *what's next*. The tools that promise more (the "who saved my post" apps) are fake, policy-violating, and get users banned. Real demand, illegitimate supply.

**3 · Solution**
**We explain performance, predict it before publishing, and generate what wins.**
Upload a Reel → multimodal AI tears it down (hook, pacing, story, caption, audio) → scores with confidence grades → exact fixes → evidence-based generation, down to Higgsfield/Fable production packs. One loop: analyse → improve → create → measure.

**4 · Product (demo stills)**
Teardown view · Pre-Flight scoring · AI Coach weekly plan · white-label agency report. *"This is your analyst's Tuesday, done in 60 seconds."*

**5 · Why now**
Multimodal AI just got good and cheap enough to read creative at scale · short-form video decides commerce · Meta's API regime has stabilised · creator economy is professionalising its tool spend.

**6 · Market**
TAM ~200M professional IG accounts / US$25B social-software market · SAM ~15M accounts (US$6B) · beachhead 1.5M creators/agencies at $30–300/mo. Wedge: creators; expansion: agencies (white-label reporting) and brands. *(Bases: assumption-flagged, validation program in progress.)*

**7 · Moat — the outcome flywheel**
Every consented published post links creative features to real outcomes → our prediction accuracy compounds with usage → we publish predicted-vs-actual accuracy publicly. Copying our features is easy; copying our labelled corpus is not. Plus: compliance-first architecture survives the platform audits that kill scraper competitors.

**8 · Business model**
Freemium SaaS: $29 / $79 / $199 / enterprise from $1.5k/mo + white-label, credits, API. 79% gross margin at scale · LTV:CAC ~4.6 · payback < 9 months self-serve.

**9 · Go-to-market**
The marketing is the product: public "why it worked" teardowns across IG/TikTok/YouTube → free tool → Report Card share loops. 50 creator partners at launch · agency motion from M6 · founder-led enterprise. CAC-payback discipline gates every paid dollar.

**10 · Traction / plan**
(Pre-launch) Validation gates: 10+ paying concierge customers → 200-creator beta (retention ≥40% wk-4) → PH launch → 1,200 paying / $0.8M ARR by M12 → $3M ARR M24. We show gate status, not vanity numbers.

**11 · Competition**
Schedulers report *what* (Later, Sprout, Iconosquare). Generic AI writes *anything* (no data loop). Grey-market apps lie. We're the only closed loop from authorised data → explanation → prediction → creation. 2×2: [explains why × acts on it].
