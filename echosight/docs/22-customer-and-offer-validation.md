# Customer & Offer Validation (v2, continuation cycle)

Decision-bearing update to docs/01 §3 using Phase-1 research. All profiles remain pre-interview constructs (**ASSUMPTION** until the 30-interview program runs); the structure below is what those interviews will confirm or kill.

## 1. Segment decision

| Decision | Segment | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Primary launch segment** | **Solo creators, 10k–500k followers, Reels-led, monetising or trying to** (fitness, food, education, personal finance, beauty, trades) | Sharpest pain match ("why did it die?"), fastest purchase (personal card), best content-flywheel fit (they share Report Cards), Reels-first matches our differentiation, and the research shows this niche is under-served (E4) |
| **Secondary** | **Social media managers & small agencies (1–15 clients)** | Same product, reporting pain adds a second value line; slower cycle but higher ARPU; enters via the free Reel review then the white-label report tease |
| **Deliberately deferred** | **Local service businesses & enterprise brands** | Local SMBs need done-for-you, not intelligence (wrong product); enterprise needs SSO/procurement we shouldn't build pre-revenue. Both revisit post-GA |

Coaches/consultants and small ecommerce fold into the primary segment when they behave like creators (Reels-led); they are not targeted separately at launch.

## 2. Primary profile — "Cara" (creator, expanded for launch use)

- **Workflow today:** shoots batches on phone → edits in CapCut (2–4 h/Reel) → agonises over hook last → posts → refreshes Insights → guesses. Tools: native Insights, sometimes Later/Metricool free tier, ChatGPT for captions (dislikes the output voice — E5 pattern).
- **Emotional core:** inconsistency feels like personal failure; "the algorithm" is the coping story. Wasted time: ~3–5 h/wk on content that data would have told her to change before posting.
- **Desired outcome:** predictable saves/shares growth; knowing *what to fix* before the edit is locked.
- **Buying triggers:** a flop streak after a viral hit; a brand-deal application that asks for engagement quality; seeing a peer's teardown Report Card.
- **Trust barriers:** ban anxiety (any tool touching Instagram); AI-slop fatigue; subscription fatigue.
- **Objections:** "astrology predictions" / "another $30" / "no time to learn" — answers in customer-language bank.
- **Willingness to pay:** $0 → prove it → $29 easily if one insight lands; $79 once habit forms (**ASSUMPTION**, price-tested in Phase-0 smoke test).
- **Activation event (product-defined):** views first teardown of *her own* content AND adopts one recommendation ≤ 7 days.
- **Retention event:** completes first Coach experiment with a measured result ≤ 21 days.
- **Cancellation risks:** novelty wear-off after 6–8 weeks; scores that feel wrong without explanation; a flop she blames on us. Mitigations: weekly experiment cadence, confidence grades, accuracy page.

## 3. Secondary profile — "Ahmed" (SMM/agency) — deltas only

Pain: monthly "why" commentary (2–6 h/client); proof-of-value at renewal. Activation: first white-label report draft on a real client. Retention: report automation becomes the monthly workflow. Willingness: $199 trivially vs. one analyst-day; barrier is client-data trust → security page + DPA availability. Cancellation risk: client churn upstream.

## 4. Offer architecture decision

The wedge offer is a **free single-Reel review** (Studio-mode teardown, no account connection, no card) — it is the product demo, the lead magnet, and the PLG entry in one, and it's honest: real analysis on their real content. Paid conversion happens when they want (a) their own baselines (connect account), (b) volume, (c) the Coach loop. Validation gates before scaling spend: free-review→signup ≥ 35%, signup→paid ≥ 5% in 60 days (**targets, not predictions**).
