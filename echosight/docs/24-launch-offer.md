# Launch Offer — Founding Creators Program

The low-risk founding-user offer. No fake urgency, no fabricated scarcity: the cap below is a real operational cap (white-glove onboarding capacity), and we say exactly that.

## The offer

**Founding Creator seat — $190 for the first year of Pro** (vs. $790 list), limited to **200 seats** because each seat includes a 1-on-1 onboarding call and we can genuinely only run 200 of them well.

Includes: Pro for 12 months · 1-on-1 teardown call of their content in week one · direct line to the product team (private Discord channel) · founding badge + locked pricing ($39/mo Pro renewal for life of the account) · their feedback shapes the roadmap, credited by name if they want.

What we ask in return (stated openly): permission to use their anonymised before/after numbers as a case study (opt-in checkbox, not buried), and honest feedback on a monthly 15-minute call for the first three months.

## Why these mechanics

- **$190 ≠ free:** filters for intent, funds nothing material but establishes willingness-to-pay data (the real purpose).
- **Real cap with the real reason published** — passes the no-fake-scarcity rule and is itself a trust signal.
- **Lifetime $39 Pro lock** rewards risk-takers without the ARPU-destroying LTD structure (R3-4): it's recurring revenue at a real price, not $49-forever-unlimited.
- **Case-study opt-in** converts the cohort into the launch's social proof pipeline — the honest replacement for the testimonials we refuse to fabricate.

## Sequencing

Waitlist (site, now) → founding cohort invited in order when beta opens → public pricing replaces the founding offer when 200 seats fill or beta ends, whichever first. Waitlist page states all of this in plain language.

## Refund posture

14-day no-questions refund, stated on the offer page. Founding seats refunded pro-rata any time in the first 90 days if the product isn't useful — we'd rather refund than keep a resentful founding member.
