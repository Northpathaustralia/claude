# Character Continuity Pack

The 30-day system uses **two recurring generated characters** plus real-footage roles that are never generated.

## Never generated (hard rule)

- **The founder** — always self-filmed (days 7, 14, 27). Generating the founder's likeness would undermine the trust position the whole brand stands on. Any video needing "the founder" without a camera day gets rewritten instead.
- **Real customers/testimonials** — none exist yet; none are generated. Ever.

## Character A — "Maya", creator archetype

Used in: days 1, 5, 8, 10, 16, 20, 22, 28 (creator-POV videos).

**Continuity token (paste into every Maya prompt):**
> Maya: woman late 20s, medium-brown shoulder-length hair usually half-tied, warm olive skin with natural texture and light freckles, small silver stud earrings, wears muted rust-orange tee or grey hoodie (per video — one per video, never both), sits slightly forward when talking, expressive hands kept below chin line, tired-but-engaged energy.

**Consistency guidance:** generate a 6-image reference sheet first (front, 3/4 left, 3/4 right, laughing, mid-sentence, hands-on-keyboard) and attach as image reference to every video shot. Same seed family where the tool supports it. Wardrobe locked per video in each `continuity-notes.md`.

## Character B — "Danh", SMM/agency archetype

Used in: days 12 (kitchen hands only), 18, 25.

**Continuity token:**
> Danh: man early 30s, short black hair, light stubble, rectangular acetate glasses, navy overshirt over white tee, sits back with coffee mug in reach, dry humour in the eyes, second monitor glow from the right.

Same reference-sheet procedure as Maya.

## Environment continuity

Each video locks ONE environment from the style bible; the environment name appears in every shot prompt of that video. Cross-video: Maya's desk stays visually consistent (same plant, same lamp) across days 1→28 — regenerate desk reference images once and reuse.

## Continuity QC (per video, gates in quality-control.md)

- [ ] Same face structure across all shots (compare stills side-by-side)
- [ ] Wardrobe identical across shots (colour + garment)
- [ ] Light direction consistent with the locked environment
- [ ] Props persist (mug/plant/tripod don't teleport)
- [ ] Hands pass close inspection in every shot they appear
