# Global Style Bible — EchoSight video system

Every Higgsfield generation for the 30-day system obeys this document. Per-video prompts reference it as "HOUSE STYLE"; anything a shot prompt doesn't specify falls back to here.

## The look, in one sentence

Believable creator environments shot like good documentary — practical light, honest skin, restrained camera, editorial cut rhythm — never AI-gloss, never corporate stock energy.

## Environments (rotate across the month; consistent within a video)

1. **Creator desk** — home office corner: ring light half in frame, phone tripod, open laptop, one plant dying slightly. Warm daylight from camera-left window.
2. **Living room studio** — couch + rug, morning light, visible cable mess at frame edge (intentional).
3. **Kitchen** — for food-format examples: worn timber bench, real steam, overhead practical.
4. **Gym corner / garage** — for fitness examples: daylight roller door, chalk dust, no neon.

## Light

Practical and motivated only: window daylight, warm lamps, ring light *as a visible object* not as the look. Contrast: soft key, gentle falloff, lifted blacks (film-warm grade). Banned: teal-orange grade, neon rim light, cyberpunk anything, HDR crunch.

## People

Natural skin with visible texture and pores; minor asymmetries kept; real fatigue allowed. Wardrobe: plain tees/hoodies/flannels in muted colours, no logos, consistent within a video (see character-continuity.md). Performance: conversational energy, "explaining to a friend," never presenter-voice. Hands: keep hand actions simple and few (prop-holding, typing, pointing) — generation risk lives in hands; QC gates on them.

## Camera

One motivated move per video maximum (slow push-in on the payoff, usually). Otherwise: locked tripod or gentle handheld sway. Angles: eye level default; 3/4 for chapter changes; overhead only for process formats. Lens feel: 24–35mm equivalent, natural perspective. Banned: drone-style sweeps, speed ramps, unmotivated orbits, AI slow-motion default.

## Edit rhythm

Target 3–4 cuts/10s in demonstration sections; hook cut inside 0.8s; hold the payoff shot 1.5s minimum. Cuts on action. Transitions: hard cuts only (one whip-pan allowed per video if motivated by subject movement).

## Text on screen

Inter SemiBold, white on subtle scrim or brand ink-950 cards; max 6 words per overlay; enter 250ms ease, no bounce. Text-safe margins 12% all sides. Score visuals use the Prism score-ring motif only when the video is about scoring.

## Sound

Location-real ambience under everything; VO recorded dry, natural pace ~3.6–4.0 words/sec; music minimal and low (or absent — silent-process format). Audio hook must land with the visual hook (sound onset inside 0.5s).

## The banned-aesthetics list (QC gates on these)

plastic skin · excessive symmetry/perfection · malformed or extra fingers · random UI text in scene · generic cyberpunk/neon · floating holographic charts · unmotivated camera moves · fake phone interfaces (real UI only via screen-record inserts) · inconsistent wardrobe/location between shots · slow-motion as default · blue-neon grade · stock-video smiling-at-laptop energy
