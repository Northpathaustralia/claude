# Camera & Movement Library

Approved moves for shot prompts, with the phrasing to use. One motivated move per video max (style bible). Reference these by name in `higgsfield-prompts.md` files.

| Name | Prompt phrasing | Use for | Never for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LOCKED | "static tripod shot, no camera movement, natural micro-sway absent" | Talking beats, cards | — |
| HANDHELD-CALM | "gentle handheld sway, documentary feel, no walk" | Authentic creator beats | Data/score shots |
| PUSH-IN-SLOW | "slow push-in toward subject, 5–8% zoom over full shot, ease-in-out" | Payoff, key claim | Hooks (too slow) |
| PUNCH-IN | "instant 15% punch-in cut (edit, not move)" | Emphasis beats mid-monologue | More than 2×/video |
| OVERHEAD-LOCK | "top-down locked shot, 90 degrees, subject hands and surface" | Process/food/desk work | Faces |
| 3Q-SWITCH | "cut to 3/4 side angle, locked" | Chapter changes | Random variety |
| WHIP-PAN | "fast whip pan following subject's head-turn, motion blur natural" | One per video, motivated by action | Transitions for their own sake |
| INSERT-MACRO | "close macro insert of object/hands action, shallow depth" | Texture, receipts, props | Longer than 2s |

**Motion intensity words:** prefer "subtle", "natural", "restrained"; ban "dynamic", "epic", "cinematic sweep".

**Pacing note for prompt writers:** the cut rhythm lives in the edit (EDL), not in camera moves — generate stable shots and cut fast, rather than generating moving shots and cutting slow.
