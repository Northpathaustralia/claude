# Voiceover — Day 25: The client asked why, and you guessed

Record dry (phone in quiet room, 20cm from mouth) or generate. Delivery: Dry comedy first half; genuinely useful second half. LinkedIn caption reframes in first person.

**[0–3.5s]** Your client asks why engagement dropped. You write two paragraphs of educated guessing. There’s a better answer.

**[3.5–11s]** Every account manager knows the Tuesday document. “Reach softened due to, uh, seasonal factors and, um, algorithm shifts.” We’ve all shipped that sentence.

**[11–20s]** But the real answer exists. It’s in the content: which hooks held, which formats earned saves, where the pacing lost people. It’s just hours of frame-by-frame work nobody billed for.

**[20–32s]** So that’s the thing we’re building for agencies: the why, written as a first draft, from the client’s own data — you edit, you own it, your logo on it. If your Tuesdays look like mine did, the waitlist is in the caption.

Timing check: read aloud at ~3.8 words/sec; each line must fit its window with ≥0.3s air. If a line runs long, cut words — never speed the read.
