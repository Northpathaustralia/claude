# Voiceover — Day 10: Same Reel, one fix, watch

Record dry (phone in quiet room, 20cm from mouth) or generate. Delivery: Minimal VO; the video carries it. Land the numbers cleanly.

**[0–3s]** Same Reel. One change. Watch the first three seconds decide everything, twice.

**[3–12s]** Version one. Logo … then a hello … the content starts at second four.

**[12–20s]** Version two. Mid-action first frame, promise on screen at zero point six. Same footage. Reordered.

**[20–30s]** In our example analysis the hook score moved from forty-one to eighty-four. Not because the content changed. Because the first second finally showed it.

**[30–40s]** Every Reel has a version-two hiding in the footage you already shot. The method for finding it is in my bio. Or just steal the rule: nothing before the promise.

Timing check: read aloud at ~3.8 words/sec; each line must fit its window with ≥0.3s air. If a line runs long, cut words — never speed the read.
