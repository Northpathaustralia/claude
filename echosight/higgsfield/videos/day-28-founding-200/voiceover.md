# Voiceover — Day 28: 200 seats, and the real reason it’s 200

Record dry (phone in quiet room, 20cm from mouth) or generate. Delivery: Flat, honest, almost administrative — the tone IS the message. Half-smile allowed at “calendars”.

**[0–3.5s]** We’re capping our beta at two hundred people, and the reason isn’t marketing. It’s calendars.

**[3.5–11s]** Every founding seat comes with a one-on-one call where we tear down your content together. Thirty minutes each. Two hundred of those is the honest limit of humans we have.

**[11–19s]** No countdown timer. No “only three left” that resets at midnight. Seats fill in waitlist order, and when they’re gone the page will just say so.

**[19–28s]** First year of Pro for a hundred and ninety dollars, the call included, refund rules printed on the page. If that’s useful, the waitlist is in my bio. If not — the free teardowns continue either way.

Timing check: read aloud at ~3.8 words/sec; each line must fit its window with ≥0.3s air. If a line runs long, cut words — never speed the read.
