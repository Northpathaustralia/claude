# Voiceover — Day 8: The 18-second desert

Record dry (phone in quiet room, 20cm from mouth) or generate. Delivery: Clear and structured; slow slightly through the three desert-breakers so they land as a list.

**[0–3.5s]** This Reel has a great hook, a strong ending, and an eighteen-second desert in the middle where everyone left.

**[3.5–10s]** The desert: one locked shot, one energy level, no new information for eighteen seconds. Viewers don’t leave because it’s bad. They leave because nothing signals that staying pays.

**[10–18s]** Deserts hide in talking-head stories, in cooking mid-sections, in “step two” of every tutorial. The tell: you can scrub ten seconds and miss nothing.

**[18–28s]** Three desert-breakers. Move a reveal into the middle. Change the frame — angle, insert, text — every four seconds. Or just cut it. Forty seconds of good beats sixty with a desert.

**[28–38s]** Save this, then scrub your last three posts and find the desert. It’s there. Mine was twenty-two seconds long and I defended it at the time.

Timing check: read aloud at ~3.8 words/sec; each line must fit its window with ≥0.3s air. If a line runs long, cut words — never speed the read.
