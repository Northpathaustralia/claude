# Voiceover — Day 20: Why this Reel did 3.1× (it wasn’t luck)

Record dry (phone in quiet room, 20cm from mouth) or generate. Delivery: Count the four decisions clearly; land “boring” with affection in the closer.

**[0–3s]** This Reel did three point one times the account’s average. Luck? No. Four decisions you can copy.

**[3–11s]** Decision one: it opened on the result. The finished thing, first frame. Curiosity does the rest — how did we get here?

**[11–18s]** Two: it changed frames every three seconds, but the story never changed lanes. Motion without confusion.

**[18–26s]** Three: the save moment was built in — a checklist card right at peak attention. And four: the caption’s first line repeated the promise instead of saying “new post!!”

**[26–36s]** None of this is magic. It’s boring, repeatable structure — which is the best news possible, because you can do boring on purpose. Method in bio.

Timing check: read aloud at ~3.8 words/sec; each line must fit its window with ≥0.3s air. If a line runs long, cut words — never speed the read.
