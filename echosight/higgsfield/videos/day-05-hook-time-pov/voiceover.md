# Voiceover — Day 5: 3 hours editing, 30 seconds on the hook

Record dry (phone in quiet room, 20cm from mouth) or generate. Delivery: Playful, rueful. The laugh is at ourselves, with love.

**[0–3s]** POV: you spent three hours editing and thirty seconds on the thing that decides everything.

**[3–9s]** Colour grade: forty minutes. Sound design: an hour. The captions kerning — the kerning —

**[9–16s]** And then, exporting at midnight: “eh, I’ll just open with a wave and my name.” Three hours, decided in second one.

**[16–24s]** Flip the ratio once. Thirty minutes on the first second, and the hours you already spend finally get seen. Comment “guilty” — you’re safe here.

Timing check: read aloud at ~3.8 words/sec; each line must fit its window with ≥0.3s air. If a line runs long, cut words — never speed the read.
