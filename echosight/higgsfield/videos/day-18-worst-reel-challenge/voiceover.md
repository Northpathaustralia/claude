# Voiceover — Day 18: Send me your worst Reel

Record dry (phone in quiet room, 20cm from mouth) or generate. Delivery: Direct, unhurried. The “seriously” should feel like a hand extended, not a dare.

**[0–3.5s]** Send me your worst-performing Reel, and I’ll tell you why it flopped. Seriously. Worst one you’ve got.

**[3.5–10s]** Not to roast you. Flops aren’t embarrassing — they’re data. Somewhere in those thirty seconds is one exact moment where the audience made a decision.

**[10–18s]** Drop the link in the comments. I’ll reply with the second it lost people and the one change I’d make. First thirty this week — that’s a real cap, I reply by hand.

**[18–26s]** Worst case: a stranger on the internet tells you your hook is fine and your pacing isn’t. Best case: your next post doesn’t flop. Comments. Go.

Timing check: read aloud at ~3.8 words/sec; each line must fit its window with ≥0.3s air. If a line runs long, cut words — never speed the read.
