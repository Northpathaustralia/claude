# Voiceover — Day 22: Find your retention cliff

Record dry (phone in quiet room, 20cm from mouth) or generate. Delivery: Instructional but quick; the DIY steps must be genuinely followable from memory.

**[0–3s]** Every Reel has a cliff — one second where most of your viewers leave. Find it and everything changes.

**[3–11s]** DIY version: open your retention graph, find the steepest drop, and write down the timestamp. Not the average. The drop.

**[11–20s]** Now watch your own Reel and stop at that second. What’s on screen? Nine times out of ten it’s one of three things: a lane change, a promise not yet paid, or the desert from last week.

**[20–28s]** Fix the cliff second before you make anything new. Repairing one post’s weakness teaches you more than shipping three more with the same one.

**[28–34s]** Want the cliff found for you, frame by frame, free? One Reel, full teardown — link’s in my bio.

Timing check: read aloud at ~3.8 words/sec; each line must fit its window with ≥0.3s air. If a line runs long, cut words — never speed the read.
