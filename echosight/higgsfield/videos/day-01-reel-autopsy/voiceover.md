# Voiceover — Day 1: The Reel that died at 212 views

Record dry (phone in quiet room, 20cm from mouth) or generate. Delivery: Dry, matter-of-fact, like a friend showing you something fascinating. No tragedy voice — flops are normal here.

**[0–2.5s]** This Reel died at two hundred and twelve views.

**[2.5–7s]** Not because the content was bad. Because of one second. This one.

**[7–14s]** Second two. The intro animation is still playing. Nothing has moved. Nothing has been promised. The viewer’s thumb is already gone.

**[14–21s]** A retention curve doesn’t slope when this happens. It cliffs. Most of the audience gone before the content started.

**[21–28s]** The fix costs nothing. Open mid-action. Put the promise on screen inside the first second. Save the branding for the end card.

**[28–34s]** Tomorrow I’ll show you the five ways Reels die in their first three seconds. Follow so you don’t miss the autopsy.

Timing check: read aloud at ~3.8 words/sec; each line must fit its window with ≥0.3s air. If a line runs long, cut words — never speed the read.
