# Voiceover — Day 3: Posting time is mostly noise

Record dry (phone in quiet room, 20cm from mouth) or generate. Delivery: Confident, a little wry. This is a “put the burden down” video, not a lecture.

**[0–3s]** Posting time didn’t kill your Reel. Your first zero point eight seconds did.

**[3–9s]** Creators A-B test six o’clock against seven o’clock like it’s science. Meanwhile the hook was decided in thirty seconds at the end of the edit.

**[9–16s]** Distribution systems feed content to a test audience whenever you post. What decides the next wave isn’t your clock. It’s whether that audience stops scrolling.

**[16–24s]** So the honest hierarchy: first second, then payoff, then save-worthiness. Posting time is a rounding error unless you’re posting at three a.m.

**[24–32s]** Send this to the friend who has a spreadsheet of posting times. They’re free now. Tomorrow: what a working first second actually contains.

Timing check: read aloud at ~3.8 words/sec; each line must fit its window with ≥0.3s air. If a line runs long, cut words — never speed the read.
