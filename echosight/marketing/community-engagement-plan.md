# Community & Engagement Plan (30-day launch window)

Content earns attention; engagement converts it. This plan is deliberately capacity-honest — every commitment here fits one founder + ~60 min/day.

## Daily rhythm (60 min)

| When | What | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First 60 min after posting | Reply to every substantive comment on today's post (question > compliment priority); pin the best question with a real answer | 25 min |
| Midday | 15 thoughtful comments on target-niche creators' posts (real observations, never "great post!", never pitching) | 20 min |
| Evening | DM replies + worst-Reel-challenge queue (see below) | 15 min |

## The worst-Reel challenge engine (day 18 onward)

Every submitted Reel gets a ≥2-line reply teardown. Hard cap: first 30 per week, stated publicly when reached ("capacity is real — you're #34, you're in next Monday's batch"). Submissions with permission become content (week-4 teardowns and post-launch pipeline). This is simultaneously: community value, product demonstration, content sourcing, and beta lead-gen. Track in the measurement CSV.

## DM policy

- Never cold-DM. Ever.
- Inbound DMs answered within 24h; questions about the product get the honest state ("closed beta, here's the waitlist, here's what's real today").
- No automation, no DM funnels, no "hey girl" energy. If volume exceeds capacity, a saved-reply says exactly that.

## Comment-section standards

Brand voice applies in comments: specific, warm, numerate, zero defensiveness. Criticism of the product gets engaged honestly (and logged as feedback); "this is AI slop" accusations get a straight answer about which assets are AI-produced and which aren't — we label, so we can point at the labels.

## Weekly

- One live "score my post" session from week 3 (IG Live, 30 min, capacity 8–10 reviews; recorded → cutdowns feed the next week's content).
- Sunday: log the week's numbers into `content-measurement-dashboard.csv`, note the top learning, adjust next week's hooks accordingly (the calendar's A/B alternates are the adjustment inventory).

## Escalation triggers

- A post materially outperforms (>3× median reach): reply-boost window extends same day; a follow-up is drafted from its comment questions within 48h.
- A compliance-adjacent question appears publicly ("can you see who saved?"): answer with the honest-limits framing within 2h — these moments are brand-building, not risk.
- Negative-sentiment cluster (3+ similar complaints): log, respond once publicly and honestly, fix upstream before posting more of that format.
