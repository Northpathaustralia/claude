# 30-Day Content Playbook

Companion to `30-day-content-calendar.csv` (the schedule, hooks, metrics and costs) — this file carries the production detail. The 12 short-form videos have complete production packages in `../higgsfield/videos/day-XX-*/` (script, storyboard, shot list, prompts, captions, EDL, QC); this playbook holds full detail for the other 18 pieces plus the system rules.

**Honesty rules for all 30 pieces:** every score/number shown on-screen is demo data and labelled; no fabricated user results; founder videos are self-filmed (AI-generating "the founder" would break the trust position — decision of record); product-demo videos are real screen captures of the actual demo page.

**Repurposing defaults** (apply unless a day says otherwise): IG Reel is master → TikTok re-export with native text placement (no IG watermark), YT Shorts direct, LinkedIn only where the calendar lists LI (re-captioned in first-person professional voice, no hashtags beyond 3). Carousels → LI document posts. All videos burned-caption by default; SRT files ship in the video packs.

**Cadence:** post daily at the audience-morning slot; engagement window per `community-engagement-plan.md`. A/B hooks: the calendar's `ab_hook_alt` runs as the TikTok variant (platform-split testing without paid spend).

## Week 1 — recognition (days 1–7): "they finally get my problem"

**Day 1 · Video V1 — "The Reel that died at 212 views"** → full pack `day-01-reel-autopsy`. The signature format: on-screen retention timeline drops in real time as the (recreated, demo-labelled) Reel plays; freeze at the death-second; one-line why; one-line fix.

**Day 2 · Carousel — "5 reasons Reels die in the first 3 seconds"**
- Slides: 1 cover (bold claim + "…#4 is the one everyone defends"), 2–6 one reason each (reason → 1-sentence why → micro-fix), 7 recap checklist (the save target), 8 CTA.
- Reasons: logo/intro stings; nothing moves; hook is spoken but not shown; text unreadable at feed size; the promise arrives after 3s. Slide 5 (#4 = "your branded intro") is the defended one — phrased kindly.
- Caption: hook line ("Your Reel gets 3 seconds. Your intro takes 2 of them."), 3 short paragraphs, CTA to save, 5 specific hashtags (#reelstips #contentcreatortips #instagramgrowth — plus 2 niche-rotated). No hashtag stuffing — matches our own advice.
- Design: ink-950 ground, one accent color per slide from Prism, oversized numerals, real-phone mock frames. Anti-AI check: passed (specifics, no hype terms).

**Day 3 · Video V2 — posting-time myth** → pack `day-03-posting-time-myth`.

**Day 4 · Teardown post 1 — public food Reel with exceptional retention**
- Format: 7-slide carousel using the public-content teardown format: hook frame screenshot (fair-dealing commentary posture — review/critique of a public post, credited to the creator with handle + "teardown with admiration"), timeline graphic, 3 craft observations, 1 stealable structure, CTA.
- Legal guardrail (from compliance review): only public posts; always credited; commentary is analytical; take-down on request honoured same-day; never imply the creator uses EchoSight.
- Production note: numbers we show are our observational estimates and labelled ("est.").

**Day 5 · Video V3 — 3h editing / 30s hook POV** → pack `day-05-hook-time-pov`.

**Day 6 · Carousel — 8 hook patterns**
- Direct adaptation of `research/hook-pattern-library.md` patterns 1–8, one per slide: name → skeleton → worked example (EchoSight-flavoured) → when it fails. Cover: "8 hook patterns that keep working. Stolen from the data, free to steal back."
- This is the highest-save-potential asset of week 1; caption CTA: "Save the library. Your next 8 posts are in here."

**Day 7 · Founder video F1 — "the anti-spy-app" (self-filmed)**
- 60–90s phone video, natural light, direct address. Beat sheet: (1) hold up phone with a fake-looking "who viewed your profile" app ad, (2) "these are lying — here's what Instagram actually lets any tool see" (10s honest list), (3) "so we're building the opposite: it can't tell you who — it tells you *why*," (4) demo page 3s screen insert, (5) founding-cohort ask, plain. 
- Performance direction: conviction without pitch-voice; one take with jump cuts beats polish. This video is the trust anchor the whole month links back to.

## Week 2 — craft authority (days 8–14)

**Day 8 · V4 retention desert** → pack `day-08-retention-desert`.

- Single-image post (bold type on ink-950: "Apps that show you who saved your post: a thread on how they work. **Day 10 · V5 before/after split screen** → pack `day-10-before-after`. Highest production value of the month; doubles as the D30 launch cutdown source.

**Day 11 · Carousel — saves beat likes**
- 6 slides: cover ("Likes are applause. Saves are homework."), why saves signal value (viewer intent), what earns saves by niche (utility/reference/checklists), the save-moment (place the save-worthy element at the moment attention peaks), one structural template, CTA.

**Day 12 · V6 silent-process trend** → pack `day-12-silent-process`. Trend evidence framing must match the honesty bar: "rising in accounts we track" not global claims.

**Day 13 · Teardown post 2 — strong content, weak result** (the empathetic autopsy; same 7-slide format as D4, this time the lesson is a buried payoff).

## Production economics (summary; full costs in `../launch/month-one-budget.csv`)

12 Higgsfield video packs ≈ A$10–15 each in generation credits (estimated conservatively per-pack in each `cost-estimate.md`); carousels/teardowns A$0 cash (template + time); founder/demo videos A$0 cash. Month-one content cash cost ≈ **A$140–180 total** at bare-minimum tier; time cost ≈ 36 hours across the month (75 min/day median). Every piece lists estimated minutes in the calendar CSV.

## Quality gates (applied before any piece ships)

1. Hook scored ≥8/10 on the `hook-pattern-library.md` rubric (calendar records the score; two pieces were rewritten to clear the bar: D5 original hook scored 7 — "POV" framing sharpened; D23 cover scored 7 — count made specific).
2. Anti-AI checklist (`brand/anti-ai-content-checklist.md`) pass.
3. Compliance pass against `../compliance/claim-register.csv` (no private-data implications, all numbers demo-labelled, no result promises).
4. Videos additionally: Creative Authenticity review ≥88/100 (`../higgsfield/videos/*/quality-control.md`).
