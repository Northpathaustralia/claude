# Captions & CTA Library

Reusable, brand-voiced building blocks for the 30-day system and beyond. Rules: first line is the hook (it's all most people see), CTA follows the verb+outcome formula, hashtags are few and specific, one honest aside per caption where natural.

## Caption skeletons

**Teardown skeleton**
> [Specific observation with a number]. 
> [Two sentences of why — cite what the eye can't see.] 
> [One transferable rule.] 
> [CTA] 
> [3–5 specific hashtags]

**Myth skeleton**
> [The myth, stated the way believers say it.] 
> [What's actually true, with the mechanism.] 
> [What to do instead — concrete.] 
> [CTA: share/save]

**Product-led skeleton**
> [The pain, in customer language]. 
> [What the product does about it, one sentence, no adjectives.] 
> [Honest limit or confidence note.] 
> [CTA to free value]

## First-line bank (tested patterns, rotate)

- "Your Reel gets 3 seconds. Your intro takes 2 of them."
- "The part you spend the least time on decides the most."
- "This one held 90% of viewers to the end. That's not luck, it's structure."
- "Likes are applause. Saves are homework."
- "Nobody can see who saved your post. Anyone who says otherwise is selling a ban."
- "Every Reel has a cliff. Find yours."
- "You're not underpaid. You're underpriced." (niche-adapted)
- "Stop blaming the algorithm for a hook that starts at second two."

## CTA bank

| Goal | CTA (IG) | Adaptation notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Saves | "Save this before your next edit." | TikTok: "Favorite this" |
| Shares | "Send this to someone who schedules posts at 6:03pm." | Make the target person specific, always |
| Comments | "Comment 'guilty' if this is you." / "Drop your worst Reel below — I'll find the second it died." | Only when we'll actually reply (community plan) |
| Free value | "One Reel, full teardown, free — link in bio." | Never "link in bio!!" alone; say what's behind it |
| Waitlist | "200 founding seats, real onboarding calls — that's why it's 200. Link in bio." | Cap always explained, never bare scarcity |
| Demo | "Poke the live demo — no signup, link in bio." | |

## Hashtag policy

3–6 per post, specificity-mixed: 1 broad (#instagramgrowth), 2–3 mid (#reelstips #contentstrategy), 1–2 niche-rotated (#fitnesscreator etc. matching the example's niche). Never 30 tags — it contradicts our own teaching (D21).

## Banned in captions

Everything in `brand/brand-system-v2.md` prohibited list, plus: "algorithm hack", "go viral with", "guaranteed reach", emoji walls, engagement-bait ("comment YES!!"), fake urgency ("last chance" when it isn't).
