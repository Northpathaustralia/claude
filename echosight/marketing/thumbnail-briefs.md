# Thumbnail & Cover-Frame Briefs

Cover frames decide grid CTR and carousel opens. System first, then per-asset briefs for the month's key pieces.

## System rules (all thumbnails)

- Readable at 110px wide (grid size): max 6 words, min 28pt-equivalent, high contrast on ink-950 or on a still frame with a scrim.
- One visual subject per cover. No collages.
- Prism palette accents only; score-ring motif is the recurring brand element (use sparingly — teardown/product assets only, so it signals "analysis inside").
- Faces: real stills from the video where available; never AI-generate the founder's face; example-content faces must pass the authenticity QC (no plastic skin).
- Text-safe: keep 12% margins clear (platform UI overlays).
- Every video pack includes its own `thumbnail-prompt.md`; these briefs are the format standard those files follow.
