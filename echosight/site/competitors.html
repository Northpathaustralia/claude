Learn from their grid. Legitimately.

Track public professional accounts through Instagram’s official Business Discovery API: posting cadence, format mix, estimated engagement quality, breakout posts — and the gaps they’re leaving open for you.

The rules we track by

Public professional accounts only

Private accounts can’t be tracked — by anyone. If a tracked account goes private, tracking stops and we tell you.

Estimates labelled as estimates

When likes are hidden we model engagement from what’s public and print “estimate” on the number. Exact things say exact; modelled things say modelled.

Craft learning, not copying

Teardowns of competitor content study the public craft. The generator produces original adaptations and blocks near-copies automatically.
