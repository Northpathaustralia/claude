FAQ

Fair questions, straight answers.

Can EchoSight show me who saved or shared my post?

No — and no legitimate tool can. Instagram doesn't expose that data to anyone. What we do instead: analyse your creative and your aggregate results to explain why people save and share, and predict how likely your next post is to earn saves and shares before you publish it.

Is this against Instagram's rules?

The opposite. EchoSight uses the official Instagram Graph API with your explicit permission, requests only the scopes it needs, and never scrapes. Tools that break the rules put your account at risk — that's exactly what we built EchoSight to replace.

How accurate are the predictions?

Every score carries a confidence grade, and we reconcile predictions against actual results for your account — you can see our track record for you at any time. When we don't have enough similar data to predict reliably, we say so and give you the analysis instead.

Do I need to connect my Instagram account?

No. Studio mode lets you upload content and get full teardowns and scores against niche benchmarks. Connecting your professional account adds your personal baselines, real outcomes and the AI Coach.

What happens to my data?

It's encrypted, never sold, and deleted when you ask (or when you disconnect your account). Model training on your outcome data is disclosed up front and you can opt out without losing features. Full detail is in our privacy policy.

Which accounts does competitor tracking work on?

Public professional (business/creator) accounts, via Instagram's Business Discovery API. Private accounts can't be tracked — by anyone — and we'll tell you that rather than pretend otherwise. Engagement quality for competitors is clearly labelled as an estimate.
