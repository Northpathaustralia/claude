Quick answers

Can EchoSight show me who saved or shared my post?

No — and no legitimate tool can. Instagram doesn't expose that data to anyone. We explain why people save and share, and predict how likely your next post is to earn them.

Do I need to connect my Instagram account?

No. Studio mode analyses anything you upload against niche benchmarks. Connecting adds your personal baselines and the Coach — your choice, changeable any time.
