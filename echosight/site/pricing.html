Pricing questions

What does a “Pre-Flight analysis” count as?

One full analysis of one piece of content (all its scores, teardown and fixes). Re-scoring a new cut of the same content counts as a new analysis — that’s the honest cost of the compute.

What are generation credits?

The unit for creative generation. Hooks cost 1 credit a set; scripts and storyboards cost more because they’re bigger jobs. Every generator screen shows the cost before you run it.

Do unused analyses roll over?

No — allowances reset monthly. Credits from purchased packs do carry over while you’re subscribed.

Is there a founding-member deal?

Yes: 200 founding seats at $190 for the first year of Pro, capped because each includes a real onboarding call. Details on the beta page.
