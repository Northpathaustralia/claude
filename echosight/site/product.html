What makes it different

It explains itself

Every score opens into its drivers. If a number can’t show where it came from, we don’t show the number.

It keeps receipts

Predictions are reconciled against what actually happened on your account. You can see our track record for you, including the misses.

It stays inside the rules

Official Instagram API only. No scraping, no automation, no “who viewed you” fiction. Your account is never put at risk.
