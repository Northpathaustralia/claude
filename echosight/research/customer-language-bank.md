# Customer Language Bank

Real phrasing patterns for copy and hooks. Sources: review-site complaint summaries gathered this cycle (register IDs E3, E5, E6), plus recurring creator-forum phrasings (pattern-level, MED confidence — refresh with 30 problem interviews in Phase 0 of launch). Rule: marketing may use *patterns*, never fabricated verbatim "quotes."

## How they describe the problem (use in hooks/headlines)

- "This Reel did 400k and the next one died. Nothing changed. I don't get it."
- "I posted the exact same style and got a tenth of the views."
- "Instagram analytics tells me the numbers went down. Cool. Why?"
- "I spend 3 hours editing and 30 seconds deciding the hook — and I know it should be the other way around."
- "My client asked why engagement dropped and I wrote two paragraphs of guesses."
- "I'm doing everything the gurus say — posting times, hashtags, trending audio — and it's not moving."
- "I can't tell if my content is bad or the algorithm hates me."

## Words they use for what they want

understand · actually explain · tell me what to fix · before I post · worth the edit time · saves (they know saves matter) · watch time / retention · hook · scroll past · flopped / died / popped / went · consistency without burnout

## Words that trigger distrust (avoid or handle carefully)

viral (as a promise) · hack · algorithm secret · guaranteed growth · "AI-powered" as the lead benefit · spy / stalk / see who viewed · bot · automation (creators fear bans)

## Complaint patterns about existing tools (attack surface)

- "The real price wasn't what the homepage said." → show full pricing incl. add-ons
- "The AI assistant writes generic mush." → "trained on your winners, not the internet's average"
- "It failed to post my scheduled content." → we don't auto-post; we make the content better
- "Great charts, no idea what to do with them." → every chart ends in an action
- "No proper Reels support." → Reels-first

## Objections verbatim-pattern → answer (used in FAQ/sales)

| Objection pattern | Answer angle |
| --- | --- |
| "Another subscription…" | One retained client / one brand deal covers a year; free tier is genuinely useful |
| "AI predictions are astrology" | Confidence grades + public predicted-vs-actual reconciliation |
| "Will this get my account banned?" | Official API, no automation, no scraping — the never-do list |
| "I don't have time to learn a tool" | First teardown in under a minute; no setup needed (Studio mode) |
