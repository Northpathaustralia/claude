## 2. What the research actually says (with implications)

1. **Pricing corridor confirmed.** Serious IG analytics starts ~$49 (Iconosquare) and complaint threads centre on price-for-value ("pretty expensive… similar features for lower prices"). → EchoSight Starter at $29 undercuts the analytics tier while offering a different (explanatory) product; Pro $79 sits below Iconosquare's upper tiers. Pricing v2 keeps the corridor (docs/23).
2. **Review complaints cluster on: hidden real cost, glitchy scheduled posting, shallow "AI assistants," missing Reels support** (Flick's no-Reels-hashtag-tracking called "a massive oversight"; Iconosquare auto-post failures; Flick AI assistant "in beta 18+ months… lacks sophistication"). → EchoSight's wedge (Reels-first creative analysis, no auto-posting promises at all) attacks exactly the weak spots; pricing page must show total cost up front (add-on prices visible).
3. **Authenticity backlash against AI content is real and measurable** — "every AI-written post sounds like the same corporate bot"; ~⅓ of consumers less likely to choose brands using obvious AI ads (MED). → Brand v2's anti-AI language rules are a commercial requirement, not taste; generator marketing must lead with "conditioned on *your* voice and *your* measured winners," never "AI writes it for you."
4. **Market is moving from generic to specialized use-case tools** (MED). → "Content intelligence for Instagram Reels" specificity is the right wedge; resist multi-platform scope creep at launch.
5. **No direct competitor found selling pre-publication creative scoring for Instagram** in current sweep (LOW-MED — absence of evidence; deeper diligence at launch). VidIQ proves the category on YouTube. → Category claim "analytics tells you what, we tell you why" remains defensible; claim wording kept comparative-free ("we haven't found another Instagram tool that…" is NOT used publicly — see claim register).
