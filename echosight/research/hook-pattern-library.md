# Hook Pattern Library

Patterns for the 30-day engine and the product's own hook playbook. Each pattern: structure → example (EchoSight-flavoured) → why it works → risk. Scoring rubric at bottom is the gate every produced hook must pass (≥8/10).

## Video hooks (spoken + on-screen within 1.5s)

1. **Autopsy open** — show the corpse first. "This Reel died at 212 views. Here's the exact second it lost everyone." Works: curiosity + recognisable pain. Risk: needs a real visual of the retention cliff.
2. **Contradiction** — attack accepted advice. "Posting time didn't kill your Reel. Your first 0.8 seconds did." Works: pattern-break + specificity. Risk: must back with the demo, not vibes.
3. **Specific number** — "6.2% of people who watch this type of hook save the post. Yours is running at 0.9%. Here's the difference." Works: numerate credibility. Risk: numbers must be real demo data, labelled example.
4. **Before/after split-screen** — same Reel, re-cut hook, scores side by side. Works: visible result, zero claims needed. Risk: production effort.
5. **Open loop with receipt** — "We predicted this Reel would underperform before it was posted. It did. Watch why." Works: stakes + accountability. Risk: only usable once real predictions exist — until then framed as demo walkthrough, labelled.
6. **High-stakes mistake** — "This one editing habit is quietly killing your saves." Works: loss aversion. Risk: overused shape; needs a non-obvious payoff.
7. **POV recognition** — "POV: you spent 3 hours editing and 30 seconds on the hook." Works: identity mirror from customer language bank. Risk: format fatigue; use sparingly.
8. **Direct challenge** — "Send me your worst-performing Reel. I'll tell you why it flopped — live." Works: engagement engine + product demo in one. Risk: must actually do it (community plan covers this).

## Carousel/text hooks

9. **The list with a twist** — "5 reasons Reels die in the first 3 seconds. Number 4 is the one everyone defends."
10. **Myth ledger** — "Things that don't matter as much as you think: posting time. Things that matter more: the first frame. Proof inside."
11. **Screenshot-of-truth** — real (demo-labelled) teardown screenshot with one circled number.

## Scoring rubric (0–10, gate ≥8; weights in parentheses)

clarity (0.15) · curiosity (0.15) · specificity (0.15) · relevance to segment (0.1) · credibility/no-hype (0.15) · visual strength of first frame (0.1) · emotional resonance (0.1) · brand fit (0.1). Every hook in the 30-day calendar carries its score and one A/B alternative; sub-8 hooks were rewritten before inclusion (see playbook).
