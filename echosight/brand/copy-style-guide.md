# Copy Style Guide

Working rules for anyone (human or model) writing EchoSight copy. Companion to `brand-system-v2.md`; the anti-AI checklist is the release gate.

## Mechanics

- US English on the global site; Australian English in AU-market materials and legal docs.
- Sentence case for headings and buttons ("Get your free Reel review", not "Get Your Free…").
- Numbers: digits, always with context ("25 analyses a month", "3.1× your median"). No naked percentages in marketing without a base.
- Em dashes: at most one per paragraph; prefer a full stop.
- Oxford comma: yes.
- Contractions: yes, always ("you'll", "it's") — the fastest de-corporatiser there is.
- Second person default; first person plural for opinions ("we think posting time is mostly noise").

## Structures that work (use)

- Claim → receipt → action. Every marketing section ends with something to do.
- The "honest aside": one sentence per page that concedes a real limitation ("Predictions carry confidence grades because sometimes we genuinely don't know"). This is a conversion asset, not a weakness.
- Concrete micro-scenarios over abstractions: "the Tuesday Reel that died at 212 views" beats "underperforming content."

## Structures that fail the AI-smell test (avoid)

- Triads of abstract nouns ("clarity, confidence, and control").
- "Whether you're X or Y…" openers.
- Conclusion paragraphs that restate the page ("In conclusion, EchoSight helps you…").
- Rhetorical-question stacks ("Struggling with reach? Tired of guessing?" — max one, rarely).
- List-mania: if prose says it better in two sentences, no bullets.
- Every sentence starting with the product name.

## Words for the product's outputs (precision = trust)

- Scores are "estimates" or "reads", predictions are "probabilities", never "results."
- "Example data" label verbatim on anything not from a real account.
- Confidence grades always shown with scores in marketing screenshots.

## Sample rewrites (calibration)

| Generic-AI draft | EchoSight voice |
| --- | --- |
| "Unlock powerful AI-driven insights to supercharge your Instagram growth!" | "Find out why your last Reel flopped — in about a minute." |
| "Our cutting-edge algorithm analyzes 400+ data points." | "We read the hook, the pacing, the caption, the audio — then show you which one cost you." |
| "Join thousands of creators transforming their content!" | "200 founding seats. Real onboarding call included, which is why there are only 200." |
