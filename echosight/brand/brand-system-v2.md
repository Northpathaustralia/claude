# Brand System v2 — Humanised

Supersedes the voice sections of docs/11 (visual tokens there remain canonical, amended by `visual-direction.md`). Driven by research finding E5: audiences are actively punishing generic-AI aesthetics and copy. EchoSight is an AI product that must not feel like one.

## Position

The craft companion for people who make Reels for a living. Not a dashboard, not a guru, not a robot ghostwriter — the editor-friend who watched your Reel three times and tells you exactly where it lost people, with receipts.

## Promise

You'll know why — why it worked, why it didn't, and what to do next. (Never: "you'll go viral." Never: "growth guaranteed.")

## Point of view (the brand's opinions, stated publicly)

1. The first second is most of the game; posting time is mostly noise.
2. Saves and shares beat likes; we optimise for what compounds.
3. If a tool can't show where a number came from, don't trust the number — including ours.
4. Nobody can see who saved your post. Anyone who says otherwise is selling a ban.
5. AI should study *your* winners, not average the internet.

## Voice principles

- **Specific beats superlative.** "Your winners cut 3–4× per 10 seconds; this one cuts 1.9" — never "supercharge your content."
- **Confident, not certain.** We publish confidence grades and say "we don't know yet" out loud.
- **Creator-native, not corporate.** Words creators use: hook, flopped, popped, retention, save-worthy. No "leverage synergies," no "unlock growth at scale."
- **Warm and dry.** Light humour allowed at our own expense and at bad-guru advice; never at the user's content, never sarcastic about a flop (flops hurt).
- **Short sentences do the work. Longer ones earn their place by carrying an actual idea, like this one.** Vary rhythm; never three same-shaped sentences in a row.

## Prohibited language (enforced by copy-lint list)

revolutionary · game-changing · cutting-edge · supercharge · unleash/unlock · 10x your growth (as promise) · viral (as promise) · algorithm hack/secret · AI-powered (as a lead benefit) · seamless · empower · "in today's fast-paced world" · "look no further" · any sentence starting "Imagine a world" · guaranteed anything

## Preferred vocabulary

know why · before you post · teardown · pre-flight · receipts · your winners · the first second · save-worthy · honest score · confidence grade · "here's the why"

## Formulas

- **Headline formula:** [Specific observable] + [tension or reversal], ≤ 9 words where possible. "Your hook has 0.8 seconds. Spend them better."
- **CTA formula:** verb + the thing they get, never "Learn more." → "Get your free Reel review" / "See why it flopped" / "Score my next Reel."

## Humour boundaries

Yes: gently roasting generic guru advice; our own beta rough edges. No: user content, competitors by name, "algorithm" conspiracy jokes, anything punching down.

## Naming things

Features get plain names (Pre-Flight, Teardown, Coach, Trends). No trademarked-sounding neologisms per feature; the product is the brand.
